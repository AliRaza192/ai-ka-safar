3. **System of Record substrate hai** — Har Worker ek durable store se padhta aur likhta hai jo agent session ke baad bhi survive kare — GTM ke liye CRM, Finance ke liye General Ledger, Support ke liye ticketing.

## The Catalog Template — 8 Fields

Har vertical entry inhi 8 fields ko follow karti hai, isi order mein:

1. **Definition** — yeh function plain language mein kya karta hai.
2. **System of Record** — authoritative store jis ke khilaf Worker fleet chalti hai. Session se bachta hai.
3. **Worker Fleet** — is vertical mein role-based Workers ki unbundled list.
4. **Authority Envelope** — supervisor kya delegate karta hai, budgets aur limits ke saath.
5. **Human Supervisor** — kaun spec, budget, aur outcome ka malik hai. Named, generic nahi.
6. **Verification Checkpoints** — kahan humans action commit hone se pehle review karte hain.
7. **Reference Engines** — current engine choices har Worker class ke liye. Replaceable.
8. **Failure Modes** — kya galat hota hai jab envelope, SoR, ya verification gate missing ho.

## Physical Workers par Ek Note

Thesis embodied AI Workers ko trajectory ke taur par name karti hai. Jab woh frontier aaye, toh is catalog mein naye verticals judenge: warehouse operations, last-mile logistics, manufacturing floor operations, autonomous vehicles. Architecture nahi badalti — wohi authority envelopes, wohi manager API, wohi systems of record. Compute layer mein ek body jud jati hai. Catalog woh reflect karega jab waqt aaye.

---

*Factory Company banati hai; Company Workers employ karti hai; Workers system of record ke khilaf chalte hain.* Thesis rule name karti hai. Yeh catalog Workers name karti hai.

## Flashcards — Yaad Karo

Yeh section ke key concepts yaad karne mein madad karta hai. Har card pe pehle khud jawab socho, phir reveal karo.

<details>
<summary>**Card 1 / 7** — Worker role se kyun alag hai?</summary>

**Jawab:**
"GTM Worker" ek single AI employee nahi jo sab GTM kare. Har vertical role-based Workers ki fleet mein unbundle hota hai — Lead Enrichment, Outreach Sequencer, CRM Hygiene, etc. Har Worker specific kaam karta hai.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 2 / 7** — Human supervisor ka role kya hai?</summary>

**Jawab:**
Human relocate hota hai — gayab nahi hota. Execution (80%) se intent aur verification (10%+10%) ki taraf shift hota hai. Har vertical mein human supervisor hota hai: Head of GTM, Controller, Head of Support, etc.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 3 / 7** — System of Record kyun zaroori hai?</summary>

**Jawab:**
Har Worker ek durable store se padhta aur likhta hai jo agent session ke baad bhi survive kare — GTM ke liye CRM, Finance ke liye General Ledger, Support ke liye ticketing. SoR ke baghair Worker hallucinate karta hai.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 4 / 7** — GTM vertical ke Workers kaunse hain?</summary>

**Jawab:**
Lead Enrichment, Outreach Sequencer, CRM Hygiene, Pipeline Analyst, Proposal Generator, Demo Customizer, Attribution Worker. System of Record: CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot). Supervisor: Head of GTM / CRO.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 5 / 7** — Finance vertical ke Workers kaunse hain?</summary>

**Jawab:**
Close, AR, AP, FP&A, Reconciliation, Tax, Audit Trail Workers. System of Record: General Ledger (NetSuite, QuickBooks). Supervisor: Controller / CFO. Key verification: materiality threshold se upar entries reviewed.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 6 / 7** — Support vertical ke Workers kaunse hain?</summary>

**Jawab:**
Triage, Resolution, Escalation, KB Curation, CSAT Analysis Workers. System of Record: Ticketing system (Zendesk, Intercom). Supervisor: Head of Support.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 7 / 7** — Envelope ke baghair Worker kya ban jata hai?</summary>

**Jawab:**
Envelope (guardrails) ke baghair: Outreach Worker **spam cannon** ban jata hai, AP Worker **double-pay** karta hai, Close Worker books **drift** karta hai. Verification checkpoints zaroori hain: outbounds approve karo, deal-size threshold se upar proposals human review, etc.

</details>
