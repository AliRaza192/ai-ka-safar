# Yeh Kitaab Kaise Parhein?

## Pehle Foundation. Jaldi Practice. Gehrai Demand Par.

Zyada tar kitaabein ek hi cheez maangti hain: pehle page se shuru karo, aakhri page par ruko. Yeh kitaab kuch aur maangti hai, kyunki yeh sirf ek cheez nahi hai. Agent Factory learning system mein paanch layers hain, aur har layer alag tareeqe se use hona chahta hai.

Agar yeh galat ho jaaye, kitaab istemal karna mushkil ho jaata hai. Reference material aakhri se padho toh haar maan ke baith jaoge, kuch banaye baghair. Courses ko reference material ki tarah skim karo toh opinions ke saath khatam karoge, skills ke baghair.

Aapko shuru karne se pehle sab kuch jaanna zaruri nahi hai. Aapko itni foundation chahiye ke safely shuru kar sakein, aur phir baaki sab us waqt seekh sakein jab zaroorat pade.

**Ek paragraph ka version**

**Introductions** ek baar, order mein, vocabulary ke liye padho. **Crash Courses** sequence mein karo, haath keyboard pe. Jaldi real kaam shuru karo. Jab woh kaam koi gap dikhaaye, **references** kholo, **Zia Tutor AI** se pucho, aur apna sawaal **margin** mein chhod do jahan agla reader milega. Phir verify karo ke samajh aa gaya, aur aage badho. Pehle foundation, phir gehrai demand par.

## Paanch Layers

Learning system mein teen content layers hain aur do interactive layers. Aap kis layer mein hain yeh batata hai ke aapke saath kya karna hai.

![Paanch learning layers aur Crash Course se guzarne ka saat-step routine. Layers 1 se 3 woh content hain jo aap padhte, practice, aur consult karte ho. Layers 4 aur 5 interactive hain: Zia Tutor AI se pucho, aur kitaab ke margin mein discuss karo.](/img/how-to-learn-layers-b21b63fdaa4cbed1c42e510339c9ba39.webp)

*Paanch layers. Teen aapke padhte waqt chupchap baithte hain. Do jawab dete hain.*

**Content layers**

| Layer | Kya Hai | Kya Karna Hai | Kab Khatam Hua |
|-------|---------|---------------|----------------|
| **1. Introductions** | Orientation pages: yeh kya hai, kisko train karta hai, kitaab ka argument kya hai | **Padho** ek baar, order mein. Do se teen ghante | Apni zubaan mein Digital FTE define kar sakein |
| **2. Crash Courses** | Chhoti, high-leverage courses jo agentic kaam ka 80% cover karte hain jo roz use hota hai | **Practice** karo sequence mein, ek ek karke, keyboard ke saath | Woh ship kar diya jo course maangta hai |
| **3. References** | Reference shelf: glossary, cheatsheets, aur catalogs | **Consult** karo jab real kaam koi gap dikhaaye. Kabhi aakhri se pehle nahi | Kabhi nahi. Yeh saalon tak open tab mein rehta hai |

**Interactive layers**

| Layer | Kya Hai | Kya Karna Hai | Kab Khatam Hua |
|-------|---------|---------------|----------------|
| **4. Zia Tutor AI** | Kitaab khud sikhaati hai — ek named teacher jo yaad rakhta hai tum kahan the | **Pucho** jab bhi page samajh na aaya ho | Kabhi nahi — yeh teacher hai, page nahi |
| **5. Social Annotation** | Shared margin: text ke bagal mein jagah jahan readers ek doosre ke liye notes chhodte hain | **Discuss** karo, us passage ke bagal mein | Kabhi nahi — margin readers ke aane se barhta rahta hai |

Yeh paanch difficulty levels nahi hain — paanch *relationships* hain. Layers 1 se 3 kitaab hai jo chupchap baithti hai: ek baar likhi gayi, har reader ke liye same, tumhara intezaar kar rahi hai.

Layers 4 aur 5 jawab dete hain, aur bilkul alag tareeqon se. Layer 4 foran jawab deta hai, privately, ek se ek, aur *tumhe* sikhaata hai — raat ko 2 baje beginners ka sawaal pucho jab koi dekh nahi raha. Layer 5 dheere jawab deta hai, publicly, bahut se bahut, aur *sabko* sikhaata hai — tumhara sawaal us page par rehta hai agle reader ke liye jo usi sentence par atka.

Tutor attention scale karta hai. Margin accumulate karta hai. Dono use karo, alag alag moments ke liye.

## Learning Loop

Paanch layers material describe karte hain. Yeh loop describe karta hai tum uske saath kya karte ho. Har major topic par chalao.

> **Orient ho, Seekho, Practice karo, Yaad karo, Lagao, Ruko mat, Discuss karo, Verify karo, Aage badho.**

| Stage | Kya Karna Hai | Layer |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| **1. Orient** | Topic rakho: kis kaam ke liye hai, kis se judta hai | 1, aur course ki opening promise |
| **2. Learn** | Zaruri concepts ek baar padho | 2 |
| **3. Practice** | Prompts chalao, project banao, commands type karo | 2 |
| **4. Recall** | Page band karke khud ko test karo | 2 (quiz aur flashcards) |
| **5. Apply** | Idea apne real kaam mein lagao | Tumhara kaam, tumhara startup, tumhara coursework |
| **6. Unblock** | Woh specific gap bharo jo real kaam ne dikhaya | 3 aur 4 |
| **7. Discuss** | Pucho, jawab do, aur usi passage ki readings compare karo | 5 |
| **8. Verify** | Result check karo, sources check karo, aur apni samajh check karo | Tum khud |
| **9. Continue** | Agla kaam lo, jo agla gap dikhaayega | Loop phir se |

![Nau stages ek ring mein, Orient se shuru hokar clockwise Learn, Practice, Recall, Apply, Unblock, Discuss, Verify, aur Continue tak, Continue se wapas Orient tak arrow.](/img/learning-loop-ring-551c4df1953592d09b2cc62607c431d7.webp)

*Nau-stage loop jo poori kitaab ko span karta hai. Stage 9 seedha stage 1 par laut jaata hai agle topic par. Colors dikhate hain kaun sa layer kaun sa stage own karta hai, aur kaun se teen sirf tumhare hain.*

Loop ek chhote topic ke liye ek ghanta ya bade project ke liye kuch haftay le sakta hai. Sequence nahi badalta. Reader jo kabhi stage 5 tak nahi pahunchta, woh material padhta hai jo table of contents ne chuna hai — problem ne nahi.

Stage 5 pe ek note, kyunki yahan loop sabse zyada toot-ta hai. Dekho table kya offer karta hai tumhare real task ke source ke taur par: tumhara kaam, tumhara startup, ya tumhara coursework. Student ke paas coursework hai. Employed developer ke paas kaam hai. Developer jo beech mein hai uske paas koi nahi hai, aur kitna bhi padho woh nahi milta.

Yeh motivation ki problem nahi hai — missing input hai. [Getting Paid as a Vertical FDE](/docs/how-to-get-paid-agentic-ai-era) woh page hai jo yeh deta hai: abhi client ko kya bech sakte ho, aur baad mein kya own karna hai. Agar tumhara loop Apply par ruk jaata hai, woh page padho — doosra course shuru karne ke bajaye.

## Layer 2: Crash Courses

[Getting Started: Crash Courses](/docs/getting-started) ke paas map hai, aur map chhota hai: sabke liye [Foundations](/docs/foundations), phir [General Agents](/docs/general-agents), phir [Personal Agent Harnesses](/docs/personal-agent-harnesses), phir ek do tracks mein se ([Mode 1](/docs/mode-1-problem-solving) ya [Mode 2](/docs/mode-2-manufacturing)), phir References aur Companions.

Do rules is layer par hain.

**Ek course ek baar mein.** Chhe courses aadhe padhe tumhein ek complete course se kam sikhate hain. Sidebar mein order dependency order hai — suggestion nahi.

**Sirf padha hua course = course liya nahi.** Har Crash Course prompts, projects, ya build par khatam hota hai. Woh closing section woh hai jahan learning hoti hai — kyunki yeh wohi hissa hai jahan machine tumhein ghalat sabit kar sakti hai.

Progress pages se naapno — is se ke kya explain kar sakein, apply kar sakein, aur verify kar sakein.

### Crash Course Routine

Upar ka nau-stage loop poori kitaab ko span karta hai. Stages 1 se 4 ek hi course ke andar hote hain, toh inka apna routine hai: saat steps, har baar. Ek loop, ek routine andar. Yehi do sequences hain jo yaad rakhne layak hain.

**1. Promise aur question list padho.** Har course shuru mein batata hai kya cover hota hai, kitna waqt lagta hai, aur kya assume hota hai. Bahut se questions bhi list karte hain. Woh list course se *pehle* padho aur do-teen dimagh mein jawab dene ki koshish karo. Galat guesses kaam ke hain kyunki yeh dikhate hain ke tumhara kaun sa mental model badalne wala hai.

**2. Slides skim karo.** Jahan course ke upar **Teaching Aid** hai, usay ek baar jaldi se click karo. Paanch minute slides se course ki shape samajh aa jaati hai — toh detail kahin baith sake.

**3. Course ek baar structure ke liye padho.** Is pehli pass mein shape ke liye padho. Mastery ka goal nahi hai. Lambi detours se bacho, lekin jab koi missing term agle section ko roke toh ruko — [glossary](/docs/glossary) kholo, matlab lo, wapas aao. Is stage par kuch confusion normal hai — doosri pass saaf kar degi.

**4. Closing work karo.** Prompts chalao. Project banao. Commands type karo. Yeh step reading ko skill mein badalta hai — aur yeh woh step hai jo thake hue log chhodte hain. Agar sirf aadha course karne ka waqt hai, toh concepts ka pehla aadha karo aur closing work poori karo — ulta nahi.

**5. Quiz lo,** page band karke, kuch dobara padhne se pehle. Phir sirf woh dobara padho jo galat hue. Details neeche hain.

**6. Flashcards schedule pe karo.** Details neeche hain.

**7. Jo confused kiya woh annotate karo.** Tab band karne se pehle, us passage par ek note chhod do jo sabse zyada mushkil laga. Yeh Layer 5 hai — aur sirf 90 seconds lagta hai.

### Khud ko imaandaari se grade karo

Yeh kitaab maangti hai ke tum AI jo produce kare usay verify karo. Wohi discipline khud par bhi lagao. Har course ke end mein teen sawaal:

1.  Kya main core idea teen sentences mein explain kar sakta hoon, page ke baghair?
2.  Kya main blank screen se wohi kaam dobara kar sakta hoon, course se copy kiye baghair?
3.  Kya main is course mein ek cheez naam de sakta hoon jo abhi bhi nahi samajh aayi?

Confident haan, haan, aur teesre sawaal ka specific jawab — matlab khatam. Dhundla jawab usually matlab nahi hua — kyunki topic samajhne wala reader apni samajh ki hamesha edge bata sakta hai.

### Teaching Aid: Presentation

Har course ke upar ka slideshow ready-made teaching structure hai. Instructors, trainers, aur study-group leaders material review karne ke baad is se padha sakte hain — bootcamps, universities, aur corporate training teams isay as-is use kar sakte hain. Padhne se pehle structure ke liye use karo, padhne ke baad revise ke liye, aur baad mein ek ek slide reminder ke liye.

Solo readers isay ek aur tareeqe se use karein: deck kisi ko sunao. Colleague, study group, sabr wale dost, ya khaali kamra. Concept aloud, order mein, slides se jo tumne nahi likhi — yeh woh gaps expose karta hai jo abhi bhi chhupa hua hai.

### Practice Engine: Quiz aur Flashcards

Yeh dono ke liye extra nahi hain — yeh course ka sabse important hissa hain. Yeh sirf retrieval test karte hain — recognition nahi.

> **Course tumhein sikhaata hai. Quiz pata lagata hai kaam kiya ya nahi. Flashcards decide karti hain ke teen mahine baad bhi paas hai ya nahi.**

**Quiz retrieval hai, judgment nahi.** Padhne se samajh ka ehsaas hota hai. Yaad se jawab dene se pata lagta hai samajh real hai ya nahi — aur in dono ke beech mein zyada tar waste study time hai. Isliye quiz kisi dobara padhne se *pehle* aata hai: galat jawab batata hai kahan dekhna hai — aur pehle padhna woh information phenk deta hai.

Galat jawab par char moves order mein:

1.  Relevant section par wapas jao.
2.  Bilkul saaf batao kya samjha.
3.  Sahi jawab apni zubaan mein batao — kitaab ki nahi.
4.  Ek din baad wohi question phir se pucho.

Step 3 woh hai jo log chhodte hain aur jo kaam karta hai. Sahi jawab pehchanna aasan hai. Paida karna skill hai. Quiz scores [Leaderboard](/leaderboard) mein bhi jaate hain — motivation ke liye, asli maqsad nahi.

**Flashcards decide karti hain ke bachta kya hai.** Crash Courses tez hain, aur tezi seekh ghoom jaati hai jab tak kuch wapas na khinch le. Spaced repetition woh cheez hai: sawaal padho, aloud ya likh kar jawab do, answer reveal karo, mushkil cards mark karo, aur unhein zyada baar review karo. Sab ek saath mat karo — jagah jagah karo: usi din, agle din, ek hafte baad, aur ek mahine baad. Ek mahine mein char chhoti reviews baar baar padhne se kam waqt leti hain aur zyada yaad rehti hain.

Yeh sabse zyada useful us cheez ke liye hain jo tumhein kaam karte waqt foran pehchanni hoti hai — vocabulary, distinctions, principles, aur mental models. Live build mein, term jo sirf aadhi pata hai tumhein galah direction mein le jaayega.

Yeh aids kyun hain, layers kyun nahi

Sawaal valid hai — Layers 4 aur 5 promoted hue aur yeh nahi. Test yeh hai ke cheez poori kitaab ko span karti hai ya ek course ke andar rehti hai.

Zia Tutor AI aur annotation margin har page tak pahunchte hain, aur dono nayi cheez dete hain: explanation, ya doosre reader ka perspective. Yeh *sources* hain — toh layers hain. Quiz aur deck ek course ke belong karte hain aur koi nayi knowledge nahi dete. Yeh woh kaam karte hain jo tumhare paas pehle se hai — isliye effective hain. Toh yeh chhata aur saatva layer nahi hain — yeh loop ka stage 4 hain, Recall, aur yeh woh stage hai jahan kitaab ka zyada tar retention jeeta jaata hai.

## Layer 3: References

Aap build ke beech mein hain. Screen par ek term kaam kar raha hai sentence mein — aur aapko nahi pata kya matlab hai. Aapko course ki zaroorat nahi — aapko ek jawab chahiye, aur phir build par wapas jaana hai.

**References** iske liye hain: [Glossary](/docs/glossary), [Cheatsheets](/docs/cheatsheets), aur [Which AI Employees Should You Use in 2026](/css/which-agents-2026). Yeh consult-on-demand layer hai.

Yahan se kam *continuous* time lagega baaki kitaab se — kyunki yeh layer seedha padhne ke liye design nahi hai. Aap kisi specific problem, question, ya gap se enter karte ho. Sidhe aana hai, lena hai, aur chale jaana hai.

Aamtaur par tab open karo jab:

-   Current page assume kare ke tum term jaante ho lekin nahi jaante
-   Build ke beech mein command, flag, ya pattern lookup karna ho
-   AI workers compare karke decide karna ho kaun sa use karna hai

Crash Courses kaam shuru karwate hain. References tab kaam rokne nahi dete jab detail missing ho.

### Just-In-Time Education

Traditional education fixed sequence follow karta hai. Students hafte, mahine, ya saalon tak broad subject padhte hain — aksar us se pehle jab pata bhi nahi hota kab use karenge.

AI ek aur model possible banata hai: just-in-time education. Concept us waqt seekho jab useful ho jaaye. Poori subject padhne ke bajaye seedha us par jao jo current problem chahiye — jo ek chhoti explanation, refresher, worked example, ya sahi course dhoondne mein madad ho sakta hai.

Yeh moments pehchanne aasan hain. Kuch refresh karna ho jo pehle seekha. Koi unfamiliar term mile. Decision ki tayari ho, ya naye area mein shuru ho raha ho, ya ek missing piece se problem mein atka ho. Education kuch aisa ban jaata hai jo demand par mil sake — working life ke kisi bhi point par.

**Yeh pattern ek layer tak mehdood nahi hai.** Zia Tutor AI se puchna just-in-time move hai. Usi paragraph ke margin padhna bhi hai. Real kaam mein aksar same 10 minutes mein teeno use karte ho: reference detail deta hai, tutor woh samjhaata hai jo nahi samjha, aur margin batata hai aur kaun atka.

### On Demand Matlab Foundation-Free Nahi

Aap agent architecture nahi samajh sakte bina jaane ke agent, tool, context window, specification, aur workflow kya hain. Bina foundation ke nahi pata chalta kaun sa sawaal pucho — aur galat sawaal puocha gaya AI fluently aur confidently jawab dega. Isliye kitaab Layer 1 aur Foundations courses se shuru hoti hai.

> **Pehle foundation, phir gehrai demand par.**

Foundation tumhein problem pehchanna sikhaata hai. Just-in-time learning tumhein solve karne ki knowledge deta hai.

## Layer 4: Zia Tutor AI

Kuch sawaal kisi page se nahi solve hote — kyunki yeh page aur tumhari situation ke beech ke gap ke baare hain. Layer 4 unke liye hai.

[Zia Tutor AI](/docs/ecosystem/zia-tutor-ai) yeh kitaab hai jo khud sikhaati hai — faceless system nahi, ek named teacher. Yeh connector-native app hai — koi app download nahi, koi subscription nahi: yeh usi AI app mein chalta hai jo tum pehle se use karte ho, us app ke free tier par.

Teen steps:

1.  Apne AI app mein ek connector add karo aur ek baar authorize karo.
2.  Normal chat kholo aur bolo **"Mujhe Agent Factory sikhaao."**
3.  Zia ki voice mein seekho, phir baad mein aao aur bolo **"jahan the wahan se continue karo."**

Yeh tumhe naam se greet karta hai, Zia ki apni method aur voice mein sikhaata hai, last piece samajh aaya ya nahi check karta hai, aur sessions aur hafton tak yaad rakhta hai tum kahan the. Yeh kitaab ka khud ka content padhta hai — open internet se nahi. Toh yeh tumhe kitaab ki terminology aur method sikhaata hai — kitaab ke alfaaz mein.

### Do Aur Tareeqe Kitaab Se Pucho

Tutor tumhe course sikhata hai. Yeh do tumhare paas already mojood agent ko ground karne ke liye hain.

**System of Record connector (Beta 1).** [Agent Factory System of Record](/docs/ecosystem/system-of-record) kitaab ko MCP ke upar serve karta hai — toh koi bhi MCP-capable agent ya client kitaab se jawab de sake aur section cite kar sake. Yeh read-only hai, aur Beta 1 About se Glossary tak core content cover karta hai. Ise [claude.ai](https://claude.ai/new?modal=add-custom-connector#settings/customize-connectors) ya kisi bhi MCP client mein custom connector ke taur par add karo.

**Copy as Markdown.** Ek single page ke liye koi setup nahi chahiye. Har page ke upar **Copy as Markdown** button hai (`Cmd` ya `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `C`). Page copy karo, AI app mein paste karo, aur actual text ke against sawaal pucho. Model us page se jawab deta hai — yaad se nahi.

### Sawaal Jo Pucho

Dhundle sawaal dhundle jawab dete hain. Yeh kaam karte hain:

-   "Context window ko beginners ki zubaan mein samjhao."
-   "Is concept ko accounting example se samjhao."
-   "Skill aur connector mein kya farq hai?"
-   "10-80-10 Rule pe quiz lo. Ek sawaal ek baar mein, aur jawab tab tak mat do jab tak maine try na kar liya."
-   "Approval workflow design karne se pehle kaun sa course loon?"
-   "Maine Foundations aur Mode 2 khatam kar liya. Main actually kya bech sakta hoon, aur kisko?"
-   "Yeh hai mera Digital FTE ka explanation apni zubaan mein. Batao kya galat ya missing hai — aur dheela mat padna."

Woh aakhri prompt us se zyada important hai jitna lagta hai. AI by default agreement ki taraf jhukta hai. Correction explicitly maangna usay teacher banata hai.

### Augmentation, Replacement Nahi

Koi bhi AI tutor human teachers ki zaroorat khatam nahi karta. Teachers professional judgment, encouragement, discipline, social understanding, emotional support, ethical responsibility, aur yeh pehchante hain ke situation ko ab insaan ki zaroorat hai — yeh sab dete hain. Woh decide karte hain kya sikhaya jaaye, evaluate karte hain learner ne sach mein samjha ya nahi, aur learning environment ke liye zimmedari rakhte hain.

> **Teacher plus AI tutor.**

Teacher learning process lead karta hai. AI personalized, just-in-time assistance deta hai. Milke woh dete hain jo hamesha scale par mushkil raha: achhi teaching plus har learner ke liye individual attention.

## Layer 5: Hypothesis ke Saath Social Annotation

Technical book akele padhna khamoshi hai — aur khamoshi tumhare sawaal chhupa deti hai. Isliye is kitaab ke paas ek margin hai.

[Hypothesis](https://hypothes.is/) — higher education mein istemal hone wala open social annotation platform — **is kitaab ke har page mein built-in hai.** Kuch install nahi karna. Passage highlight karo aur directly comment, sawaal, explanation, link, ya example attach karo — aur doosre readers wahan reply karte hain. Discussion usi exact sentence ke bagal hota hai — isliye kaam karta hai: tumhara sawaal wahan baithta hai jahan confusion hua.

Yeh woh layer bhi hai jo kitaab improve karta hai. Margin mein har sawaal signal hai ke kaun sa paragraph kaam nahi kar raha.

### Kaise Use Karein

![Annotation sidebar ka schematic. Bayen: kitaab ka page, text ki lines, ek line highlight, aur uske upar floating popup jo Annotate aur Highlight buttons dikhata hai. Daen: toolbar jo speech bubble (12 annotations count), pencil, aur eye dikhata hai. Sidebar panel mein Public selector, Login link, aur do annotation cards.](/img/hypothesis-sidebar-06dea7099db3df76bb174187431a2ec0.webp)

*Margen use mein kaisa lagta hai. Numbers char cheezein hain jo post karne se pehle jaanna chahiye.*

**1. Sidebar kholo.** Kisi bhi lesson ke upar toolbar mein, uske daen ke menu se **Notes & Highlights** chuno. Har page ke daen edge par ek chhoti toolbar bhi hai: upar ka button sidebar kholta hai aur us page par annotations ka count dikhata hai, eye highlights dikhata ya chhupata hai, aur note icon poore page ke liye **page note** chhodta hai.

**2. Likhne se pehle padho.** Public annotations padhne ke liye account nahi chahiye. Highlighted passages dikhate hain kahan notes hain, aur us par click karne se us note tak jaata hai. Course lene se pehle uska margin padhna achha tareeqa hai padhne ka.

**3. Login karo likhne ke liye.** Annotations banane ke liye free Hypothesis account chahiye — sidebar se ek minute mein bana sakte ho.

**4. Group check karo, phir passage select karo.** Upar ka selector control karta hai kaun tumhara note dekhta hai: **Public** matlab is kitaab ka har reader, aur **private group** matlab sirf woh group. Phir koi bhi text highlight karo aur **Annotate** chuno doosron ke liye, ya **Highlight** sirf apne liye.

**5. Likho, tag karo, post karo.** Annotations plain text, links, aur images leti hain. Kisi bhi note ke neeche **Reply** se doosre reader ka jawab do.

Ek cheez jo eventually confuse karegi: agar page annotation ke baad edit ho jaaye, annotation apna anchored text kho sakta hai aur **orphan** ban jaata hai. Kuch nahi hota — bas ab kisi passage point nahi karta.

Tags karo annotations ko

Tags margin ko searchable banate hain — jo scattered comments ko resource banata hai. Do second ka convention:

| Tag | Kahan Use Karein |
|-----|-----------------|
| `question` | Jo samajh na aaya |
| `correction` | Jo purana, galat, ya rename ho gaya |
| `example` | Apne field se worked example |
| `bridge` | Do courses ke beech ka link |
| `accounting`, `legal`, `medicine` | Tumhara domain — taake professionals ek doosre ko dhoond sakein |

Baad mein tag search karo — tumhare paas ek study guide hai jo kisi ne nahi likha.

### Paanch Annotations Jo Likhne Layak Hain

Zyada tar readers ko nahi pata kya likhein. Achhi annotation kam se kam yeh paanch kaam karti hai:

**1. Clarify.** Passage phir se zyada aasan zubaan mein bolo.

> "Iska matlab hai agent ko ek authoritative source chahiye padhne ke liye — model jo yaad rakhta hai us par depend karne ke bajaye."

**2. Connect.** Kisi doosre course, field, ya apne experience se jodo.

> "Yeh wohi role hai jo general ledger accounting system mein play karta hai: ek jagah jahan sab reconcile hota hai."

**3. Question.** Kuch unclear, incomplete, ya debatable naam do.

> "Kya yeh rule tab bhi hold karta hai jab agent sirf draft banata hai jo insaan review karta hai bhejne se pehle?"

**4. Apply.** Batao idea real kaam mein kaise use hoga.

> "Law firm is pattern ko use kar sakta hai taake agent client ko advice bhejne se pehle partner approval require ho."

**5. Correct.** Kuch purana flag karo, aur evidence lao.

> "Yeh command January release mein rename hua. Current flag yahan documented hai: [link]."

Imaandaar sawaal paanchon mein sabse valuable hai aur log sabse zyada isay chhodne se darte hain. Chhod do phir bhi. Agle hafte us paragraph par aane wale ke paas wohi sawaal hai — aur ho sakta hai tumse bhi zyada puchne se dare.

**Kya nahi likhna.** "Great point" margin mein kuch nahi add karta, aur na hi argument passage ke alawa kisi cheez par. Aadha page highlight karo bina bataye kyun matter karta hai — yeh mat karo. Annotation kam se kam ek sawaal ka jawab de: Iska matlab kya hai? Yeh matter kyun karta hai? Sahi hai? Aur kahan apply hota hai? Kya abhi bhi unclear hai? Main ise kaise use karunga?

### Teachers ke Liye: Private Group Use Karo

Week one se pehle apni class ke liye ek **private Hypothesis group** banao, aur students ko wahan post karo — Public layer mein nahi. Do cheezein hoti hain. Students darwaze wale kamre mein zyada imaandaari se likhte hain — toh woh confusions jo woh haath nahi uthayenge woh margin mein aa jaati hain. Aur tumhare paas reading list hoti hai ke kya padhana hai: class se pehle group kholo, jo flag kiya woh padho, aur session wahan se shuru karo.

Annotation kitne worth ka hai

Hypothesis strong results report karta hai social annotation use karne wale institutions se — jaise 32% class retention mein increase, roughly double comprehension scores, aur improved grades. Yeh figures company ne khud report ki hain — independent studies nahi. Alag Hypothesis pages aur case studies alag grade numbers dete hain — toh specific case study link karo agar quote karein.

Mechanism seedha hai: annotation reading ko active aur social process banata hai — kyunki yeh tumse sawaal karne, samjhane, jodne, aur jawab dene ka kehta hai — sirf ainein hilane ke bajaye.

**Shuru karne mein lagbhag do minute lagte hain.** [Notes & Highlights](/notes) walk-through karta hai: panel kholna, free account banana, aur woh ek setting jo sahi karni hai — likhne se pehle group chuno, galti se Public par post mat karo.

## Classroom Pattern

Teachers, trainers, aur study-group leaders wohi material structured lesson ke taur par chala sakte hain:

1.  Presentation se topic introduce karo.
2.  Crash Course assign karo, aur har learner se karo ke class group mein do passages annotate karein — ek important laga, ek confusing.
3.  Session confusing se shuru karo. Annotations batate hain kya padhna hai — tum bolne se pehle.
4.  Practical exercise saath mein karo, phir quiz checkpoint ke taur par use karo aur flashcards baad ke liye assign karo.
5.  Class ko real problem do jise concept chahiye, aur references aur Zia Tutor AI ki taraf bhejo jab gap dikhe.
6.  Har learner se bolo apne decisions explain aur defend kare.

Steps 2 aur 3 asli kaam karte hain. Baaki sab aam achhi teaching hai.

## Learner Ki Zimmedari

AI explain, summarize, quiz, guide, aur personalize kar sakta hai. Woh tumhari samajh ki zimmedari nahi le sakta. Tum abhi bhi responsible ho ke critically socho, claims test karo, sources check karo, apni uncertainty naam do, skills practice karo, AI jo produce kare usay evaluate karo, aur jab situation maange insaani madad maango.

Chhe habits yeh practically leke aati hain:

1.  **Sab kuch verify karo — is kitaab ko bhi.** Tools badalte hain, prices badalte hain, aur live product hamesha sahi hota hai jab woh page se disagree kare. Jab kuch purana mile, `correction` tag se annotate karo.
2.  **Glossary open rakho.** Ek tab, hamesha. Term jo sirf aadhi pata hai har baar paragraph chura leta hai.
3.  **Ek course ek baar mein.** Khatam karo shuru karne se pehle.
4.  **Kuch chhota ship karo, jaldi.** Chhota working cheez bade planned cheez se zyada sikhata hai.
5.  **Sunao.** Deck use karo, dost, ya khaali kamra. Explanation woh test hai jo fake nahi ho sakta.
6.  **Akele mat padho.** Tumhare paas ek tutor ek taraf hai aur margin doosri taraf hai.

Inn chheon ke neeche woh rhythm hai jo kitaab AI workers banane ke liye sikhata hai — tumhari apni learning par palta hai. Tum intent set karte ho — pehla 10%: decide karo kya seekh rahe ho aur kyun. Layers execution carry karte hain — 80%. Tum outcome verify karte ho — aakhri 10%: khud ko sabit karo ke samajh aa gaya aur dobara kar sakte ho.

Yeh do hisse tum kisi aur ko nahi de sakte — isliye practice karne layak hain.
