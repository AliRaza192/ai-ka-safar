# Marketing Catalog — AI-Native Products ki Demand Kaise Banao

## Agar Naye Ho — Yahan Se Shuru Karo

**Is hafte:** Ek platform chunno — LinkedIn agar buyer business operator hai, X agar developer hai. Ek cheez post karo: industry observation, koi masla, ya koi sikh.

**Aglay hafte:** Usi platform par ek aur post karo.

**Is mahine:** Har hafte ek post. Calendar reminder set karo. Pehle 6 hafte pointless lagenge — chale jao.

**Is quarter:** Ek aur cheez add karo: mahine mein ek long article (1,000-1,500 alfaaz) sabse important customer question par.

**Yeh itna simple kyun?** Shuruaat mein koi bhi marketing motion meaningful results nahi deta siway founder-led content ke. Paid ads waste hain kyunki tumhein nahi pata buyer kaun hai. Agencies ke paas koi story nahi hai. Jo kaam karta hai woh hai founder, posting karna.

## Beginner ke Liye 10 Minute Version

### Marketing Motion Kya Hai?

Marketing motion woh specific tareeqa hai jisse company awareness, trust, aur demand create karti hai — kaun relationship shuru karta hai, kitna waqt lagta hai, kaunse channels use hote hain.

### Char Families

1. **Pull motions (1-4):** Audience khud tumhein dhundta hai
2. **Push motions (5-8):** Tum audience ke paas jate ho
3. **Earned motions (9-10):** Third parties tumhara message amplify karte hain
4. **Community motions (11-12):** Existing audience future audience grow karta hai

### Motion Chunne ka Aasan Tareeqa

Do sawaal: *Tumhara buyer kaun hai?* aur *ROI ke liye kitna intezaar kar sakte ho?*

- Developer buyer → Pull motions (Content + SEO, DevRel)
- Business operator → Push + targeted Pull
- Enterprise executive → Earned motions + ABM

## Executive Summary

Marketing Catalog 2026 aur uske baad AI-native products ke liye demand banane ki recipe book hai. AI Worker ke liye awareness aur pipeline banane ke bohot tareeqe hain, aur sahi tareeqa tumhare buyer, category maturity, budget, aur kitna waqt compound effects ke liye intezaar kar sakte ho par depend karta hai. Yeh document twelve motions name karta hai, unhein char families mein organize karta hai, aur batata hai kaunsi tumhari situation ke liye fit hai.

**Char families** — har qisam ki motion pehle kis cheez par compete karti hai.

_Pull motions_ (Motions 1-4) tab kaam karti hain jab audience discovery initiate karti hai. Marketer ka kaam hai findable, useful, aur credible hona jab buyer search karta hai. Audience tumhein dhundne ka kaam karti hai.

_Push motions_ (Motions 5-8) tab kaam karti hain jab marketer relationship initiate karta hai. Marketer ka kaam hai precise targeting, message-channel fit, aur conversion-rate discipline. Marketer buyer ke paas jata hai.

_Earned motions_ (Motions 9-10) tab kaam karti hain jab third parties marketer ka message amplify karte hain. Marketer ka kaam hai relationship management — journalists, analysts, podcasters, aur creators ke liye aasan banana ke tumhein feature karein, aur unke liye aasan banana ke woh acha karein.

_Community motions_ (Motions 11-12) tab kaam karti hain jab existing audience future audience grow karti hai. Marketer ka kaam hai advocacy se friction hatana aur community-building ki practice mein invest karna multi-year time horizons par.

**Paanch marketing assets** — har motion inhein capture karne ke liye compete karti hai.

_Audience_ woh log hain jin tak tum bina har baar third party ko pay kiye pahunch sakte ho. Email lists, app users, DevRel community members, podcast subscribers, social followers — sab owned audience ki forms hain.

_Authority_ tumhari credibility hai apni category mein recognized expert ki. Authority dheere dheere consistent contribution se earn hoti hai, aur jaldi ek high-profile mistake se kho jaati hai.

_Reach_ woh total set hai jiske saamne tum message rakh sakte ho — owned audience plus paid placement plus earned coverage mila kar. Reach ek flow metric hai; audience ek stock metric hai.

_Content equity_ durable inventory hai articles, talks, tools, aur resources ka jo time ke saath traffic, leads, ya trust produce karte hain. Content equity patient ke liye compound karta hai; impatient ke liye kabhi accumulate nahi hota.

_Pipeline_ marketing-attributable contribution hai qualified sales opportunities mein. Har motion ko eventually apni pipeline contribution defend karni hoti hai — chahe motion primarily baaki char assets build karta ho — kyunki eventually CFO poochega.

Sabse mazboot motions ek saath teen ya zyada assets capture karte hain. Sabse kamzor motions ek ke liye optimize karte hain (aam taur par pipeline) baqiyon ki qeemat par — jo short-term win aur long-term collapse produce karta hai.

![Paanch Marketing Assets](/img/five-marketing-assets.png)

**Scope ka note.** Yeh catalog primarily **B2B marketing** par focus karta hai — programs jo doosri businesses ko bechi jaane wali AI-native software aur services ke liye qualified sales pipeline produce karne ke liye design kiye gaye hain. Consumer-facing AI marketing (mobile app stores, paid social acquisition, consumer apps ke liye brand campaigns) mukhtalif rules follow karti hai aur yahan primary subject nahi hai, haalanke teen motions — Performance Marketing, Influencer Partnerships, aur Founder Thought Leadership — dono contexts mein apply karti hain.

**Maturity spectrum.** Har motion Proven, Emerging, ya Speculative tag ki gayi hai — is par based ke AI-native companies aaj kitni successfully chal rahi hain.

_Proven_ motions ke paas bohot saari at-scale companies hain jo in par operate kar rahi hain, established playbooks aur benchmarks ke saath.

_Emerging_ motions AI-native companies 2026 mein chal rahi hain lekin underlying tooling ke saath tezi se evolve ho rahi hain.

_Speculative_ motions un buyer behaviors ya platform dynamics par depend karti hain jo abhi scale par mojood nahi hain.

## Marketer Diagnostic: Aath Sawaalat

Motion chunne se pehle, neeche aath dimensions par khud ko honestly score karo. Har row jis motion ki taraf point karti hai, woh us condition ke saath sabse aligned hain. Jo team teen ya chaar mein High score karta hai, woh aam taur par jaldi do-teen candidate motions par narrow ho jaata hai.

1. **Buyer ki technical sophistication.** Tumhara primary buyer kitna technically literate hai? _Developer / engineer → DevRel, Content & SEO, AEO. Operator → Educational Content, Demand Gen, ABM. Executive → PR & Analyst, ABM, Customer Advocacy._

2. **Category maturity.** Tumhari category well-known hai, ya tum use define kar rahe ho? _Defining → Founder Thought Leadership, Content & SEO, PR & Analyst, Educational Content. Mature → Performance Marketing, ABM, Customer Advocacy._

3. **Average deal size.** _Under $10K → Content & SEO, AEO, Performance Marketing. $10-100K → AI-Email, Demand Gen, Influencer. $100K+ → ABM, PR & Analyst, Customer Advocacy._

4. **ROI ka time horizon.** _Hafte → Performance Marketing, Demand Gen, Customer Advocacy. Mahine → Content & SEO, AEO, AI-Email, ABM. Saal → Founder Thought Leadership, PR & Analyst, DevRel._

5. **Founder content ke liye available hai.** Kya founder regular content produce karega (essays, podcasts, videos)? _Haan → Founder Thought Leadership, Content & SEO. Nahi → Performance Marketing, Demand Gen, ABM, Influencer._

6. **Existing customer base.** Kya tumhare paas customers hain jo advocate karna chahein? _Haan → Customer Advocacy, Educational Content. Nahi → Pull aur Push motions jab tak customers worth featuring na hon._

7. **Budget ki shape.** Kya tumhara budget people par heavy hai ya media spend par? _People-heavy → Content & SEO, DevRel, PR & Analyst. Media-heavy → Performance Marketing, Demand Gen, Influencer._

8. **Audience ki location.** Kya tumhara buyer specific channels se reachable hai? _Developers (GitHub, Hacker News, X) → DevRel, Content. Executives (LinkedIn, podcasts, conferences) → Founder Thought Leadership, PR & Analyst. Mid-market operators (LinkedIn, search, email) → Performance Marketing, ABM, AI-Email._

Diagnostic tumhein nahi batata kaunsi motion sahi hai. Yeh batata hai kaunsi motions tumhare liye _available_ hain tumhari starting position ko dekhte hue. Upar ka matrix aur neeche deep sections batate hain ke available motions mein se kaunsi sharpest hai us buyer ke liye jise tum reach kar rahe ho.

## Kaunsi Motion Chalao? Faisla Guide

Ek decision flowchart sabse important sawaal sequence karti hai tumhari motion choices narrow karne ke liye.

Char key sawaal hain: (1) Kya tumhara buyer developer hai? (haan → DevRel + Content). (2) Kya tumhari category comparable competitors ke saath mature hai? (nahi → Founder Thought + PR + Education; haan → Performance + ABM + Advocacy). (3) Kya tumhare paas customers hain jinhe feature karna chahiye? (haan → abhi Customer Advocacy shuru karo; nahi → customers acquire karne ke liye Pull aur Push par focus karo). (4) ROI ke liye tumhara time horizon kya hai? (hafte → Performance + Demand Gen; saal → DevRel + PR + Founder Thought).

Zyada tar companies do-teen motions ek saath chalati hain. Sabse common combinations ke liye document ke end mein _Common Hybrid Motions_ dekho.

## Kharidar Agahi Aur Waqtbandi

Har marketing motion ko buyer ki awareness journey mein ek window milti hai. Jo buyer abhi nahi jaanta ke uska masla hai, woh marketing ka mukhtalif tareeqe se jawab deta hai — us buyer ke muqable mein jiske haath mein teen vendors ke quotes hain. Eugene Schwartz ki 1966 ki _Breakthrough Advertising_ ne buyer awareness ke paanch foundational stages define kiye; neeche ki teen-stage curve unki framework ki B2B-AI adaptation hai — 2026 mein product marketing teams ke liye teen usable stages mein consolidated.

Teen stages **Awareness Curve** define karti hain:

**Stage 1 — Unaware / Problem-Aware.** Buyer ya to nahi jaanta ke uska woh masla hai jo tumhara product solve karta hai, ya jaanta hai lekin actively solutions dhundna shuru nahi kiya. Is stage mein marketing ka kaam hai education aur frame-setting: buyer ko masla name karne mein madad karna, dikhana ke yeh kyun matter karta hai, aur sikhaana ke solution ki kaunsi categories mojood hain. Sabse achhi motions: Founder Thought Leadership, Content & SEO, PR & Analyst, Educational Content, DevRel.

**Stage 2 — Solution-Aware.** Buyer actively category research kar raha hai. Woh articles parh raha hai, approaches compare kar raha hai, podcasts sun raha hai, webinars attend kar raha hai. Abhi vendors shortlist nahi kiye. Marketing ka kaam hai ke tumhara product un channels par baar baar aur credible tareeqe se nazar aaye jahan woh search karte hain. Sabse achhi motions: Content & SEO, AEO, Educational Content, Demand Gen, Influencer Partnerships.

**Stage 3 — Vendor-Aware.** Buyer ne vendors shortlist kar liye hain aur unhein compare kar raha hai. Woh case studies parh raha hai, demos request kar raha hai, references check kar raha hai, pricing evaluate kar raha hai. Marketing ka kaam hai comparison mein friction hatana aur social proof aur technical depth provide karna jo bake-off jeet sake. Sabse achhi motions: Customer Advocacy & Case Studies, ABM, PR & Analyst (khaas taur par Gartner/Forrester-style placements), Demand Gen (late-stage technical content ke liye).

![Awareness Curve](/img/awareness-curve.png)

Geography curve ko tez ya slow karti hai. San Francisco, Seattle, Boston, New York, London, Toronto, Berlin, Bangalore, aur Singapore mein AI-native categories ke buyers do-teen saal aage hain — un ecosystems ke buyers ne pehle se AI vendors evaluate kiye hain, internal AI procurement playbooks likhe hain, aur sophisticated comparative criteria develop kiye hain. Un markets ka buyer aam taur par Stage 2 ya Stage 3 par hota hai jab woh tumhari category ke baare mein sunta hai. Duniya ka baqi hissa — continental Europe, Latin America, Middle East, Africa, aur Southeast Asia ke zyada tar enterprise buyers samait — solidly Stage 1 mein hai, Stage 2 ki taraf transition kar raha hai.

Global B2B marketing ke liye implication yeh hai ke wohi content har market mein kaam nahi karta. Stage 3 vendor-comparison page (feature-by-feature tables aur pricing-tier breakdowns ke saath) San Francisco buyer ke liye bilkul sahi hai aur Sao Paulo buyer ke liye bilkul evaluate karne ke liye tayar nahi hai. Stage 1 educational article (category define karna, explain karna ke yeh kyun matter karta hai, masla name karna) Sao Paulo buyer ke liye bilkul sahi hai aur San Francisco buyer ise too basic samajh kar scroll past karega. Ek stage ke liye calibrated marketing programs aur markets across target karna doosre stage ke buyers par bura land hota hai.

Stage-mismatched marketing ki cost compound hoti hai. Stage 3 buyers ke liye calibrated motion (case studies, comparison content, aur ROI calculators par heavy) Stage 1 buyers par confusing land hoti hai — unke paas abhi comparison interpret karne ka context nahi hai, aur woh bounce kar jaate hain. Stage 1 buyers ke liye calibrated motion (category education aur problem-naming par heavy) Stage 3 buyers par too basic land hoti hai — woh educational stage se guzar chuke hain, aur woh content ko signal parhte hain ke vendor unsophisticated hai. Global B2B marketers ke liye fix hai stage-appropriate content libraries maintain karna aur traffic market se route karna: emerging markets ke liye Stage 1 educational paths, established AI markets ke liye Stage 3 comparison paths, localization ke saath jo translation se aage jaaye aur local buyer ki actual research address kare.

## Maturity Legend

* **Proven.** Motion ke paas bohot saari AI-native (aur pre-AI) companies hain jo aaj isse scale par chal rahi hain, established playbooks aur benchmarks ke saath.
* **Emerging.** Motion AI-native companies 2026 mein chal rahi hain lekin tezi se evolve ho rahi hai — canonical playbook abhi stabilize nahi hua.
* **Speculative.** Motion un buyer behaviors ya platform dynamics par depend karti hain jo abhi scale par mojood nahi hain.

## A. Pull Motions

### Motion 1 — Content & SEO Marketing
*Proven. Beginner: Easy.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Public library banao — articles, guides, comparison pages jo buyer ke sawaalon ka jawab dein. Search engines road signs hain. Library compounds: har naya article chance badhata hai ke agla visitor tumhein dhunde.

**Kab use karein:** Hamesha, har B2B AI-native company ke liye.

**Execution disciplines:** Keyword research, production depth (original research/data), distribution (LinkedIn, email, X)

**Primary Risk:** Content velocity without distribution — consistently publish karo lekin koi nahi dekhta.

**Pehla qadam:** Highest-intent search query dhundo. Us par internet par best article likho. Aglay hafte doosri query par.

**Core Idea.** Evergreen content produce karo jo buyer ke sawaalon ka jawab de, search mein rank kare, aur time ke saath value mein compound kare.

**Mechanism.** Content marketing isliye kaam karta hai kyunki B2B buyers apni research ka zyada tar kisi vendor se contact karne se pehle karte hain. Content layer woh jagah hai jeh yeh research hoti hai. Jo company apni category mein high-intent search results own karti hai, use pre-qualified buyers ki constant stream milti hai jo masle ke baare mein pehle se educated hokar aate hain.

Economics motion ko durable banati hain. Ek achi tarah produce ki gayi 1,500-lafzon ki article 2026 mein taqreeban $300-$1,500 mein banti hai (AI madad se tez aur sasta, jab credible domain experts se commissioned ho to mehngi). Ek high-intent category mein ranking article saalon tak qualified leads produce karti hai. Math har mahine behtar hota hai jab article ranking continue karta hai.

Execution mein teen disciplines chahiye. _Keyword research_ tumhare buyer ke actual questions map karta hai. _Production depth_ 2026 mein production volume se zyada matter karti hai — shallow content AI se commoditize ho rahi hai. Jeetne wali articles woh hain jin mein original research, original data, customer quotes, ya arguments hain jo competitors aasani se replicate nahi kar sakte. _Distribution_ content ko pure search se aage dekhta hai — har published article ko ek distribution checklist chahiye (LinkedIn post, X thread, email blast, partner inclusion, podcast mention) jo deliberately execute ho.

Constraint patience hai. Search rankings compound hone mein chah mahine se barah mahine lagte hain; company ko weak-looking metrics ke lambe daur mein consistent content production fund karni hoti hai jab tak trend na badle. Jo founders mahine char par program band karte hain — jab kuch nahi ho raha lagta hai — woh is motion mein sabse common failure pattern hain.

**Walk-through.** _Imagine PromptForge_, ek AI tool prompt engineering ke liye. Team aath mahine ke liye hafte mein do long-form articles publish karne ka faisla karti hai — comparison guides, tutorials, case studies. Mahine nau tak, "best AI prompt engineering tools" PromptForge ko top result deta hai. Aath mahine tak, company search se do hazar qualified leads mahina mein leti hai, $50 se neeche CAC ke saath — paid acquisition se orders of magnitude neeche.

**Example.** _Confirmed examples:_ HubSpot ne content-led inbound playbook par multi-billion-dollar company banayi. AI-native mein, playbook reproduce ho raha hai — Anthropic ka blog, OpenAI ke research posts, aur AI-native company blogs ki long tail sab inbound funnels anchor karti hain.

### Motion 2 — Answer-Engine Optimization (AEO)
*Emerging. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Agar SEO "Google par kaise dikho" tha, AEO hai "ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity mein kaise dikho." Buyer AI assistant se pooche "best AI tool for legal research" — tumhara product cited ho.

**Teen vectors:**
- Citation worthiness (original research, unique data)
- Brand mention frequency (news, reviews, podcasts mein)
- Schema and structure (clean hierarchy, named entities)

**Primary Risk:** Optimizing without content worth citing.

**Pehla qadam:** Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity se 10 buyer questions poochho. Dekho kahan tum aur competitors aate hain.

**Core Idea.** AI assistants ko yeh dilao ke woh tumhari brand cite karein jab woh tumhari category mein sawaalon ka jawab dein.

**Mechanism.** AEO teen vectors se kaam karta hai. _Citation worthiness_: AI assistants un sources ko cite karte hain jinhe woh credible samajhte hain — jo widely linked-to hain, well-structured hain, unique data contain karte hain, ya domain authority rakhte hain. _Brand mention frequency_: AI assistants training data composition se influence hote hain. Jo brands news, reviews, podcasts, aur analyst reports mein baar baar nazar aate hain — khaas taur par named contexts mein — woh zyada cite hote hain. _Schema aur structure_: AI assistants clean structure, clear claims, aur verifiable facts wali content ko cite karna pasand karte hain.

Constraint measurement hai. AEO ke paas abhi Google Search Console jaisa equivalent nahi hai — AI answers mein tumhari brand kitni baar cite hoti hai, yeh track karne ka koi saaf tareeqa nahi hai. 2026 mein AEO chalane wali companies proxy metrics se kaam karti hain: brand-search lifts, self-reported attribution, aur dedicated AEO measurement tools.

Strategic implication yeh hai ke AEO authority se downstream hai. Jis brand ke paas koi original research nahi, koi analyst coverage nahi, koi podcast presence nahi, aur koi creator mentions nahi, AI assistants ke paas usse cite karne ke liye kuch nahi — aur koi bhi schema optimization ise fix nahi karta.

**Walk-through.** _Imagine LegalAgent_, ek AI legal-research tool. Team systematic tareeqe se apne docs, blog posts, aur product pages ko structured FAQs, clear "what is" definitions, aur original legal sources ke citations se update karti hai. Chah mahine baad, team prospect signups dekhna shuru karti hai jo "How did you find us?" ka jawab dete hain: "Maine Claude se poocha ke M&A diligence ke liye kaunse tools use karoon aur tumhara aaya." Traffic source Google Analytics ke liye invisible hai — conversational hai, click-through nahi — lekin asli hai aur grow ho raha hai.

**Example.** _Emerging analogues:_ Profound aur Athena jaisi companies AEO measurement tools bana rahi hain. Zyada tar badi content-driven SaaS companies 2026 mein AEO experiments chalati hain; kuch ne systematic playbooks publish kiye hain.

### Motion 3 — Founder Thought Leadership
*Proven. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Shehr mein sabse respected expert bano — jo us problem ke baare mein baat karta hai jo tumhara product solve karta hai. Woh expert ka business default par hire hota hai.

**Kab use karein:** Jab founder credible domain expert ho, category nayi ho, aur commitment 24+ mahine ki ho.

**Primary Risk:** Sporadic posting momentum kill karta hai.

**Pehla qadam:** Ek platform chunno. 60 hafte ke liye hafte mein ek post commit karo. Pehle 6 mahine mein kuch nahi lagega — chale jao.

**Core Idea.** Company ki earned authority ek person — founder — mein concentrate karo, aur us person ki publishing cadence se ek durable audience banao.

**Mechanism.** Founder thought leadership isliye kaam karti hai kyunki B2B audiences brands se zyada logon par bharosa karte hain. Founder accounts aam taur par same content par company accounts se behtar perform karte hain — founder posts meaningful tareeqe se zyada reach aur engagement produce karte hain. Time ke saath, founder ki audience ek captive marketing channel ban jaati hai jiska har impression par koi cost nahi hai — aur competitors replicate nahi kar sakte, kyunki unke paas tumhara founder nahi hai.

Execution mein teen disciplines chahiye: consistent publishing cadence (primary platform par hafte mein do baar minimum — B2B operators ke liye LinkedIn, technical audiences ke liye X), ek defensible point of view (discussion provoke karne ke liye kaafi controversial, defend karne ke liye kaafi sachhi), aur publicly engage karne ki willingness (comments ka jawab dena, podcasts host karna, live conversations karna). Jo founders writing ya engagement outsource karte hain woh content produce karte hain jo inauthentic padhta hai aur durable audience build nahi karta.

Constraint founder ka waqt hai. Ek serious thought-leadership program founder se hafte mein paanch se das ghante consume karta hai.

**Walk-through.** _Imagine DataSpace_, ek founder-led AI analytics company. Founder, Maria, LinkedIn par hafte mein ek essay publish karne ka faisla karti hai — analytics market par sharp opinions, customer war stories, category kahan ja rahi hai ki predictions. Aath mahine baad, uski LinkedIn following 80,000 hai. Tees mahine baad, 200,000. Jab DataSpace naya product launch karta hai, launch post 48 ghanton mein 10,000 demo requests drive karta hai — effectively zero CAC ke liye.

**Example.** _Confirmed examples:_ Mathilde Collin (Front), Andrew Wilkinson (Tiny), Lenny Rachitsky (Lenny's Newsletter), David Cancel (Drift), Sahil Lavingia (Gumroad). AI-native mein, Sarah Tavel (Benchmark, AI investing par) aur kuch Anthropic aur OpenAI researchers ne personal audiences banayi hain jo unki companies ya funds ki awareness anchor karti hain.

### Motion 4 — Educational Content & Certification
*Proven. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** University chalao jahan graduates customers bhi hain. Courses, tutorials, certifications banao — tumhara product foundation ho.

**Compounding effects:** Top-of-funnel acquisition, trust/dependency, network effects (graduates aglon ko recommend karte hain)

**Primary Risk:** Content production without graduation funnel — graduates product nahi bante.

**Core Idea.** Apni category ke liye credentialing system banao — woh jagah jahan practitioners skill seekhte hain — aur ise lifelong customer acquisition channel ki tarah use karo.

**Mechanism.** Educational content teen compounding effects se kaam karta hai. _Top-of-funnel acquisition_: log "how to do X" search karte hain aur tumhara course dhundte hain; course practitioners ki constant stream produce karta hai. _Trust aur dependency_: graduates tumhari terminology, frameworks, aur tooling preferences apni baqi zindagi ke liye use karte hain — matlab jab woh hiring authority tak pahunchte hain to tumhara product default hota hai. _Network effects_: graduates next-cohort students ko sikhate hain, meetups mein present karte hain, blog posts likhte hain, aur peers ko recommend karte hain — muft, kyunki woh apne earned credential mein emotionally invested hain.

Economics kaam karti hain kyunki curriculum reusable hai. Pehli cohort produce karne mein sabse zyada cost aati hai — typically serious certification program ke liye $50K-$300K. Baqi cohorts near-zero marginal cost par chalti hain. Jo programs certification $200-$2,000 per learner par price karte hain woh apne aap mein meaningful revenue centers ban sakte hain.

Constraint curriculum quality hai. Educational content genuinely useful honi chahiye warna woh koi graduates nahi deti jo worth hon — aur bura hai, woh graduates produce karti hain jo tumhare product ke khilaaf advocate karte hain kyunki certification ne real skill development nahi di.

**Walk-through.** _Imagine PromptCert_, ek AI-native company jo AI Engineering Certification offer karti hai. Team ek free 12-hour curriculum banati hai jo prompt engineering, evaluation design, aur agent architecture cover karti hai, $200 paid certification exam ke saath. Pehle saal, 50,000 practitioners free curriculum complete karte hain aur 8,000 certification ke liye pay karte hain. Doosre saal, certified practitioners apne LinkedIn profiles par "PromptCert Certified" list karte hain, advocates unofficial study groups sikhate hain, aur PromptCert AI engineering hires ke liye de facto credential ban jaata hai.

**Example.** _Confirmed examples:_ HubSpot Academy, Salesforce Trailhead, Google Skillshop, Snowflake University. AI-native mein: DeepLearning.AI, Anthropic Academy (2026 mein development mein), aur kuch Cohere/Mistral practitioner programs.

## B. Push Motions

### Motion 5 — Performance Marketing
*Proven. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Click per dhyan rent karo. Google, LinkedIn, Meta, TikTok par paid ads. Kaam karta hai jab unit economics clear hon.

**Math clear hona chahiye:**
- LTV/CAC &gt; 3
- CAC payback &lt; 18 months
- Cohort LTV empirically validated (modeled nahi)

**Primary Risk:** Paid acquisition without unit economics.

**Pehla qadam:** $5,000 test run karo best channel par. CAC aur conversion rates measure karo. Scale sirf tab jab math kaam kare.

**Core Idea.** Scale par targeted attention kharido, acquired customer ki cost ko lifetime value ke relative disciplined measurement ke saath.

**Mechanism.** Performance marketing isliye kaam karta hai kyunki platforms (Google, LinkedIn, Meta, TikTok) ki duniya ki sabse valuable targeting data hai. Google jaanta hai log kya actively search kar rahe hain. LinkedIn jaanta hai woh kahan kaam karte hain, kya karte hain, aur kitne senior hain. Meta jaanta hai unke interests, behaviors, aur life events. Advertiser woh targeting rent karta hai aur targeted attention ko traffic, leads, ya sales mein convert karta hai.

Math teen thresholds clear karni chahiye motion safely scale karne ke liye. _LTV/CAC > 3_ — standard B2B SaaS benchmark; 3 se neeche, program payback periods, retention, aur discount rates factor hone par paisa kho deta hai. _CAC payback 18 mahine se kam_ cash ko pre-revenue cohorts mein trap hone se rokta hai. _Cohort LTV empirically validated_ — modeled nahi, projected nahi — spend scale karne se pehle. Performance marketing mein sabse common failure pattern spend scale karna optimistic LTV projections par jo cohort eventually deliver nahi karta.

**Walk-through.** _Imagine SyncFlow_, ek $50/month AI productivity tool. Team "AI scheduling assistant" aur "calendar AI" terms par Google Ads chalata hai. Woh $4 per click pay karte hain; 2% clicks free-trial signups mein convert hote hain; 15% free trials paid mein convert hote hain. CAC $166 per customer hai, $600 LTV ke muqable mein. Math kaam karti hai, team spend ko $10K/month se $200K/month tak twelve months mein scale karti hai. Aath mahine baad, paid CAC $220 tak badh gaya hai (zyada competitors bidding) aur team Content & SEO banana shuru karta hai taake blended CAC neeche aaye.

**Example.** _Confirmed pattern:_ Aam taur par har B2B SaaS company Performance Marketing chalati hai. AI-native mein, Performance Marketing self-serve products (Cursor, Linear, Notion AI, Perplexity Pro) aur category leaders (OpenAI ke brand campaigns, Microsoft Copilot) ke liye sabse zyada visible hai.

### Motion 6 — Demand Generation Programs
*Proven. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Prospective customers ke liye party — entry ticket email address. Webinars, white papers, gated reports.

**Funnel:** Traffic → Gated content → Email → Nurture → Sales handoff

**Primary Risk:** Webinars without follow-through — leads cool ho jate hain, pipeline nahi banta.

**Pehla qadam:** Highest-stakes buyer question identify karo. 30-page thorough answer produce karo. Gate karo, promote karo, leads nurture karo.

**Core Idea.** Contact information ke liye useful content exchange karo, phir contacts ko systematic nurture se pipeline mein convert karo.

**Mechanism.** Demand gen teen-stage funnel se kaam karta hai. _Acquisition_: paid ads ya organic distribution traffic ko landing page par drive karte hain jahan users contact info content se exchange karte hain. _Nurture_: contact emails, retargeting ads, aur personalized content ke sequence mein enter hota hai jo use awareness curve mein aage badhata hai. _Sales handoff_: jab contact behavioral threshold tak pahunchta hai (multiple downloads, repeated visits, calendar request), use sales ko qualified lead ki tarah route kiya jaata hai.

Typical mid-market B2B demand-gen programs mein 1-5% paid traffic gated-content downloads mein convert hoti hai, 5-15% leads nurture sequences se meaningfully engage karti hain, aur 5-10% engaged leads sales-qualified opportunities mein convert hoti hain. Overall paid-traffic-to-pipeline conversion rate typically 0.05-0.5% hai — matlab gated asset ki paid promotion ko ek qualified opportunity generate karne ke liye hazaron impressions produce karni padti hain.

Doosra constraint content quality aur freshness hai. Gated asset genuinely useful honi chahiye warna lead gussa hota hai warmed nahi. Aur opt-in permission decay hoti hai — jisne nau mahine pehle tumhari white paper download ki aur tab se engage nahi kiya, woh ab warm lead nahi hai.

**Walk-through.** _Imagine FinanceAI_, FP&A teams ke liye ek AI tool. Team 40-page report produce karta hai — "The State of FP&A Automation in 2026" — email form ke peeche gated. Woh ise Performance Marketing, partner emails, aur PR se promote karte hain. Chah hafte mein, woh 8,000 contacts capture karte hain. Un mein se 1,200 12-week nurture sequence mein enter karte hain. Un mein se 80 sales meetings book karte hain. Un mein se 25 average $30K ACV par customers ban jaate hain. Program $750K pipeline produce karta hai taqreeban $1,200 CAC par.

**Example.** _Confirmed examples:_ Gartner ki annual reports, HubSpot ki State of Marketing report, Salesforce ki State of Sales report. AI-native mein: Anthropic ki Economic Index reports, OpenAI ke research publications, company-published industry reports ki long tail.

### Motion 7 — Account-Based Marketing (ABM)
*Proven. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Broad fishing ki bajay targeted hunting. 50-200 specific companies select karo. Har ek ko personalize karo — custom landing pages, industry-specific ads, executive-name direct mail.

**Key requirement:** Sales alignment — same named-account list, weekly review

**Primary Risk:** ABM without sales alignment — expensive campaigns without follow-up.

**Pehla qadam:** 25 most-wanted accounts identify karo. Sales ko brief karo. 6-month coordinated awareness campaign chalao.

**Core Idea.** Marketing spend aur personalization ko named accounts ki finite list par concentrate karo, sales ke saath tight coordination mein.

**Mechanism.** ABM teen coordinated tracks se kaam karta hai. _Awareness_: har target account branded ads (LinkedIn, IP-targeted display, podcast) consistently mahino tak dekhta hai kisi bhi sales conversation se pehle. _Personalization_: jab sales engage karta hai, buyer ne pehle se brand dekha hai, messaging unki specific industry/use case fit karti hai, aur marketing assets pre-customized hain. _Joint orchestration_: marketing aur sales named accounts par hafte waar milte hain, intelligence share karte hain, aur touches coordinate karte hain.

Constraint sales-marketing alignment hai. Bina aligned sales execution ke ABM sirf mehngi marketing hai. Jo companies ABM achi tarah chalati hain unke paas ek single shared list hoti hai, hafte waar account reviews hain, aur tight feedback loops hain; jo buri tarah chalati hain unke paas marketing accounts ke liye personalization karti hai jinhe sales actively pursue nahi kar raha.

**Walk-through.** _Imagine ClaimsAI_, insurance carriers ke liye ek AI tool. Marketing team aur sales team 75 target carriers par agree karte hain. Marketing chah mahine mein $300K spend karta hai carrier-specific LinkedIn ads chala kar, executive teams ko direct mail bhej kar, industry podcasts sponsor karke, aur carrier-specific case studies ke saath custom landing pages banakar. Sales ABM-aligned outbound chalata hai. Nau mahine baad, 75 carriers mein se 22 ne sales se engage kiya hai; 8 active evaluation mein hain; 3 average ACV $850K par close karte hain. Program $2.5M ARR produce karta hai taqreeban $100K CAC par — absolute terms mein high lekin deal size ke liye excellent.

**Example.** _Confirmed examples:_ ABM playbook Sangram Vajre aur doosron ki kitaabon mein documented hai. AI-native mein: zyada tar enterprise AI vendors (Glean, Harvey, Sierra, Writer) apne sabse bade target accounts ke liye ABM motions chalate hain.

### Motion 8 — AI-Augmented Email & Outreach
*Emerging. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Library jahan har subscriber slightly different newsletter mile — specifically unke liye likha. AI agents personalization scale par karte hain.

**Disciplines:** Prompt design, segmentation depth, human-in-the-loop quality control

**Primary Risk:** AI-generated content without distinction — recipients detect aur ignore karte hain.

**Core Idea.** AI agents istemal karo email, newsletters, aur digital outreach ko personalize aur scale karne ke liye — human marketing teams jo produce kar sakte hain usse aage.

**Mechanism.** AI-augmented email isliye kaam karta hai kyunki traditional email marketing mein limiting factor hamesha personalization aur scale ke beech trade-off tha. Humans din mein darzon contacts ko deeply personalized emails likh sakte the; AI agents hazaron ko personalized emails likh sakte hain. Constraint production volume se distinctiveness mein shift hoti hai — jab AI industry bhar mein millions personalized emails simultaneously generate kar raha hai, to "valuable" email ka bar tezi se badhta hai.

Execution mein teen disciplines chahiye jo AI augmentation se pehle mojood nahi thin. _Prompt design_ — AI ki draft quality prompt se bound hoti hai; teams jo prompt engineering ko marketing-operations function ki tarah treat karti hain (test, version-control, aur refine karti hain) dramatically better output produce karti hain. _Segmentation depth_ — AI personalization well-defined segments par sabse effective hoti hai. _Human-in-the-loop quality control_ — higher-stakes communications ke liye, send se pehle human review jo point-of-view, opinion, ya personal context inject kare, woh AI-augmented ko AI-replaced se alag karta hai.

Doosra constraint deliverability infrastructure hai. High-volume AI-augmented email ESP penalties, spam classification, aur domain reputation damage trigger kar sakta hai jo theek karne mein mahine lagta hai.

**Walk-through.** _Imagine GrowthCRM_, ek B2B sales-tools company jiske paas 50,000-contact email list hai. Team AI agents use karta hai weekly newsletter content generate karne ke liye jo saat mukhtalif buyer segments ke liye tailored ho — sirf subject-line personalization nahi, content-personalization. Open rates 18% se 31% tak badhte hain; click rates 1.4% se 3.9% tak. Newsletter company ka sabse bada single source of pipeline ban jaata hai, 30% qualified inbound produce karta hai.

**Example.** _Emerging analogues:_ Lavender, Smartlead, Hyperbound, aur AI-native sales-and-marketing tools ki long tail AI-augmented email productize kar rahi hain. Zyada tar AI-native vendors jinke paas substantial email lists hain 2026 mein AI augmentation ka koi form chalate hain.

*Proven. Beginner: Advanced.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Doosron ka trust borrow karo. Tier-1 press (WSJ, Bloomberg, TechCrunch), analyst firms (Forrester, Gartner, IDC) mein coverage earn karo.

**Teen vectors:** Press relationships, analyst placements, speaking/conference circuit

**Timeline:** 12-24 month investment before measurable returns

**Primary Risk:** Vanity coverage jo pipeline nahi move karta.

**Core Idea.** Third-party media mein placements earn karo jin par tumhara bharosa karta hai, aur un placements ko compounding brand authority mein convert karo.

Aath mahine baad, SecureAI ko Gartner "Cool Vendor" name karta hai. Chaubees mahine baad, woh Forrester Wave mein "Strong Performer" ki tarah nazar aate hain. Placements 200+ inbound enterprise inquiries produce karti hain — aur un inquiries se jo deals close hote hain woh company ki history mein sabse bade hain.

**Key discipline:** Creator-audience fit (follower count vanity metric hai, engagement matters)

**Primary Risk:** Misalignment with creator's audience.

**Core Idea.** Un creators ke saath pay ya partner karo jinke paas tumhare buyer ki pehle se attention hai; audience build karne ki bajay rent karo.

## D. Community Motions

### Motion 11 — Developer Relations (DevRel)
*Proven. Beginner: Advanced.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Clubhouse banao jahan developers hang out karna chahein. Technical content, sample apps, hackathons, ambassador programs — tumhara product natural starting point bane.

**Critical mistake:** DevRel ko marketing budget se treat karna — product budget se treat karo.

**Primary Risk:** Community backlash — misjudged announcement trust damage karta hai.

**Pehla qadam:** Ek DevRel engineer hire karo technically credible. Ek excellent sample app ship karo, ek community event host karo.

**Core Idea.** Technical community banao jis par practitioners bharosa karte hain, aur apne product ko space mein build karne wale community members ke liye natural choice banao.

**Mechanism.** DevRel teen compounding effects se kaam karta hai. _Trust through technical authenticity_: developers marketing fluff ki tolerance kam rakhte hain; DevRel content technically accurate aur useful honi chahiye warna backlash hota hai. _Community advocacy_: jo developers tumhara product use karte hain aur tumhari team se respected feel karte hain woh peers ko recommend karte hain — growth jo paid acquisition replicate nahi kar sakta. _Ambassador effects_: kam tayadad mein high-credibility developers (5-50) community signal ka disproportionate share drive karte hain; un relationships mein invest karna compound returns deta hai.

Execution mein teen disciplines chahiye: technically credible developer-relations engineers hire karo (former practitioners, pure marketers nahi), genuinely useful technical content mein invest karo (sample apps, working code, deep guides — surface-level tutorials nahi), aur community ko product ki tarah treat karo (community ki needs hain jo serve karni hain; unhein achi tarah serve karna woh hai jo trust banata hai).

### Motion 12 — Customer Advocacy & Case Studies
*Proven. Beginner: Easy.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Existing customers ko sales force mein badlo. Case studies, testimonials, customer-led webinars, referral programs.

**Operations:** Ek case study per month, structured reference program, advocacy ladder

**Primary Risk:** Case studies as one-offs — hafte mein 3 ki bajay saal mein 3.

## Cross-Cutting Concepts

Kai concepts motions mein aati hain aur ek baar define hone ki haqdaar hain har baar repeat karne ki bajay.

**Attribution aur multi-touch journeys.** B2B buyers aam taur par sales-qualified lead banne se pehle 7-15 touchpoints se interact karte hain. Ek buyer blog post parh sakta hai (Motion 1), LinkedIn ad dekh sakta hai (Motion 5), webinar download kar sakta hai (Motion 6), creator partnership se guzar sakta hai (Motion 10), aur akhir mein peer recommendation ke baad sign up kar sakta hai (Motion 12). Single-touch attribution (sirf last interaction count karna) systematically pull aur earned motions ko underweight karta hai; multi-touch attribution (journey bhar mein credit distribute karna) zyada accurate hai lekin operationalize karna mushkil hai. Jo companies attribution mein under-invest karti hain woh sabse measurable channels (Performance Marketing) ko over-fund karti hain aur sabse compounding ones (Founder Thought Leadership, DevRel, PR) ko under-fund karti hain.

**Owned/earned/paid framework.** Ek foundational marketing taxonomy. _Owned media_ woh hai jo tum control karte ho (tumhari website, email list, app, community). _Earned media_ woh hai jo doosre tumhein dete hain (press, analyst reports, organic mentions). _Paid media_ woh hai jo tum rent karte ho (advertising). Sabse sehatmand marketing programs teeno ko blend karte hain; paid par over-reliant programs ko margin problems hain; owned par over-reliant programs ko reach problems hain; earned par over-reliant programs ko predictability problems hain.

**Brand vs. demand-gen tension.** Brand marketing long-term recognition aur trust banata hai; demand-gen marketing near-term qualified leads produce karta hai. Dono har marketing org mein budget ke liye compete karte hain. Pure-demand-gen programs ceiling hit karte hain — jab tak actively searching buyers ki harvest ho jaati hai, growth stall hoti hai jab brand investment addressable audience expand kare. Pure-brand programs unaccountable hain — woh awareness generate karte hain jise koi pipeline mein translate prove nahi kar sakta. Sabse sehatmand programs budget ko roughly 60/40 demand-gen aur brand ke beech split karte hain, accept karte hain ke brand wala hissa imprecisely measure hoga, aur multi-year horizons par brand investment ke compounding effects ka faida uthate hain.

**MarTech stack.** 2026 mein marketing chalane wali infrastructure typically shaamil hai: CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, ya AI-native equivalent), marketing automation platform (HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot), attribution/analytics stack (Google Analytics 4, Segment, AI-native attribution tool), ad-platform aggregator (Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads), email-deliverability stack (SendGrid, Postmark, Mailgun), content management system (company website), aur badh raha hai AI-augmented content production stack (LLM tools, image generation, video generation).

## Common Hybrid Motions

| Sequence | Kaise Kaam Karta Hai |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Content & SEO → DevRel** | Content seed karta hai community; community compound karta hai |
| **Founder Thought → PR** | Personal authority institutional credibility mein convert hoti hai |
| **Performance → Demand Gen** | Paid media gated content drive karta hai, email nurture convert karta hai |
| **ABM → Customer Advocacy** | ABM customers produce karta hai; advocacy next ABM cycle fuel karta hai |
| **Educational Content → Customer Advocacy** | Graduates customers bante hain; customers evangelists bante hain |

## AI Ne Marketing Kaise Badla

1. **AI-generated content at infinite scale** — Average content bury ho jati hai; original research kaam karta hai
2. **AEO replacing SEO** — AI assistants se research growing hai
3. **AI-augmented buyer evaluation** — Buyers AI se vendor compare karte hain
4. **Generative ads at near-zero cost** — 5 variants/month se 50/month
5. **New role: AI Marketing Engineer** — AI-augmented stack build karta hai

3. **Earned (9-10):** Third parties message amplify karte hain
4. **Community (11-12):** Existing audience future audience grow karta hai

**Jawab:**

3. **Reach** — total log jo message dekh sakte hain
4. **Content Equity** — durable articles/resources jo time ke saath value produce karein
5. **Pipeline** — marketing-attributable qualified sales opportunities

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 4 / 7** — Solo founder ke liye sabse important motion kya hai?</summary>
