# Yeh Kya Roles Seekhati Hai?

> Bazaar titles ko define karne se tez bana raha hai. Zyada tar titles wohi discipline hain alag alag gehrai mein: wohi discipline jo yeh kitaab sikhata hai. Yeh naqsha hai, aur yeh kitaab aapko har role kitna door tak le jaati hai.

## Har Title ke Peeche ka Sawaal

Itihaas kaan Yuval Noah Harari ne modern taleem ka sabse mushkil sawal uthaya hai: pehli baar itihaas mein, hamein nahi pata das saal baad job market kaisa hoga, jo ka matlab hai ke aaj ke naujawanon ko kya sikhayein. Purana jawab saaf tha: unhein coding sikhao. Woh jawab toot raha hai, kyunke AI coding seekh raha hai, aur ek saal mein zyada tar code insaanon se behtar likh sakta hai. Syntax sikhana woh skill sikhana hai jo machine real time mein absorb kar rahi hai.

Yeh page kitaab ka jawab hai Harari ke sawal par. Syntax likhne walon ko mat sikhao. Un logon ko sikhao jo machine ke oonche khade hain: jo batate hain kya banana hai, AI Workers ko supervise karte hain jo banate hain, aur wapas aane wale ko verify karte hain. Syntax automate ho sakta hai. Judgment, specification, aur deployment nahi: yeh seedhiyon par upar jaate hain jab machine chadhti hai. Har niche wala role us seedhi ka ek kursi hai, aur is page par demand data dikhata hai ke bazaar waqai isi ke liye pay kar raha hai: kyunke woh bhi Harari ke sawal ka jawab nahi de sakta, aur woh un logon ko hire kar raha hai jo de sakte hain.

Yahan hum naye agentic AI era ke roles define karte hain: woh jobs jo isliye maujood hain kyunke ab companies AI Workers manufacture karti hain, chalati hain, aur un par governance karti hain. Entries us tarah sorted hain jaise kaam waqai cluster karta hai, aur har ke saath woh verdict hai jo imaandari se scope line batati hai: yeh kitaab aapko kitna door tak le jaati hai, aur certification tracks kahan se le lete hain. Verdicts names se zyada ahem hain. Jahan kitaab rukti hai, woh kehti hai. Aur kyunke role sirf uske peechhe ki demand jitna real hai, yeh page har market mein demand track karta hai jo in roles ke liye hire kar raha hai: AI labs, hyperscalers, consulting bade, independent services firms, aur khula freelance market.

**Naqsha: teen levels, ek line.** Poora page ek jumle mein bandha hai. Yeh kitaab aapko AI Workers ([Digital FTEs](/docs/digital-fte-crash-course)) banane sikhati hai, aur un Workers ko mila kar ek company banati hai jo un par chalti hai (AI-Native Company). Job market ke paas is insaan ke liye ek garam naya naam hai: Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE). Toh teen levels hain, aur har ek agla banata hai: insaan (aap), unit (aap banaya AI Worker), aur enterprise (woh company jismein Workers jama hoti hain). Is page har role ya toh us line par ek stop hai, ya line ko support karne wala role hai, ya kitaab imaandari se uske gird boundary khinchi hai. Naqshay se pehle ek aur baat: "FDE" batata hai aap kahan kaam karte hain, nahi ke kya jaante hain. Client ki company ke andar kaam karein toh bazaar aapko FDE kehta hai. Usi company mein hire ho jaayein toh wohi skills aapko unka AI-Native Company Architect banati hai. Kitaab skills sikhata hai. Bazaar naam chunta hai.

Woh definition sahi hai, aur adhuri bhi hai, kyunke pata darwaze se kuch nahi batata ke aap kya le ke andar ja rahe hain. Vendor ka FDE vendor ki platform le ke aata hai. Vendor-neutral wala kya le ke aata hai — yeh mushkil sawal hai, aur iska ek precise jawab hai: [do Systems of Record](#pod-mein-kya-hai-do-systems-of-record), ek use di gayi aur ek usne khud banayi. Woh section neeche FDE naqshay ke andar hai, kyunke jawab tabhi samajh aata hai jab aapne dekha ho ke vendor ka version kaisa dikhta hai.

Sab ek hi Foundations par shuru karte hain, browser skills jo har reader ko agent kaam se pehle chahiye. Us floor par general agent use ke do modes hain. Mode 1 hai general agent istemal karke apna kaam tez karna, yeh har reader ki proficiency hai, koi job title nahi. [Mode 2](/docs/mode-2-manufacturing) hai AI Workers banana jo aapke liye kaam karein, aur wahan job titles hain. Naqsha Foundations floor aur Mode 1 Practitioner se shuru hota hai, phir Mode 2 roles par aata hai, jo ke lagbhag sab kuch hain.

Naye shabdon mein (Digital FTE, SKILL.md, Agent Factory)? [Thesis](/docs/thesis) aur [glossary](/docs/glossary) se shuru karein, kyunke yeh page unhein assume karta hai.

## Sab ki shuruwat ka baseline

Foundations: floor, dono modes se pehle. Har reader ek hi tarah shuru karta hai, browser tab mein, Foundations par: kaise prompt karein, agentic kaam ki do document languages, code commission karna jo aap kabhi nahi likhte, skills aur connectors, aur AI era mein kaise sochein. Koi mode nahi, koi role nahi, kuch install nahi karna. Yeh woh floor hai jis par poora naqsha khada hai. Jahan sab shuru karte hain, koi title jo aap rakhein nahi.

Mode 1 Practitioner: title nahi, proficiency. Us floor par, aap general agent istemal karte hain apna kaam tez karne ke liye: sochne, likhne, code karne, analyze karne, plan karne, ek outcome bhejne, aur session band karne ke liye. Yeh Mode 1 hai, aur yeh kitaab ise sab ke liye sikhata hai, engineers Claude Code ya OpenCode ke zariye, domain experts Claude Cowork ya OpenWork ke zariye, General Agent Problem Solving ke Seven Principles ke under. Yeh pehla mode hai jo har reader Mode 2 roles se pehle chalata hai, aur yeh aapko aapke maujooda kaam mein tez banata hai, naya kaam nahi deta. Pehla mode jo sab chalate hain, koi title jo aap rakhein nahi.

## Generalist core

Yeh core roles ek single pipeline ke taur par chalte hain, intent se production tak: Outcome Architect (kya) → Digital FTE Builder (banana) → AI-Native Company Architect (system) → Cloud AI Engineer (chalana). Apni company ke andar chalayein toh yeh chaar roles hain. Client ke andar chalayein, ek embedded, vendor-neutral engineer ke zariye end to end, toh yeh Forward Deployed Engineer hai. Naqshay par baaki sab is line ko support karta hai, extend karta hai, ya bound karta hai.

![Core pipeline: chaar internal roles, ya ek embedded Forward Deployed Engineer client ke paas](/img/core-pipeline.png) Chaar roles apni company ke andar line chalate hain. Ek embedded engineer wohi line client ke andar le ke jaata hai.

**Outcome Architect:** intent ka maalik hai, execution nahi. Agent era mein kaam teen hisson mein banta hai: intent, execution, verification. Worker execution ka maalik hai. Yeh role intent ka maalik hai. Yeh decide karta hai ke Worker kya achieve kare, spec likhta hai jo use fix karta hai, "sahih" ka matlab set karta hai, aur priority deta hai ke kaun se Workers pehle bane: woh insaan jo what aur why ka jawab deta hai Builder ke how se pehle. Jahan Strategist track client-facing discovery aur ROI ka maalik hai, Outcome Architect internal Worker roadmap aur uske specs ka maalik hai. Yeh kitaab ise seedha sikhata hai: spec-driven development asal mein discipline hai intent likhne ka jis par Worker ko pakda ja sake.

Software ke zyada tar itihaas mein, dheemi cheez banana tha. Coding agents ne woh toda. Ab ek engineer pehle se zyada ship karta hai, kyunke agent banana kar raha hai. Lekin us tezi nayi dheemi cheez le aayi. Agar ek engineer paanch cheezein ek saath bana sakta hai, toh kisi ko abhi bhi decide karna hai ke paanchon mein se kaun si banne ke layak hain: aur itna saaf likhna hai ke har ek kya kare taake agent use execute kar sake. Woh decide karna woh kaam hai jo yeh role intent kehta hai, aur woh tez nahi hua.

Ek number mein shift dekho. Companies pehle lagbhag ek product manager, ek direction set karne wala, har aath engineers ke liye rakhti thin. Jab har engineer ka output badha, toh wohi ek insaan ab effectively bees ka kaam khilata hai.9 Banana scale ho gaya. Decide karna nahi. Toh decide karna bottleneck ban gaya: woh point jahan sab baaki intezaar karte hain.

Bazaar yeh dekhta hai aur faisla karta hai ke product managers ki kami hai. Yeh kitaab padhta hai: yeh woh moment hai jab Outcome Architect, jo intent ka maalik hai, company mein sabse ahem kursi ban jaata hai. AI workforce jitna bada hota hai, utna zyada us insaan par depend karta hai jo saaf keh sake ke kya banana hai.

![2024 se 2026 tak do lines: execution output per engineer tezi se upar jaata hai jabki intent capacity per decision-maker lagbhag flat rehti hai, aur beech ka barhta terracotta gap bottleneck hai.](/img/intent-bottleneck.png) Execution agentic coding ke saath scale hua. Decide karna kya banana hai nahi hua. Barhta gap, engineers ki kami nahi, yeh naya ratio measure karta hai.

Ise poora sikhata hai: woh discipline jis par poora method khada hai.

**Digital FTE Builder:** unit product, end to end bana hua. Bazaar ise AI Engineer kehta hai: jo AI components se applications banata hai aur AI coding agents chalata hai. Yeh kitaab ka naam zyada saaf hai, kyunke jo aap banate ho woh zyada saaf hai: Digital FTE, jis se poori company banti hai. Yeh kitaab ka primary graduate hai. Yeh poora spine sikhata hai: spec-driven development, SKILL.md authoring, agent architecture, tool aur MCP interfaces (MCP woh standard hai jisse agent bahar ke tools aur data se judta hai), evaluation, aur human oversight, deployment kaafi ship karne ke liye, aur gehrai Cloud AI Engineer ke liye chhod di. Poora end to end sikhata hai.

**AI-Native Company Architect:** company design karta hai, single Worker nahi. Poora enterprise: Two-Layer Model, management layer, workforce, nervous system jo events beech mein carry karta hai, aur system of record jis par sab chalta hai. Agent Factory woh process hai jo yeh architect practice karta hai. AI-Native Company woh product hai jo woh ship karta hai. Yeh kitaab uska canonical source hai. Paanch-quarter Certified Agentic AI Architect program uska credential hai. Poora train hua, aur Architect track se certified.

**Cloud AI Engineer:** jo AI Worker aur AI-Native company production mein chalata hai. Digital FTE banana adha kaam hai. Use reliable chalana doosra adha, aur poori AI-Native company chalana bhi. Jahan AI-Native Company Architect enterprise design karta hai, yeh role use operate karta hai: Workers, management layer, aur nervous system ko asli cloud infrastructure par deploy aur scale karna, Azure Container Apps se ship karna, Inngest se durable execution ke liye, Dapr aur Kubernetes se scale karne ke liye. Yahan system prototype se nikal kar woh company ban jaati hai jis par organization bharosa kar sakta hai. Production path sikhata hai. Gehri scale aur platform operations cloud track ke liye hain.

## Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE): woh vendor-neutral version jo bazaar ko nahi mil raha

Upar ke chaar core roles apni company ke andar line chalate hain. Wohi line client mein le ke jaayein, ek embedded engineer, end to end, toh usay bazaar ab hire karne ke liye beqarar hai.

![Forward Deployed Engineer kya karta hai: ek engineer client ki building mein embedded, uske kaam ka arc gird: client ke andar baithna, asli needs samajhna, AI systems design aur adapt karna, production mein ship karna, value aane tak rehna. Cards hain: kahan kaam karte ho, kya karte ho, market (postings ek saal mein 729% barhi, median pay $190K ke qareeb), skills, aur FDE se in-house architect tak career path. Footer: 95% AI pilots koi measurable return nahi dikhate, vendor-neutral woh version hai jo yeh kitaab sikhata hai.](/img/fde-infographic.png) FDE waqai kya karta hai. Zyada tar software engineers headquarters par product banate hain aur kabhi customer se nahi milte jo use karta hai. FDE ulta karta hai. Woh customer ki asli kaam ki jagah jaata hai, kaam karne walon ke saath baithta hai, un ke asli problems samajhta hai, aur wahan par solutions banata hai, on-site, apni company ki platform istemal karke. Demo nahi. Slide deck nahi. Working software jo customer ke asli environment mein chalta hai.

Sochiye jaise shehar se doosre shehar se aapki report padhne wale doctor aur kamre mein baith ke aapka muayena karke turant ilaj shuru karne wale doctor mein fark hai. FDE doosra doctor hai.

Palantir, ek badi data analytics company jo sarkaron aur bade enterprises ke liye software banati hai, ne yeh role 2010 mein banaya, pehle inhein "Deltas" kehta tha.1 2016 tak, Palantir ke paas actually regular software engineers se zyada FDEs thin, kyunke unke customers (sarkari idare aur bade riwayati enterprises) ko chahiye tha koi on-site jo internal bureaucracy ko startup mentality se kaat sake. Palantir ka fark samjhane ka tareeka saaf hai: regular developer ek capability par focus karta hai, bahut saare customers (ek feature banata hai, sab ko bhejta hai), jabki FDE ek customer par focus karta hai, bahut saari capabilities (ek client ke saath embedded ho, jo bhi chahiye solve kare). Un kaam ka scope jo woh describe karte hain: startup CTO jaisa dikhta hai. Aap high-stakes projects mein shuru se end tak sab ka maalik ho. Woh lineage ab bahar se confirm ho chuki hai: 2026 mein apna 6,000-logon ka FDE unit launch karke, Microsoft ne Palantir ko title popularize karne ka credit publicly diya.11

### FDE 101: Palantir ko yeh role kyun chahiye tha

**Kaun describe kar raha hai, aur kyun count karta hai.** Yeh role kyun maujood hai uska sabse saaf bayan Kevin Bai se aata hai, jis ka talk ka naam tha Forward Deployed Engineering 101, mid-2026 mein AI Engineer World's Fair par diya gaya, usi nine-session FDE track par jo baad mein Brunet ka talk leke chala.24 Unka career yeh role ka chhota itihaas hai ek insaan ke zariye. Unho ne Palantir mein FDE engagements lead ki, company ne jo yeh kaam invent kiya, unke liye duniya ke sabse ahem idaron ke liye. Phir unho ne kahin jaakar yeh function wahan banaya jahan woh nahi tha, Rippling join kiya apni FDE team ke pehle insaan ke taur par aur us team ko ek saal mein lagbhag pachhees engineers tak badhaya. Toh unho ne andar se woh kiya jo yeh page bahar se describe karta hai: ek company mein jahan koi aisi practice nahi thi, FDE practice zero se shuru ki aur use staff kiya. Woh ab Anthropic ki Applied AI team par member of technical staff hain, jo woh team hai jis ke peeche title hai jo yeh page batata hai Anthropic kis ke liye istemal karta hai, Applied AI Engineer. Aur unka apna background ka bayan Venn diagram jaisa lagta hai neeche ke diagram se pehle bhi: diplomacy, sales, business development, product management, customer success, aur software engineering, jismein forward deployed engineering woh ek kaam hai jo sab ko jodta hai.

Lekin pehle do imaandaar notes. Unho ne discipline ke baare mein baat ki aur explicitly apna current kaam discuss karne se mana kar diya, isliye yahan kisi bhi frontier lab ke internal practice ka zikr nahi hai. Aur jo neeche aata hai woh practitioner ka framework hai jo stage par yaad kiye gaye figures ke saath hai, audited research nahi. Yeh page ise wohi standing deta hai jo Aggarwal's arithmetic aur Brunet's playbook ko deta hai: kisi ke jo waqai cheez chalata raha hai, uski gawahi, us tarah label ki gayi.

**Masla software kabhi nahi tha.** Palantir waqai kya bechta hai us se shuru karein. Foundry kisi bhi size ke organization ko apna data centralize karne aur ontology banane deta hai: table ek, table do, aur table teen ko proper nouns mein badalna, taake ek company jiske warehouses hain uske paas ek table ho jo warehouses ke baare mein sach hai. Us ke upar customers applications banate hain.

Ab yeh kisi industry leader ko dikhao. Bai ka jawab: aapne mera data organize kar diya, aur yeh mere business ke liye kya karta hai? Yahan tak akela technology bechne ka kaam nahi karta. Aur bura, vendor ki ab success customer ki software use karne ki capability par depend hai, toh customer do baar deta hai: ek baar platform ke liye, aur phir apne logon ko train karne ke liye ke woh kuch bhi bana sakein. Bai ise business karne ka bura tareeka kehta hai, aur unka fix woh jumla hai jo is page pehle role ke naam par hai. Software bechna chhodo, hours bechna chhodo, aur outcome becho. Aise log bhejo jo customer ke business ko samjhein, unhein platform par solution banane do, aur result thahrao. Ek consumer goods executive shelf placement aur sales throughput par khush hai. Data kaise organized hai woh implementation detail hai, aur Bai kehta hai ke woh honi chahiye.

**Kaun se buyers ko yeh chahiye tha, aur kyun.** Foundry ek app-building platform hai, jo us companies ko boring banata hai jinke paas pehle se achhe engineers hain: Google, Meta, labs, jo sab kuch bana sakte hain jo organization ko chahiye. Buyer ko yeh chahiye tha Fortune 500 mein oil and gas mein, jahan, Bai ke alfaaz mein, pipelines data pipelines nahi hain. Us buyer ko offer tha engineers on loan: log jo client ko hire, recruit, manage, ya retain nahi karna, platform par train, aur kaam ke qareeb baith ke asli problem dhoond ke us ke liye banayein.

**Saboot contract size mein hai.** Fortune 500 ko serve karne wali public SaaS companies ko average contract value se measure karein, matlab ek customer us vendor ke saath kitna kharch karta hai, toh Bai Palantir ko pehle rakhta hai lagbhag $4 million, ServiceNow ko agla lagbhag $1.2 million, Workday ko lagbhag $600,000, aur koi doosri public SaaS company $500,000 se oopar nahi.24 Woh figures padho un headcount ke saath jo woh usi saans mein batata hai, kuch hazar logon, aur is page neeche market capitalization ke figure ke saath. Outcomes bechna seats bechna se alag price karta hai, aur average contract value woh number hai jo yeh kehta hai. Yeh kitaab ka Digital FTEs ke baare mein claim bhi hai, vendor ki table ki taraf se aa raha hai.

#### Sab se pehle ka test: kya aapko FDE chahiye bhi?

Bai ka screen do by two hai: aap jo bech rahe hain woh kitna technical hai, aur khareedne wala kitna technical hai. Char mein se teen cells ko koi forward deployment ki zaroorat nahi.

-   **Technical product, technical buyer.** GitHub, Datadog. Software complicated hai, lekin buyer CTO ya CIO hai aur user software engineer hai, aur us complexity ko absorb karna unka kaam hai. Unhein documentation aur developer relations se serve karein.
-   **Configurable product, technical buyer.** Yahan bhi kuch khaas nahi chahiye. Technical buyer simple product configure karta hai bina kisi ke udane ke. Self-serve, ya sales-led motion.
-   **Configurable product, non-technical buyer.** Rippling, Jira, Slack. Yeh complex ho sakte hain, lekin configure kiye jaate hain, develop nahi. Riwayati sales-led motion kaam karta hai.
-   **Technical product, non-technical buyer.** Sirf yeh cell jahan FDE zaroori hai, aur Palantir ki market ki kona.

Us diagram par uski discipline diagram se zyada ahem hai. Sawaal yeh nahi hai ke mujhe FDE function chahiye, kyunke jo fashionable hai woh chahna aasaan hai. Sawaal yeh hai ke kya aapko kuch technically complicated kisi buyer ke paas le ke jaana hai jo use implement nahi kar sakta. Agar jawab na hai, toh woh saaf kehta hai: forward deployment shayad sahi fit nahi hai, aur developer engagement ya sales-led motion aapko behtar serve karega.

![Bai ka two by two: aap kya bechte hain bane khareedne wale ke khilaf. Horizontal axis technical buyer se non-technical buyer tak jaata hai. Vertical axis configurable product se deeply technical product tak. Teen cells ko forward deployment ki zaroorat nahi. Technical product jo technical buyer ko becha jaaye, GitHub ya Datadog, documentation aur developer relations se serve hota hai. Square ke neeche, gold mein, agentic turn: kyunke lagbhag har platform ab agentic hai aur isliye customizable, vendors us ek cell mein ja rahe hain, isliye 2026 mein role ki demand phat padhi. Aakhri line: sawaal yeh nahi hai ke mujhe FDE function chahiye, sawaal yeh hai ke kya mujhe kuch complicated bechna hai kisi buyer ko jo implement nahi kar sakta.](/img/fde-need-matrix.png) *Bai ka sab se pehle screen: char mein se teen cells ko FDE ki zaroorat nahi, aur agentic turn vendors ko chauthe mein dhakel raha hai. Uska framework, koi measurement nahi.*

**Do matrices, do alag sawal.** Bai ka square vendor ka build-or-not faisla hai: kya is company ko FDE function hona chahiye. Brunet's matrix baad mein is page par per-engagement hai: kya yeh client FDE client hai. Is kitaab ka reader dono istemal karta hai teesre position se, kyunke woh khud koi platform nahi bechta. Bai ka square batata hai kaun se clients ke paas jaana hai, jo kuch technical rakhte hain jo implement nahi kar sakte, jo neeche describe shift ke baad lagbhag sab hain. Brunet's matrix batata hai engagement ko kaise scope karein jab room mein aa jaayein.

Yeh Solutions Architect ya Sales Engineer jaisa nahi hai. Solutions Architect advise karta hai: demos chalata hai, whiteboards par solutions design karta hai, aur sample data se proof-of-concept prototypes banata hai ke prospect sign kare. Jab deal band hoti hai, unka involvement aam tor par kam ho jaata hai. FDE wahan se pick up karta hai jahan Solutions Architect chhodta hai. Woh production code seedha customer ki infrastructure par likhta hai, asli data ke saath, aur tab tak rehta hai jab tak customer ko asli value na mile. Aasan test: agar role customer-specific kaam waqai production mein chalane ka jawab hai, toh yeh FDE ke qareeb hai. Agar product ko prove ya explain karne ka jawab hai, toh yeh solutions architect ke qareeb hai.

Ek asli example hai OpenAI ka kaam John Deere ke saath, lagbhag 190 saal purani farming company, jo wohi deployment logic dikhata hai: AI asli operational context ke andar apply, See & Spray, customer success, dealer workflows, aur preseason recommendations ke gird. John Deere See & Spray ko up to 70% kam chemical use ka credit deta hai, aur OpenAI ki case study dikhata hai AI kaise setup, in-season recommendations, dealer support, aur ROI reporting ke gird use ho raha hai.2 Kaam customer ki planting calendar par hai, product roadmap par nahi, jo FDE kaam ek line mein hai: asli production software, customer ki duniya mein bana hua aur tab ship jab customer ko waqai chahiye.

![Forward Deployed Engineer teen roles ke intersection par baithta hai: Software Engineer (features banata hai, production code likhta hai, end to end ship karta hai), Platform Engineer (core product improve karta hai, data models aur APIs banata hai, deployment handle karta hai), aur Solutions Architect (customer discovery, technical consulting, integration design). FDE teeno ko bridge karta hai: engineering, product, aur customer impact.](/img/forward-deployed-engineer-venn.png) FDE wahan hai jahan code, product, aur customer milte hain: software engineer ka build, platform engineer ki product instincts, aur solutions architect ka customer ka read, ek insaan mein jo customer ki duniya mein banata hai, headquarters par nahi.

Bai usi tasveer ko hiring test mein compress karta hai do conditions ke saath: FDE kuch nahi hai customer-facing software engineer ke ilawa, matlab jisay aap engineering bar par sirf engineering team mein hire karein, aur customer ke saamne bhi bharosa karein.24 Dono aadhe sahi hone chahiye. Pehla chhod dein toh account manager hai jo build nahi kar sakta. Doosra chhod dein toh engineer hai jise un logon se door rakhna hai jinke problems solve ho rahi hain.

Kyun har AI company ab FDEs chahti hai. FDE job postings 2025 ke pehle teen quarters mein 800% se zyada barhi.3 Salesforce ne apni Agentforce platform support karne ke liye dedicated FDE team banayi.4 OpenAI ne "Deployment Company" banaya, ek majority-owned subsidiary jo lagbhag $4 billion investor consortium se back hai, zyada tar enterprises ko FDEs staff karne ke around bana.5

Mid-2026 mein model hyperscalers tak pahuncha: AWS ne $1 billion ek naye Forward Deployed Engineering unit mein commit kiya, paanch-chhe engineers ke pods clients ke paas bhej rahe lagbhag 45-day deployments ke liye, plans ke saath ke hazaaron tak staff karein, pehla major cloud provider jo bet lagata hai, aur apni balance sheet se poora fund, OpenAI ya Anthropic ki tarah private-equity joint venture nahi.10 Do din baad Microsoft ne sabse bade commitment se jawab diya: Microsoft Frontier Co., ek naya operating unit jo $2.5 billion se back hai aur 6,000 logon se staff hai, maujuda Microsoft FDEs, technical consultants, support staff, aur industry experience wale salespeople, seedha clients ke saath embedded, shuru ke customers mein Unilever aur Novo Nordisk.11 Ek hafte mein, do sabse bade cloud providers ne $3.5 billion ek title ke peeche lagaye. Aur model ab bilkul alag sector mein chala gaya: McKinsey ka QuantumBlack seedhe Lead Forward-Deployed Engineers hire kar raha hai, aath saal se zyada hands-on engineering maang raha hai, ek consulting giant jo maanta hai ke deployment ke bina advice ab nahi bikta.12

Microsoft ki apni announcement mein ek detail qaim rakhne ke layak hai. Yeh promise nahi karta ke platform install ho ya models integrate ho. Yeh team ka promise karta hai jo co-design kare, deploy kare, aur AI systems ko behtar banaye rehte, measurable business outcomes se judge ho.11 Yeh customer ki apni success test hai, vendor ki charter mein likhi hui, aur wohi test hai jo vendor ka FDE lead delivery side se baad mein is page par kehta hai.

Yeh sab ke peeche ki wajah saaf hai: 2025 MIT Media Lab study (Project NANDA) ne paya ke lagbhag 95% custom enterprise AI pilots koi measurable return nahi dikhate.6 Is liye nahi ke AI kaam nahi karta, balke company ke messy, asli-world systems mein fit karna intehai mushkil hai. FDEs is gap ko band karne ke liye hain. Woh Palantir ke $136 billion market cap ke peeche hain, jo late 2024 mein Lockheed Martin ko cross kiya,7 aur ab har AI company woh model copy karna chahta hai.

**Wohi study batata hai survivors ne kya kiya.** 95% figure failure batata hai. Usi report mein doosri finding exception batati hai: bahar ke partners ke saath chalne wale initiatives deployment tak lagbhag 67% waqt pahunchi, jabke andar ke liye gaye tools sirf 33%.6 Dono numbers dhyan se padho, kyunke yeh alag cheezein measure karte hain. 95% profit-and-loss impact ke baare hai. 67% deployment tak pahunchne ke baare hai. Dono milke kehte hain ke pilots kamzor models se nahi marte. Yeh integration kaam se marte hain jisko koi embedded nahi karta, aur jinhone bahar se log laaye kaam karne unhone dobara zyada ship kiya.

Us number par do limits. Report kehti hai ke bahar ke partners aur success ke beech ka link sabit nahi karta, apni findings ko preliminary kehti hai, aur har deployment ko sirf chhe mahine dekhti hai.6 Aur dekho kis ki tulna ki: vendor se khareedna, vs akele banana. Vendor-neutral teesra column hai jo study ne kabhi sample nahi kiya, kyunke 2025 mein woh barely maujood tha. Toh finding reader ko is section ke front door tak le jaati hai aur us se aage nahi. Bahari expertise akele ke behtar hai. Kya woh expertise ek vendor ki platform ke saath judna zaroori hai — yeh woh sawal hai jo yeh section neeche leta hai.

**Kyun ab, aur 2012 mein kyun nahi.** Failure rate batata hai ke role kyun pay karta hai. Timing nahi batata. Palantir ka model public, profitable, aur visible das saal se tha, aur lagbhag kisi ne copy nahi kiya. Bai ka hypothesis structural hai, aur yeh sabse achha jawab hai: jo badla woh yeh nahi ke industry ne finally notice kiya ke Palantir sahi tha. Jo badla woh software business khud hai. Lagbhag har platform ab agentic hai. Agentic ka matlab customizable. Aur customizable ka matlab hai ke customer ko nahi pata product kya karta hai ya kitna push ho sakta hai, jo har vendor ko us ke square ke us cell mein rakhta hai jo forward deployment maangta hai: kuch technical, buyer ko becha jaaye jo implement nahi kar sakta.24 Apni product ki success customer ki implement karne ki capability par chhod dein, woh warn karta hai, toh aap upmarket nahi bechoge aur naye industry mein bhi nahi phailoge.

Dono findings saath padho aur yeh bilkul fit hote hain. Bai wajah batata hai: agentic platforms ne har vendor ko Palantir bana diya. MIT asar measure karta hai: pilots jo kahin nahi jaate, kyunke koi embedded nahi tha implementation gap band karne ke liye. Aur is page par paisa, $4 billion, $1 billion, $2.5 billion, woh industry hai jo ise band karne ke liye pay kar rahi hai.

**Bazaar kya pay karta hai: imaandari se padho.** Role ke viral posts "up to $1M a year" se shuru hote hain. Asli numbers inflation ke bhi kaafi hain. Indeed ne April 2025 mein 643 US FDE postings gine aur ek saal baad 5,330, jo 729% rise hai, with typical salary bands $170,000 se $200,000+.12 Anthropic ki khud ki FDE postings $200,000 se $300,000 tak hain.12 Market mein median lagbhag $190,000 hai, lagbhag $160,000 se $220,000 ke darmiyan.13 Frontier labs par senior aur staff FDEs $450,000 se $600,000 kamate hain, aur seven-figure numbers bhi hain, kisi lab ki engineering ladder ke bilkul oopar, lekin yeh career ki chhat hai, darwaza nahi.14 Data mein do details kisi bhi single figure se zyada ahem hain. Pehla, role ki postings 800% se zyada barhi 9 mahine mein jabki candidate pool lagbhag 50% badha: supply gap, jo salary pressure ki wajah hai aur jahan trained reader enter karta hai.3 Doosra, verified FDE roles ke ek review mein, ek bhi mein sales quota nahi tha:14 bazaar FDEs ko engineers ke taur par pay karta hai, salespeople ke nahi, jo upar sales engineer se poori distinction hai, price mein fix. Is section ki market data July 2026 mein last verify hui. Role tezi se badal raha hai, aur jo qaim rehta hai woh discipline hai, koi single salary band ya hiring count nahi.

"FDE" kabhi engineer ya unki skills ka description nahi tha. Woh batata hai woh kahan kaam karte hain: client ki company mein embedded, bahar se aake, poora pipeline end to end le ke. Toh decide karne wala sawal saaf hai: kiski company mein woh build kar rahe hain? Apni company mein toh kaam chaar core roles hai. Client ki company mein toh wohi kaam FDE kehte hain. Ab seedha hire karein. Client ki company unki apni company ban jaati hai. Woh ab forward deployed nahi hain: sirf deployed. Kaam mein kuch nahi badla. Sirf pata badla. Toh FDE title gir jaata hai, aur woh phir se core mein aa jaate hain: ek insaan jo poora pipeline apni company ke andar sambhalta hai. Uska ek naam hai AI-Native Company Architect jo enterprise design karta hai, aam taur par Cloud AI Engineer bhi chalata hai.

Yeh sirf vendor-neutral FDE ke paas hai: woh seedha company mein join kar sakta hai. Client aapke graduate ko full employee hire kar sakta hai, aur engineer agle din bilkul wohi kaam karta rahta hai: kyunke discipline insaan ke saath hai, kisi vendor ki platform ke saath nahi. Woh darwaze se saath mein chali aati hai. Vendor ka FDE yeh nahi kar sakta. Jab woh Palantir ya OpenAI chhodta hai, platform jis par poora kaam tha woh reh jaata hai: engineer jo nikalta hai woh talented hai lekin leverage vendor ke paas reh gaya. Toh vendor ka FDE permanent loan par hai: embedded hone ka kaam karta hai, vendor relationship khatam hote hi gayab. Hamara graduate hamesha ke liye hire hone layak hai. Client use FDE ke taur par rent kar sakta hai, phir in-house apna AI-Native Company Architect bana sakta hai, aur kadam pehle jaisa rahe. Yeh vendor-neutrality kharidta hai: engineer jo company waqai apna bana sake, sirf borrow nahi.

Ek asymmetry safar se survive karta hai, aur woh naam dene layak hai. Do mein se pehla, [Agent Factory System of Record](/docs/ecosystem/system-of-record), uske saath chalta hai aur jahan bhi jaaye wahan maujood hai, kyunke woh ecosystem ka hai aur khula hai. Doosra simple travel nahi karta. Uska domain startup use rakhta hai, aur woh uske expert ki licence aur third-party terms par khada hai, toh direct hire us business ke baare mein baat hai, automatic transfer nahi. Discipline hamesha portable hai. Asset ka maalik hai.

Seedha hire karo toh core mein wapas fold hota hai AI-Native Company Architect ke taur par. Sirf vendor-neutral FDE yeh safar kar sakta hai.

Job landing apna discipline hai. FDE resume software engineer ke resume se alag signals par screened hota hai, aur FDE interview ek round ke liye mashhoor hai jo zyada tar strong engineers fail kar jaate hain. ## Subject Matter Expert as Skill Author

Subject Matter Expert as Skill Author: woh role jo bazaar ne abhi tak naam nahi diya. Accountant, lawyer, ya supply-chain expert jo judgment SKILL.md mein encode karta hai (plain-text file jo ek skill package karti hai jo agent load aur follow kar sake) aur Digital FTE ka knowledge engine ban jaata hai. Kaam concrete hai: woh tacit rule lo jo aap sochne ke bagair lagate ho, kaise ek experienced auditor decide karta hai ke kaun se transactions flag kare, kaise claims adjuster borderline case padhta hai, aur use itna saaf likho ke agent execute kar sake, phir check karo ke agent ke calls aapke match karte hain aur tab tak SKILL.md revise karo jab tak na karein. Zyada tar market lists yeh role miss karti hain kyunke woh abhi bhi AI kaam ko sirf engineering samajhti hain. Yeh kitaab domain judgment khud ko kuch samajhti hai jo author, test, aur deploy kiya ja sakta hai, aur expert ko teeno sikhata hai. Jaise vendor-neutral Forward Deployed Engineer, yeh role hai jo lagbhag koi aur nahi sikhata. Poora sikhata hai: judgment andar, working agent bahar.

Dekho ke yeh do unnamed roles sirf similar nahi hain. Unhein ek doosre ki zaroorat hai. Vendor-neutral FDE ka doosra System of Record bagair author ke nahi ban sakta, kyunke andar ke procedures practitioner ki awaaz mein likhe hain aur practitioner ki asli files se derived hain. Aur Skill Author ko kisi chahiye jo us judgment ke liye governed ghar banaye. Koi junior partner nahi hai. Expert le ke aata hai bees saal aur licence. Engineer le ke aata hai method aur build. Woh pairing unit hai jo [FDE AF Model](/docs/ecosystem/fde-af-model) vertical kehta hai, aur isliye [Choosing Your Vertical](/docs/ecosystem/choosing-your-vertical) bina committed expert ke ek shuru nahi karta.

Bazaar ne ab is role par price print kar diya. Mid-2026 mein, Business Insider ne Yousuf Imran ka profile kiya, ek Google account executive jis ke sales commissions ne $170,000 base ko lagbhag $986,000 saal mein jama kiya. April mein woh chhod ke Mangosteen Studio banane gaya, AI product lab jo sales tools banata hai salespeople ke liye.22 Headline number se aage padho aur dekho woh kya nahi hai: software engineer. Uska bataya asset bees saal seekhna tha ke salespeople ko kya problems aati hain, aur uska bet hai ke woh judgment, jo AI products mein encode ho aur uske apne ho, laakh dolar ki salary se zyada qeemat rakhta hai. Usne faisle ko ownership terms mein frame kiya: agar equity is era ki upside hai, toh equity us company mein honi chahiye jo woh khud banaye. Yeh Skill Author ka wager hai, sabse visible price par jo bazaar ne abhi tak publish kiya: aur, jaise Aggarwal ki arithmetic upar, yeh ek insaan ka bet hai, signal statistic ke bajaye. Headline kehta hai ek banda $986,000 chhod ke gaya. Mechanism kehta hai domain expertise manufacturing input ban gayi, aur expert ne factory rakhi.

## Connector aur Plugin Engineer

Connector aur Plugin Engineer: unhein extend karta hai jo agents already chalate hain. Jab tak aap Worker nahi banate jo apna loop khud rakhta hai, poora discipline hai un cheezon mein jo agent reach karta hai, aur bazaar ise paanch tarahon se naam de raha hai: MCP engineer, integrations engineer, connector developer, plugin developer, agent-tooling engineer. Yeh ek kaam hai do pataon par. Connector-native app end users ke liye chat app (claude.ai) extend karta hai: aap remote MCP server, tools, stored state, asli sign-in, fail-closed session gate ship karte hain, jo ek ajnabi ek pasted URL se add karta hai, aur uske baad model khud aapka customer hai. Plugin builders ke liye coding agent (Claude Code, OpenCode) extend karta hai: skills, subagents, hooks, aur MCP servers ek install ke peeche, jahan deterministic hook woh line hai jo model ke skip karne wale advice aur rule ke beech hai jo har baar chalta hai. Wohi move, do hosts: aur dono ke neeche wohi artifact hai, MCP server, isliye kitaab unhein back-to-back sikhata hai. Through-line thesis se ek idea hai: aap ek unit ship karte hain jo host load karta hai, aur aap extension ke maalik hote ho jabki host loop ka maalik hai. Dono end to end sikhata hai, har ek ko ek deployed artifact tak. Identity issue karna, matlab aapka khud ka sign-in server aur agents ke liye identity, [AI Identity](/docs/ai-identity-crash-course) course hai, aur runtime khud banana scope se bahar rehta hai (dekho kitaab kahan rukti hai).

## Supporting roles

Har pipeline ko log chahiye jo kaam check karein, rules set karein, aur zimmedari lein. Yeh teen roles woh karte hain.

**Evals Engineer:** jo AI Workers ko live hone se pehle crash-test karta hai. Aap bina crash-test kiye gaadi ship nahi karte. Bina clinical trials ke dawa release nahi karte. AI Worker jo real logon aur real paison par faisla karta hai usay wohi discipline chahiye. Evals Engineer woh tests design karta hai: kya Worker sahi jawab deta hai? Kya woh gracefully fail hota hai jab kuch dekhe jo pehle nahi dekha? Kya woh un lines ke andar rehta hai jo use di gayi? Yeh baad mein attach nahi hota. Yeh har chapter mein built-in hai. Core curriculum hai, addon nahi.

**AI Governance Officer:** decide karta hai AI ko kya karne ki ijazat hai. Company mein har employee ki limits hain. Junior accountant $500 tak expenses approve kar sakta hai lekin us se oopar manager ki signature chahiye. Bank teller deposit process kar sakta hai lekin loan approve nahi kar sakta. AI Workers ko wohi structure chahiye. Governance Officer company level par woh rules likhta hai: AI akele kya decide kar sakta hai, kya insaan ke paas approval ke liye jaana chahiye, aur AI ko kabhi kyun nahi chhuna chahiye. Woh regulations ke mapping ka bhi handle karta hai jo company ko follow karni hain, bank mein fair lending rules, hospital mein patient privacy, Europe mein data residency laws. AI-Native Company Architect woh system banata hai jo in rules ko enforce karta hai. Governance Officer decide karta hai rules kya kahein. Yeh kitaab framework discipline seedha sikhata hai. Aapki industry ke specific regulations woh inputs hain jo aap late ho. Framework sikhata hai. Aapke jurisdiction ke rules aapke hain.

**Digital FTE Supervisor:** woh insaan jiska naam line par hai. Jab AI Worker claim process karta hai, contract draft karta hai, ya transaction flag karta hai, toh kisi ka jawab hona chahiye. Woh Supervisor hai. Woh human-in-the-loop hai: reviewer jo kaam check karta hai, manager jo output approve karta hai, naam jo audit trail point karta hai jab kuch galat ho. Yeh woh insaan nahi hai jis ne Worker banaya. Yeh woh hai jo roz chalata hai, jaise shift manager team chalata hai. Ise sikhata hai.

## Kahan kitaab jaan bujh ke rukti hai

**LLMOps Engineer:** model tak, model khud nahi. Agents ko production mein chalana Cloud AI Engineer ka kaam hai, aur kitaab ise sikhata hai. Kitaab fine-tuning bhi hands-on sikhata hai: lekin last resort ke taur par, default nahi. Fine-tune aapke system ko ek model snapshot se band deta hai aur woh optionality cost karta hai jo poora method protect karta hai, toh aap sirf tab takpte ho jab prompting, context, tools, aur retrieval waqai kam na padein. Hard stop hai model khud banana: foundation model ki pre-training scratch se scope se bahar hai, kyunke woh capability commoditize ho rahi hai. Fine-tuning aur model ke gird ka ops sikhata hai, foundation models banana nahi.

**Harness Engineer:** jo runtime aap istemal karte hain, nahi ke jo aap banate hain. Harness woh agent runtime hai, OpenAI Agents SDK, Claude ke managed agents, wagera, jo agent loop chalata hai, state manage karta hai, aur tool calls execute karta hai. Kitaab aapko inhein fluently istemal karna aur unke beech portable rehna sikhata hai, kyunke aapki discipline jo runtime jeete us se zyada rehti hai. Runtime khud banana kaam nahi hai. Operator sikhata hai jo koi bhi runtime istemal kare, nahi ke engineer jo ek banaye.

**AI Data Engineer:** agent-facing data layer. System-of-record kaam agent-facing data layer chhuta hai: Postgres, pgvector, aur MCP spine ke taur par jo agent padhta hai. Classic pipeline aur warehouse engineering adjacent hai, central nahi. Agent-facing data layer sikhata hai, general data engineering nahi.

## Appendix A: FDE resume, chhe signals

Interview aur screening practices tezi se badalte hain. Yeh appendix aur agla mid-2026 ke sources se verify hua hai.

Recruiters FDE profile ko us tarah nahi padhte jaise software engineer ka padhte hain. Asli screening practice ka analysis chhe signals par converge karta hai, aur profile jo inhein dafan kar de usse technical bar se pehle reject kar diya jaata hai.20 Pehle teen poochte hain kya aapne waqai deliver kiya: shipped production systems (asli deployments, team ke backlog par features nahi), quantifiable impact (sirf "feature X banaya" nahi balke customer ko kya mila, numbers mein), aur direct customer exposure (aap stakeholder ke saath baithe, product manager ke peeche nahi). Doosre teen poochte hain aap kaise deliver karte ho: messy-data kaam (asli client environments kabhi saaf nahi hain), ownership under ambiguity (aap project chalaye jab kisi ne define nahi kiya), aur AI/LLM depth (RAG, agents, evals, woh reason jo role hai).

Signals se teen rewrites follow karte hain. Pehla, har bullet ko activity se outcome mein frame karo: "ETL pipeline banaya" bano "pipeline ship kiya jo client ka month-end close paanch din se do din tak cut kar diya." Doosra, "main" likho, "hum" nahi, FDE screeners "hum" ko "was carried" padhte hain, aur hiring-manager round mein uski jaanch karte hain. Teesra, competitive-programming awards kaato. Data-structure puzzles solve karne ka prize galat interview ki tayyari signal karta hai. Jo uski jagah aata hai woh portfolio hai. Freelance section ne pehle se rule bataya, portfolio credential hai: ek deployed Worker, ek shipped plugin, ek live connector app, har ek ke saath ek line ka outcome. Is kitaab ka har capstone bilkul woh line banane ke liye design hai.

Us list mein ek cheez baaki se alag hai, aur vendor-neutral candidate ko usay lead karna chahiye: profession ka ek governed slice, dono readers ke liye published. Deployed Worker prove karta hai ke aap build kar sakte ho. Governed slice prove karta hai ke aapke paas kuch hai jo kisi employer ne nahi diya, aur woh resume ki woh ek line hai jo screener ne lagbhag pakka pehle kabhi nahi dekhi.

## Appendix B: FDE interview, loop aur trap

Loop paanch se aath stages par chalta hai teen se chhe hafte mein: recruiter screen, hiring-manager screen, practical coding round, system-design round, decomposition case study, client simulation, aur behavioral round, kuch AI labs apne APIs par take-home bhi lete hain.20 Do rounds faisla lete hain, aur na toh woh hain jinke liye engineers tayyari karte hain.

Decomposition round filter hai. Aapko ek dhundla, asli enterprise problem di jaati hai: "ek badi city emergency response times kam karna chahti hai; unke paas call data hai, traffic data hai, aur ambulance GPS; aapke paas saath minute hain." Aur sabse zyada common rejection hai us ka jawab dena. Candidate jo "main XGBoost use karke predictive model banaunga" se khulta hai woh pehle se fail ho chuka hai, kyunke usne scope kiye bagair solve kar diya. Jo score karta hai woh sequence hai: asli goal clarify karo, stakeholders aur success metric naam do, batayo kya data hai aur kaun rakhta hai, risk ke hisaab se sequenced subproblems mein decompose karo, aur sabse pehle sabse patla end-to-end skeleton propose karo, assumptions zor se bolo, failure modes bina mange surface karo, thinking lagatar narrate karo. Is kitaab ke readers woh pehchanenge: yeh spec-driven development hai jo verbally perform ho raha hai. Round yeh test nahi kar raha ke aap jawab jaante ho. Yeh test kar raha hai ke aap spec likhte ho kisi ko bhi, insaan ya agent, execute karne se pehle.

Client simulation doosra filter hai. Interviewer customer ka role play karta hai, kabhi pareshan, kabhi non-technical, aur aap bura news dete hain, governance-compromising request ke khilaf push back karte hain, ya explain karte hain ke system 100% accuracy ka promise kyun nahi kar sakta, bina jargon aur bina promise kiye jo nahi kar sakte. Interviewers ke paanch red flags sources mein consistent hain: clarify kiye bagair solve karna, cost aur constraints ko ignore karna, patli deployment stories (production mein API fail hone par kya hua uska koi account nahi), regulated domains mein zero compliance vocabulary, aur koi customer instinct nahi.20

Ek aur format barh raha hai aur alag line ka haq hai: live build. Ek documented loop teen ghante chala: saath minute ek dhundle use case ko requirements mein badla cross-examination ke under, unnabees minute working solution banaya AI coding assistant ke saath har suggestion ko validate karte hue aur live debug karte hue, aur saath minute result ko role-played stakeholder ko pure business language mein present kiya.21 Kisi bhi stage par LeetCode nahi aaya. Beech ka round is kitaab ki Mode 1 discipline hai jo observation ke under perform ho rahi hai: agent ko direct karo, woh jo bhi produce kare use verify karo, loop narrate karo.

Company flavor margin par matter karta hai: Palantir data engineering, ontology thinking, aur decomposition round par depend karta hai jise usne invent kiya. OpenAI systems banane aur unhein APIs ke khilaf evaluate karne par depend karta hai, "aap kaise jaante ho ke yeh actually kaam kar raha hai?" differentiator question ke taur par. Anthropic (jo role Applied AI Engineer kehta hai) production LLM systems, evals, aur mission alignment par depend karta hai.20 Lekin upar ke fundamentals wohi hain jo har jagah hain, aur tayyari chhe hafte ka discipline hai: fundamentals aur pehle stories, phir system design, phir timed decomposition practice partner ke saath, record karke, kyunke zyada tar candidates shock ho jaate hain ke woh kitni tezi se solutions par jump karte hain, aur company-specific tuning sab se aakhir mein.

## Appendix C: Is kitaab se FDE ki tayyari

Interview is kitaab ke around design nahi hua tha, lekin jaise hua ho. Har round us material se map hota hai jo aapko pehle se assign hua hai:

| Interview round | Kya test karta hai | Kahan yeh kitaab sikhata hai |
|----------------|-------------------|------------------------------|
| Decomposition case study | Solve karne se pehle scope karna, spec discipline zor se | [Spec-Driven Development](/docs/spec-driven-development-crash-course), How to Think in the AI Era |
| Practical coding | Agent ke saath asli engineering, verified | Python in the AI Era, Code You Never Write, Seven Principles of Problem Solving |
| AI-specific depth | RAG, agents, evals, "kaise jaante ho ke kaam kar raha hai?" | [Give Your AI Searchable Context](/docs/postgres-ai-crash-course), Build AI Agents, [Eval-Driven Development](/docs/eval-driven-development-crash-course) |
| System design | Constraints ke under enterprise deployment | Thesis invariants, Choosing Agentic Architectures, Deploy the Agent Harness |
| Client simulation | Trust, pushback, business language | Human-Agent Teams, Strategist track |
| Live build | Observation ke under agent ko direct karna | Claude Code aur OpenCode, Agentic Engineering Fundamentals |
| Portfolio behind it all | Shipped, demonstrable outcomes | Har capstone: deployed Worker, plugin, connector-native app, governed slice |

Table ko neeche se upar padho aur kuch notice karne layak kehta hai: interview ke sabse mushkil rounds, decomposition, live build, eval question, curriculum se extra material nahi hain jo jode gaye hain. Woh curriculum hain, examine. Candidate jo waqai spec discipline ke under Worker manufacture kar chuka hai decomposition round mein jaata hai jise usne dozens baar rehearse kiya hai, kyunke intent likhna jis par Worker ko pakda ja sake aur dhundle enterprise problem ko zor se scope karna wohi skill hai do alag volumes par.
