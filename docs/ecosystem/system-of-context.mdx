# System of Context: Records ko Real Kaam se Jodna

**Agent Factory System of Record, customer ke har Vertical System of Record, aur customer ke apne applications ko aik sath jod — taake insaan aur agents ko sahi waqt pe sahi context mile.**

**Tum ne Vertical System of Record ko first principles se design kiya hai, aur Agent Factory SoR Framework pe uska pehla governed slice build kiya hai. Nateeja ek governed source hai jise do log padhte hain: insaan browser mein padhte hain, aur Workers MCP over cite karte hain. Lekin professional kaam kabhi sirf us source ke andar nahi hota. Woh customer ki company ke andar hota hai. Woh company tumhare record mein kabhi na aane wali maloomat se bhari hai: sign kiya gaya contract, aaj subah ki delivery, running balance, pichle saal ka working paper, aur woh message jismein kisi manager ne koi decision explain kiya jo kisi ne likha nahi. Tumhara Worker kaam khatam karne ke liye us sab ki zaroorat hai. Usse kabhi yeh ghalat nahi samajhna chahiye ke woh sab qanoon hai. Agla architectural masla yeh nahi hai ke ek aur source of truth kaise banaye. Yeh hai ke sahi truth aur sahi supporting context ek task ke liye kaise laye jayen, baghair unhein aik ungoverned dher mein milaye.**

**Sukhun mein**

System of Record decide karta hai kya sach hai. System of Context decide karta hai kya pahunchta hai.

Tumhare records Worker ko batate hain ke profession ko kya chahiye. Customer ke applications Worker ko batate hain ke abhi kya ho raha hai. System of Context dono ko jodta hai, current task ke liye zaroori cheezein dhundhta hai, check karta hai ke kaun dekh sakta hai, har source ko label rakhta hai, aur cite kiya gaya bundle de deta hai jis pe soch sakti hai. Yeh apna koi sach nahi jodta. Yeh kabhi naya source of truth nahi ban-ta. Iska poora kaam delivery hai, aur imandari se delivery karna us se mushkil hai jo lagta hai.

## Yeh kyun zaroori hai: Vertical System of Record zaroori hai lekin kaafi nahi

**Sukhun mein**

Tumhara record profession jaanta hai. Yeh is customer ke current contract, aaj subah ki delivery, taaza approval, ya us message ko nahi jaanta jo kisi exception ko explain karta hai. Worker ko dono chahiye.

Ab tumhare paas do Systems of Record hain, aur [ownership argument](/docs/what-you-carry-in) ne dono ka naam liya.

[Agent Factory System of Record](/docs/ecosystem/system-of-record) shared method ka maalik hai: architecture, FDE AF Model, manufacturing discipline, aur woh standards jin pe har Worker ko rakha jaata hai. Yeh har customer pe identical hai.

Tumhara [Vertical System of Record](/docs/ecosystem/designing-the-vertical-sor) profession ka maalik hai: corpus, map, reflexes, invariants, source hierarchy, permissions, checkers, aur evaluations. Yeh batata hai ke kis contract type pe kaun sa rule lagta hai, candidate ko aage barhne ke liye kya evidence chahiye, kaun se clauses hamesha partner ke paas jaate hain, aur kaun sa approval kabhi delegate nahi ho sakta. Yeh sirf tumhara hai.

Dono customer ki operating situation nahi jaante, aur dono ja nahi sakte.

Revenue-recognition Worker ko tumhare record se professional method chahiye. Usse sign kiya gaya contract, har modification, delivery record, current billings, project progress, customer acceptance, aur is company ke approval limits bhi chahiye. Recruiting Worker lawful screening method bilkul jaanta ho sakta hai lekin phir bhi open role, approved description, candidate ki application, aur hiring manager ki authority chahiye. Ledger Worker standard jaanta ho sakta hai lekin phir bhi yeh jaanna chahiye ke is firm ne 2023 mein lease ko kyun waisa treat kiya jaisa kiya, jab decide karne wala chala gaya.

Toh Worker ko ek sath do alag cheezein jaanni hote hain:

> **Profession ko kya chahiye?**

aur:

> **Is case mein abhi kya sach hai?**

Systems of Record pehle sawal ke jawab rakhte hain, aur doosre ke authoritative hisse. System of Context dono ka jawab dene ke liye zaroori cheezein assemble karta hai.

Yeh layer chhod do toh sabak mushkil se milega. Tumhara Worker standard bilkul sahi cite karega aur contract dhoond sakega nahi. Yeh ek brilliant graduate hai apne pehle din pe, textbook haath mein, ek building mein jismein kisi ne filing cabinet dikhayi nahi.

### QuickBooks data rakhta hai. Profession nahi rakhta.

Sabse familiar example accounting mein lo, kyunki oopar ka abstract table tab sahi lagta hai jab real product ka naam lo.

QuickBooks ek traditional accounting System of Record hai. Yeh chart of records, har transaction, balances, invoices, reconciliation state, aur yeh record rakhta hai ke kisne kab kya post kiya. Pucho account 1200 mein aaj subah kya hai toh bilkul sahi jawab deta hai, aur sahi hai, aur building mein us sawal ka koi behtar jawab nahi deta. Yeh real controls bhi enforce karta hai: double-entry, period locking, user roles, aur audit trail. Yeh filing cabinet nahi hai.

Ab pucho ke Henderson lease ko current standard ke tehat finance lease classify karna chahiye tha ya nahi.

Iske paas koi jawab nahi hai. Yeh is liye nahi ke yeh kamzor product hai, balki is liye ke yeh uska kaam kabhi tha nahi. Classification rule standard mein hai. Firm ki threshold policy document mein hai. Is particular lease ke baare mein judgment partner ke dimagh mein hai, ya kisi memo mein jo kisi ne version nahi kiya. QuickBooks us judgment ka natija wafadari se record karta hai. Yeh judgment nahi rakhta, aur check nahi kar sakta.

**QuickBooks jo record hua hai us pe authoritative hai. Tumhara record authoritative hai ke usay waisa record karna chahiye tha ya nahi.** Dono ki zaroorat hai ek working paper khatam karne ke liye, aur unhein jodne se na kisi ka authority badalta hai. Yeh gap, aur koi accounting software ki kami nahi, tumhare Vertical System of Record ke hone ki wajah hai.

Yeh sentence har vertical pe kaam karta hai. Salesforce deals rakhta hai aur qualification method nahi. Workday employment records rakhta hai aur lawful screening standard nahi. Epic patient chart rakhta hai aur clinical guideline nahi. Har ek apne domain pe complete hai aur us profession ke baare mein chup hai jis se ghera hua hai, aur har ek purposely waisa banaya gaya.

### Ek lafz ka khayal — kaise kehte hain

Yeh farq tumhein vendor material mein milega. Yeh aksar ek lafz ke sath aata hai jo seller ki taraf jhuka hota hai: traditional record sirf data *store* karta hai, jabke context layer usay *interpret* karta hai.

Neeche ka point sahi hai. Lekin yeh phrasing tumhari meeting le jayegi.

ERP ya practice system sirf store nahi karta. Yeh transactional integrity, segregation of duties, approval limits, period control, aur audit trail enforce karta hai, aur woh controls hain jiski wajah se business chal sakta hai. Tumhare apne record ke invariants un pe depend karte hain. Finance director ne das saal se us system ko auditors ke saamne defend kiya hai, aur usay storage sun kar usay bilkul pata chal jaata hai ke tum uski duniya ke baare mein kitna samajhte ho.

Toh sahi cheez bolo, kyunki woh zyada persuasive bhi hai. Traditional record apne domain pe authoritative aur complete hai, aur baaki sab pe chup hai. **Gap scope ka hai, serious ka nahi.** Buyer jo yeh sun-ta hai usay ab pata chalta hai ke tum us system ko replace karne nahi aaye jis pe woh bharosa karta hai.

Yeh aadat ke taur pe sochne laya hai, sirf fact ke taur pe nahi. Vendor material vendor ke layer ko woh batata hai jahan intelligence hai, aur neeche ke har layer ko plumbing. Yeh [teesri survival skill](/docs/about#four-skills-to-survive-the-agent-era) ki sabse aam shakal hai. Architecture padho. Adjectives ko kam karo.

## Aik qanoon: authority kabhi nahi hoti

**Sukhun mein**

Delivery driver ke baare mein socho. Driver aapke darwaze pe parcel laata hai. Parcel bhejne wale ka hai, driver ka nahi.

System of Context driver hai. Record bhejne wala hai. Jab Worker batata hai ke jawab kahan se aaya, usay record ka naam dena hota hai. Driver ka naam kabhi nahi dena.

Is page ka har cheez ek jumle pe depend karti hai, aur isay deewar pe likhna chahiye.

**System of Context authority le ke chalta hai. Kabhi rakhta nahi.**

Jab Worker standard cite karta hai, citation tumhare Vertical System of Record ki register row pe point karta hai: publisher, class, jurisdiction, version, stable ID. Yeh context layer pe point nahi karta, aur na hi us copy pe point karta hai jo context layer ke paas hai. Copy finding aid hai. Record source hai.

Yeh sab se zyada zaroori hai jab kuch badalta hai. Standard supersede hota hai, aur tumhara [impact record](/docs/ecosystem/designing-the-vertical-sor#build-one-thin-slice-and-prove-it) har map, reflex, aur evaluation case ko trace karta hai jo uske sath chalna chahiye. ## Model bhi source nahi hai

**Sukhun mein**

Oopar ka qanoon kehta hai authority records mein hai, aur connecting layer mein kabhi nahi. Woh model mein bhi nahi hai. Model tumhein di gayi maloomat pe bohat achi tarah soch sakta hai. Lekin woh jagah nahi hai jahan se maloomat aati hai.

Kisi frontier model se pucho ke current lease classification threshold kya hai toh shayad jawab de, fluently, aur shayad sahi bhi ho. Yehi cheez khatarnak hai. Toh jo missing hai usay sahi tarike se samajhna zaroori hai, kyunki gap accuracy nahi hai. Accuracy ke ird gird sab kuch hai.

**Model ke paas koi source nahi.** Iske paas weights hain, aur weights training corpus ke compression hain, record nahi. Jawab ke peeche koi register row nahi: koi publisher, koi authority class, koi jurisdiction, koi version, koi effective period, koi owner, koi rights basis, aur jawab aane wale text tak koi rasta nahi. Tumhare record ki [source register](/docs/ecosystem/designing-the-vertical-sor#template-4-the-source-register) mein nau columns hain. Model ke jawab mein se koi bhi nahi hai, aur baad mein koi bhi add nahi ho sakta.

**Plausibility provenance nahi hai.** Model sabse zyada likely continuation banata hai. Rule jo sunne mein rule lagta hai aur rule jo rule hai, dono ka shape same hai, toh fluency correctness ke baare mein koi information nahi deta. Tumhare record ne yeh failure pehle doosre domain mein naam diya: assumption jo confirmed fact ke taur pe likha gaya hai woh sales version hai invented citation ka. Model-supplied rule wohi error hai better grammar ke sath.

**Model release ka naam aur date ho sakta hai. Uske andar ke propositions nahi.** Yeh woh argument hai jo settle karta hai. Jab standard badalta hai tum page edit karte ho, aur [impact record](/docs/ecosystem/designing-the-vertical-sor#build-one-thin-slice-and-prove-it) har map, reflex, aur evaluation case ko trace karta hai jo uske sath chalna chahiye. Tum weights ke andar ek statement edit nahi kar sakte. Wahan ka aik rule ke paas koi source lineage nahi, koi authority class nahi, koi jurisdiction nahi, koi effective period nahi, aur koi version nahi jisay tum akele correct kar sako. Model release ka naam dene se yeh theek nahi hota, kyunki release ek label hai millions of propositions ke upar. Source jiske individual claims correct ya date nahi ho sakte woh source nahi hai jis pe profession bharosa kar sake.

**Model permission enforcement point nahi hona chahiye.** Identity uske ird ke system ko di ja sakti hai. Lekin jo kuch bhi model ke context mein rakha jaata hai woh uski reasoning ke liye available hai, jo bhi pooch raha ho. Yehi wajah hai ke [permission filtering context ke model tak pahunchne se pehle hoti hai](#permission-comes-before-the-model), aur baad mein kabhi nahi.

**Aur uski knowledge ka cutoff hai, jabke profession ka nahi.** Rules regulator ke schedule pe badalte hain, laboratory ke nahi.

### Teen jagah model chupke se source ban jaata hai

In mein se koi bhi us waqt faisla nahi lagta. Isi liye in ka naam lena zaroori hai.

**Gap fill.** Retrieval kuch useful nahi deta, aur model phir bhi jawab de deta hai jo usay lagta hai ke usay pata hai. Output form mein grounded se alag nahi dikhta, aur koi error message nahi aata. Ilaaj structural hai: missing evidence packet mein aik field hai, aur reflex jo apna governing source nahi dhoond pata escalate karta hai, aage nahi badhta.

**Drifting paraphrase.** Model sahi passage retrieve karta hai aur usay apne lafzon mein likhta hai, aur dubara likhne mein chupke se koi threshold, condition, ya scope kho jaata hai. Tumhare record ne pehle corpus ke andar paraphrased authority ke baare mein warning di thi, jahan re-explained standard citable ho jaata hai aur phir tumhari muhar ke sath drift karta hai. Yehi drift jawab ke waqt hota hai, aur ilaaj wohi hai: governing text ko quote aur cite karo, dobara express mat karo.

**Unmanaged persistent model memory.** Model jo facts sessions mein carry karta hai, jismein koi owner ya version nahi, woh unversioned aur unauditable store ban jaata hai. Yeh shadow record hai jo product feature se aata hai, design decision se nahi, isliye teeno mein se sab se mushkil pakarna hai.

### Model asal mein kya hai

Yeh sab model ko kam participant nahi banata. Yeh alag banata hai.

Model execution layer hai, aur [10-80-10 rule](/docs/thesis#the-10-80-10-rule-the-operating-rhythm-of-the-ai-workforce) mein yeh darmiyan ke eighty rakhta hai. Isay governing rule, current facts, aur supporting context do. Yeh reflex lagaayega, calculation chalayega, missing evidence notice karega, do sources ke conflict ko dekhega, working paper draft karega, aur apni reasoning explain karega taake reviewer check kar sake.

Yeh bohat zyada professional kaam hai. Iska koi bhi hissa model ko source banne ki zaroorat nahi.

Poora architecture padho toh yeh us ke liye hai. Records pehle ten percent rakhte hain, jo governed intent hai. Model eighty carry karta hai. Named human aakhri ten percent karta hai. **System of Context is liye hai kyunki darmiyan ke eighty ko supply ki zaroorat hai, aur model khud supply nahi kar sakta.**

Aik test kisi bhi borderline case ko settle karta hai, aur woh wohi test hai jo tumhare record working paper pe lagta hai. **Kya reviewer is conclusion ko record se rebuild kar sakta hai, model se baghair?** Agar haan, toh model ne socha. Agar nahi, toh model source tha, aur conclusion defensible nahi hai.

## Authority bohat saari records mein scoped hai

**Sukhun mein**

Koi aik document har sawal jeet-ta nahi.

Tumhara record professional kaam govern karta hai. Sign kiya gaya contract apne terms govern karta hai. ERP govern karta hai ke invoice post hua ya nahi. Aur ek company ek sath bohat saare governed records chalati hai: sales ke liye aik, accounting ke liye aik, law ke liye aik, aur mazeed.

## Layer kya assemble karta hai: context packet

**Sukhun mein**

System of Context har matching paragraph ko prompt mein nahi phenkta. Yeh ek task ke liye aik controlled package banata hai: governing rules, current facts, supporting context, conflicts, missing evidence, permissions, aur citations.

Saat operations woh package banate hain. **Connect** har source se controlled interface ke zariye jo sirf content nahi balki source identity, synchronization state, access rules, aur provenance bhi carry karta hai. **Identify** actor, Worker, customer, case, jurisdiction, effective date, aur intended action, kyunki bina identity aur scope ke retrieval sirf guess hai. **Retrieve** task ko jo chahiye uske sources se, jo bhi method sahi ho: semantic, lexical, structured, relational, ya tool-based. **Filter** har wo cheez jo is reader ko nahi milni chahiye, aur jo customer, jurisdiction, version, effective period, ya case ke bahar hai. **Rank** sirf textual similarity se zyada, kyunki sabse similar sentence aksar sabse useful source nahi hota. **Assemble** packet. **Deliver** karo, aur record karo jo hota hai.

Ranking ko apni aik line chahiye. Production ranking policy authority, applicability, freshness, case identity, source confidence, aur completeness ko retrieval relevance ke sath weigh karti hai. Jo layer sirf similarity pe rank karta hai woh chupke se model ki embedding function ko tumhari firm ki source hierarchy bana deta hai.

Assembly ko bhi aik warning chahiye, usi wajah se. Packet budget ke against banta hai, kyunki model ki context window limited hai aur har token jo stale message pe kharch hota hai woh token hai jo governing rule pe kharch nahi hota. Toh layer select karta hai, compress karta hai, aur kabhi summarize karta hai. Yeh legitimate kaam hai, aur yeh wohi jagah hai jahan [invariant paanch](#the-eight-invariants-of-the-connecting-layer) sab se zyada tootta hai. Compression jo version stamp chhod de, do sources ko aik jumle mein merge kar de, ya authority class utaar de usne tokens nahi bachaye. Usne evidence ko text bana diya. Prose compress karo. Provenance kabhi compress mat karo.

Packet khud output hai, aur yeh ek useful packet mein kya hota hai:

Hissa

Kya rakhta hai

Request

Task, actor, customer, case, jurisdiction, date, aur intended action

Governing authority

Woh exact rules, standards, policies, ya reflex steps jo lagte hain

Current facts

Un systems se live ya synchronized facts jo unhein rakhte hain

Supporting context

Messages, notes, drafts, prior cases, aur mazeed useful background

Conflicts

Sources jo disagree karte hain, authority, scope, aur dates preserved

Missing evidence

Jo reflex chahiye aur layer nahi dhoond saka

Permission envelope

Kya padha, recommend, prepare, ya perform ho sakta hai

Citations

Har material claim ke liye stable source references

### Packet temporary hai, aur yeh design hai

Context packet ek request, ek actor, ek case, aur ek waqt ke liye hota hai.

Yeh boundary technical detail nahi hai. Packet current balance, contract clause, policy passage, manager ki note, aur pending approval rakh sakta hai. Dus minute baad un mein se koi fact badal chuka hota hai. Aik doosre colleague ko wohi set dekhne ki permission nahi. Aik doosre task ko doosri authority route chahiye.

Toh layer company ke liye aik giant permanent prompt nahi banata. Jab task chalta hai relevant packet rebuild karta hai. Teen rules follow karte hain, aur yeh packet ka poora constitution hai:

1.  **Packet authority inherit karta hai.** Kabhi create nahi karta.
2.  **Packet permissions inherit karta hai.** Kabhi widen nahi karta.
3.  **Packet expire hota hai.** Current facts tab refresh hote hain jab freshness jawab badal sakta hai.

Jo durable hain woh source aur evidence hai jo task ke baad write back hota hai. Packet sirf working view hai.

## Kya index ho sakta hai, aur kya poochna zaroori hai

**Sukhun mein**

Teen tarah ki maloomat Worker tak pahunchti hai, aur har ek aik alag route se aati hai.

Customer ke emails, chat, aur files ko search index mein rakha ja sakta hai. Tumhare governed pages bhi index ho sakte hain, lekin record ko confirm karna chahiye pehle ke Worker un pe rely kare. Live numbers har baar poochne hote hain, unse jo numbers rakhta hai.

Do ghalatein aasan aur mehengi hain. Live numbers ko index mein copy karna, jahan woh chupke se purane ho jaate hain. Aur customer ke emails ko qanoon jaisa treat karna.

Lagbhag har real failure is layer mein in dono ghalaton mein se aik hai. Toh pehle poora rule, phir reasoning.

Maloomat

Kaise pahunchta hai

Ordinary company working context

Permission-aware indexing

Record se stable governed knowledge

Discovery index, record se confirm karne ke baad rely karo

Current records, applicable versions, aur koi bhi action

MCP ya API over live query

> **Working context index karo. Governed knowledge discover karo. Current truth live poocho.**

### Working context index hota hai

Unki shared drive, unki chat, unki email, unke purane working papers. Usey freely search karo, permissions inherited. Iske liye layer hai, aur uska koi bhi authority nahi hai.

### Governed knowledge discover hota hai, phir confirm

Governed pages search index mein synchronize ho sakte hain, aur karna useful hai. Student jo accounting rule dhoond hi nahi pata woh us se bhi bad-haal hai jo copy dhoond leta hai. Jo kabhi nahi hona chahiye woh hai **copy pe rely karna**.

Toh layer yahan do alag kaam karta hai, aur unhein mix karna sabse aam tarike hai jismein careful architecture chupke se careless ban jaata hai.

**Discovery puchta hai: relevant maloomat kahan ho sakti hai?** Yeh recall, similarity, speed, aur reach pe optimize karta hai. Iska output pointer hai. Yeh kehta hai *yahan dekho*.

**Confirmation puchta hai: kaun sa source is decision pe officially applicable hai?** Yeh check karta hai domain, authority class, jurisdiction, version, effective date, applicability, approval status, permission, aur canonical identity. Iska output jawab hai. Yeh kehta hai *yeh govern karta hai*.

Sequence fixed hai. Search discover karta hai. Layer route karta hai. Applicable record confirm karta hai. Worker cite karta hai aur sochta hai. Checkers validate karte hain. Governed tools perform karte hain aur record karte hain.

### Current state har baar poocha jaata hai

Balances, transactions, reconciliation status, open items, approval state. In mein se koi bhi authored nahi, koi bhi stable nahi, aur sab exact hain. Index mein daal do toh tumne kuch rough, ageing copy bana liya jiski poori value current aur precise hone mein hai. Worker phir balance question ka jawab deta hai us chunk se jo last Tuesday sahi tha, aur confidently kehta hai.

Aik jumla har borderline case decide karta hai, aur yeh har engagement ke design record mein hona chahiye:

> **Agar stale value conclusion, permission, payment, filing, ya customer action badal sakta hai, toh live fetch karo.**

Aik source ko aksar ek sath do modes chahiye. Contract document index hota hai taake uske clauses mil sakein, aur contract system live query hota hai taake confirm ho sake ke jo version mila hai woh abhi bhi active hai. Format aur length kuch decide nahi karte. Freshness risk sab kuch decide karta hai.

### Aur ungoverned rehta hai

Yahan aik line ka khayal rakhna hai, kyunki customer ki duniya uniformly ungoverned nahi hai. Unke sign kiye gaye contracts, approved policies, posted transactions, delivery records, aur authorizations governed hain, aur woh oopar ke operational records mein hain. Yeh section jo hai woh baaki sab ke baare mein hai: working material.

Woh working corpus tumhara corpus nahi hai, aur tumhara corpus kabhi nahi banega.

Unki ordinary working material sirf is liye governed nahi hai ke customer ne banaya. Kisi ne owner nahi assign kiya. Kisi ne version nahi record kiya. Kisi ne rights basis clear nahi kiya. Kuch galat hai, aur kuch teen saal pehle kisi memo se replace ho gaya jo kisi ne file nahi kiya. Yeh genuinely useful hai. Yeh authority nahi hai.

Toh yeh **supporting context** ke taur pe aata hai, aur rehta hai supporting context. Yeh tumhare Vertical System of Record mein kabhi nahi jaata, kyunki yeh wohi contamination hai jise [promotion law](/docs/ecosystem/fde-af-model#the-one-law-of-the-model-repeated-work-moves-down) rokne ke liye hai. Customer material tab tak nahi hilta jab tak pattern teen ya zyada customers mein repeat na ho, de-identify na ho, promotion review na guzre, aur tumhare expert khud apni awaaz mein rewrite na kare. Yeh authorship hai, indexing nahi.

### Chunking kahan governance ghaib hota hai

Aik mechanism ka naam lena zaroori hai, kyunki yeh invisible hai aur automatically hota hai.

Vertical System of Record ki entry ke sath baarah cheezein hoti hain. Stable ID. Domain. Authority class. Jurisdiction. Version. Effective date. Approval status. Applicability conditions. Owner. Superseded-by link. Required checker. Permission boundary.

Generic indexing pipeline jumle ko preserve karta hai:

> Revenue tab recognize ho sakta hai jab control transfer hota hai.

Words bach jaate hain. Baarah controls ghaib ho jaate hain, aur retrieved text mein unki absence announce nahi hoti. Worker cheh cheezein nahi bata sakta. Kaun sa standard statement pe govern karta hai. Kya yeh is contract type pe lagta hai. Kya yeh current hai. Kya yeh is country pe lagta hai. Kya yeh authority hai ya sirf orientation. Aur kaun se exceptions jawab badal dein.

Yeh woh failure hai jo tumhare record ne corpus mein pehle naam diya tha, jahan paraphrased authority tumhari muhar ke sath drift karta hai. Yahan yeh ingestion pe hota hai authorship pe nahi, jo isay dekhna mushkil aur scale pe karna zyada aasan banata hai.

Ilaaj indexing band karna nahi. Yeh hai ke har search hit ko pointer treat karo, kabhi answer nahi, aur yeh require karo ke har synchronized item apni canonical identity rakhe taake confirmation step ke paas check karne ke liye kuch ho.

Aur ab shortcut jo refuse karna chahiye. Aik single vector database jo chat messages, purane contract draft, sales rule, accounting policy, legal note, aur historical example rakhta hai woh System of Context nahi hai. Yeh aik unsorted pool hai, aur char cheezein ek sath galt hoti hain:

-   chat message standard se upar rank ho sakta hai
-   pichle saal ki policy is saal ki se upar rank ho sakti hai
-   sales rule accounting question ke liye use ho sakta hai
-   ek country ka rule doosre country mein lagu ho sakta hai

Yeh accidentally shadow System of Record ban gaya hai, aur kisi ne kabhi decide nahi kiya ke banaye.

## Permission model se pehle aata hai

**Sukhun mein**

Sab kuch retrieve mat karo aur mana kiya gaya baad mein chhupao. Reader ko nahi dikhna chahiye woh maloomat model context mein kabhi aani nahi chahiye.

Yeh woh invariant hai jise sab se zyada skip kiya jaata hai, kyunki skip karna cheezein kuch hafton ke liye aasan bana deta hai.

Document ke access rules uske home system mein rehte hain. Private channel private hai. Restricted folder restricted hai. Partner ki file partner ki hai. Jab tumhara layer us document ko copy karta hai, toh access rules bhi saath copy karne hote hain, aur har query pe phir se check karne hote hain, us specific person ya Worker ke liye jo pooch raha hai. **Permission inherit hoti hai, invent nahi.**

Teen alag permission questions hain, aur teeno ke teeno alag jawab hain:

1.  Kya yeh human yeh source dekh sakta hai?
2.  Kya yeh Worker yeh source dekh sakta hai, is task ke liye?
3.  Kya yeh Worker woh action perform kar sakta hai jo follow ho sakta hai?

Yeh lagatar badalte hain. Manager salary data padh sakta hai. Recruiting Worker ek narrow field padh sakta hai taake approved check chal sake. Dono permission Worker ko payroll change karne nahi deti. Padh, soch, recommend, prepare, aur execute alag grants hain, bilkul jaisa tumhare record ki [permissions section](/docs/ecosystem/designing-the-vertical-sor#rules-judgment-and-permissions-are-three-different-things) ne pehle establish kiya.

Safe order hai: identity resolve karo, source permissions resolve karo, eligible records filter karo, retrieve aur rank karo, packet assemble karo, aur phir action permission tool boundary pe alag resolve karo. Unsafe order hai: sab kuch retrieve karo, model ko bhejo, aur model se pucho ke jo reader ko nahi dikhna chahiye usay mat batao.

**Model context mein chhupa passage chhupa nahi hai.**

Apna permission model banao kisi ka inherit karne ki jagah, aur tumne leak se bhi bura kuch bana diya. Junior poochta hai aur pehle rank pe, friendly summary ke sath, woh compensation milta hai jisey kabhi padhne ki permission nahi thi. Kisi ne attack nahi kiya. Layer ne sirf ghalat rules pe apna kaam kiya.

### Yeh sirf privacy ka sawal nahi, control ka sawal hai

Zyada tar domains mein permission failure privacy ka masla hai. Regulated profession mein yeh us se zyada door jaata hai. Lekin exact hona chahiye kaise, kyunki loose version ghalat hai, aur controller pakar lega.

**Segregation of duties ability ke combinations ke baare mein hai, visibility ke nahi.** Classic conflicts hain: journal entry banana aur usay approve bhi karna, ya transaction originate karna aur reconcile bhi karna. Audit walkthrough user ko sample karta hai aur puchta hai ke uski entitlements ki combination documented rule tod-ti hai ya nahi. Read access akele aksar aisi combination nahi hota. Claim karo ke tumhara context layer "segregation of duties todta hai" toh room mein argument haar jaoge.

Asal exposure ek layer neeche hai, aur woh segregation of duties se bhi zyada hai.

Access control baaki control environment ka foundation hai. Data integrity assume karta hai ke sirf authorized log data tak pahunch sakte hain, toh jab access hi governed nahi, toh uske upar ka har control us kamzori ko inherit karta hai. Isi liye auditors weak IT general controls ko application controls pe doubt karne ki wajah maante hain: neeche ka environment trusted nahi.

Ab us environment mein context layer daal do. Firm ki periodic access review certify karti hai ke named user ke paas specific set of entitlements hai. Tumhara layer phir us user ko woh content deta hai jo entitlements ne kabhi grant nahi kiya. Kuch chori nahi hua. Kisi documented rule ne formally break nahi kiya. Lekin ab access review ek picture certify kar rahi hai jo sahi nahi hai, aur access management pehle se hi sab se zyada reported control deficiencies mein hai.

Toh argument yeh hona chahiye, aur yeh accurate bhi hai aur zyada strong bhi. **Context layer jo effective access entitlements ke model ke bahar deta hai woh ek control nahi todta. Yeh chupke se woh review invalidate karta hai jo sab ko certify karta hai.**

Worker ke liye jo invariants tumne likhe hain unka koi matlab nahi agar unke neeche ka retrieval ke paas boundaries na ho. Isi liye permission fidelity feature nahi hai jo end mein add hota hai. Yeh decide karti hai ke tum konsa tool use kar sakte ho aur konsa deployment afford kar sakte ho, aur yeh pehle haftay mein pata lagana compliance officer ke saamne pata lagane se kai guna sasta hai.

**Apne expert ke liye aik note.** Oopar framing IT general controls aur access-review language use karti hai, jo zyada tar regulated professions mein travel karti hai. Tumhari firm ko jo exact rule hai, aur tumhari jurisdiction kisi given access pattern ko reportable deficiency maanta hai ya nahi, yeh tumhare domain expert ka sawal hai. Jaldi pucho, kyunki jawab decide karta hai ke tumhara layer kya block karna chahiye sirf log karne ki jagah.

### Governed truth aur supporting context

Tumhare record mein pehle se [do classes ke content](/docs/ecosystem/designing-the-vertical-sor#two-kinds-of-content-authority-and-orientation) hain: authority jo Worker cite karta hai, aur orientation jo Worker padhta hai lekin cite kabhi nahi karta. Context layer ko upar ek level pe, alag axis pe, wohi discipline chahiye.

**Governed truth** System of Record ya kisi doosre authoritative instrument se aata hai. Yeh citable hai. Reviewer check kar sakta hai. **Supporting context** customer ke working material se aata hai. Yeh cite ya attribute ho sakta hai us evidence ke taur pe jo kaha gaya, observe kiya gaya, request kiya gaya, ya pehle kiya gaya. Email sabit kar sakta hai ke client ne kya maanga. Failure dekho, kyunki yeh subtle aur fatal hai. Worker se pucha jaata hai ke koi cost capitalise karna chahiye ya nahi. Sabse zyada matching passage aik internal email hai teen saal purana: *hum hamesha inhein capitalise karte hain.* Fluent, relevant, aur basis ke taur pe precisely galat. Sahi jawab requirement ko governed record ke standard pe route karta hai, email ko sirf past practice ke evidence ke taur pe use karta hai, aur agar disagree karein toh dono ke beech flag karta hai. Accounting error jo baarah mahine se repeat ho raha hai woh error hai, aur wohi baat aik hazaar indexed messages mein ek se hota hai.

## Conflict result hai, retrieval failure nahi

**Sukhun mein**

Jab do sources disagree karein, unhein aik jumle mein mix mat karo jo kisi source ne nahi kaha. Disagreement ko visible rakho, pata karo kaunsa source govern karta hai, aur jab authority settle nahi kar sake escalate karo.

Yeh woh section hai jo System of Context ko aik ache search tool se alag karta hai, kyunki search tool ka disagreement pe koi opinion nahi hota aur professional system ko hota hai.

Contract-revenue case lo. Sign kiya gaya modification optional service add karta hai. Project system dikhata hai kaam pehle se shuru ho chuka hai. Salesperson chat mein likhta hai ke option "sirf extension" hai. Tumhari Vertical SoR kehti hai ke modification ko existing contract ke governing recognition criteria ke against evaluate karna chahiye pehle ke usay combine kiya jaaye.

Chaar cheezein relevant hain. Lekin sab equally authoritative nahi hain. Toh layer charo ko alag rakhta hai. Har ek ko apni authority, scope, version, aur date se label karta hai. Sign kiya gaya modification aur record ko question ke alag alag parts pe governing mark karta hai. Chat message ko supporting context treat karta hai, contract ke taur pe nahi. Dikhata hai ke abhi bhi unresolved kya hai. Aur Worker ko rokta hai jaise disagreement hai hi nahi.

Conflict ke bilkul teen outcomes hain:

-   **Resolved by scope.** Sources alag sawalon ka jawab dete hain, aur dono apne pe sahi hain. Do verticals ki seam everyday case hai: sales ne deal June mein band kiya, accounting abhi revenue recognize nahi kar sakta, aur dono ghalat nahi.
-   **Resolved by authority.** Ek applicable source govern karta hai, aur hierarchy batati hai kaun sa.
-   **Unresolved.** Worker escalate karta hai, conflicting evidence organized hai.

Escalation bar woh hai jo tumhare record ne pehle set kiya tha: yeh insan ka kaam kam karna chahiye. Toh yeh kabhi nahi kehta *sources conflict karte hain.* Yeh batata hai ke kaun se sources conflict karte hain, har ek kya kehta hai, kaun sa authority test lagaya gaya, kya khula hai, aur kaun named person settle kar sakta hai.

> **Trustworthy System of Context disagreement ko ghaib nahi hone deta. Yeh disagreement ko reviewable banata hai.**

## Yeh FDE AF Model mein kahan baithta hai

**Sukhun mein**

System of Context teesra System of Record nahi hai, aur shared kernel mein doosra permanent layer nahi hai. Vertical pattern tayyar karta hai. Customer deployment real applications, identities, aur records jodta hai.

Yeh [FDE AF Model](/docs/ecosystem/fde-af-model) ke do layers pe do alag forms mein failta hai, aur galat karna mehenga hai.

**[Layer 3](/docs/ecosystem/fde-af-model#layer-3-vertical-ecosystems) pattern tayyar karta hai.** Vertical ecosystem reusable pieces rakhta hai jismein koi customer data nahi hota. Profession ke liye source classes aur routing rules. Context packet ka shape. Metadata aur citation formats. Connector templates, retrieval evaluations, conflict tests, tool contracts, aur permission patterns.

Sab kuch profession ke liye hai, kisi aik client ke liye nahi, aur sab tumhara hai.

**[Layer 4](/docs/ecosystem/fde-af-model#layer-4-customer-instances) customer ko baandhta hai.** Instance woh sab supply karta hai jo aik company ke liye hai. Unke applications. Unka identity provider aur user groups. Unke contracts, records, policies, aur thresholds. Unke credentials aur source permissions. Unke case identifiers, approvals, aur audit rules.

Woh jagah hai jahan System of Context real hota hai. Yeh ek company ke data, rules, workflows, aur logon ke liye fit hota hai, aur kahin aur nahi fit hota.

Promotion law abhi bhi unke beech traffic govern karta hai. ## Default mein consolidate karo, deliberately specialize karo

**Sukhun mein**

Pehle kitab mein kaha gaya: apna data ek jagah rakho. Yeh page aisa lagta hai ke opposite advice de raha hai. Aisa nahi hai.

Yeh section explain karta hai kab ek store sahi hai, aur kab do stores sahi hain.

Kitab ka infrastructure pe stance seedha hai, aur yeh page usay tootta lagta hai. Lekin nahi, aur reconciliation batana zaroori hai kyunki careful reader notice karega.

[Thesis](/docs/thesis) kehti hai: default mein consolidate karo. Aik Postgres jo relational data, documents, full-text search, aur AI vectors sab rakhe, rather ke systems mein bikhre jo drift out of sync. [AI Searchable Context](/docs/postgres-ai-crash-course) kaise banate hain, aur tumhara governed record us stance pe exactly chalta hai.

System of Context us rule ka doosra half hai. **Consolidation sahi hai jab aik set of invariants sab data pe govern kare. Specialization sahi hai jab do sets of invariants opposite direction mein khinchen.**

Dekho ke har side ko asal mein kya chahiye. Tumhare record ko exactness, versioning, aur audit trail chahiye, aur har source ke liye rights basis. Customer ki duniya ko breadth, freshness, aur permission fidelity chahiye jo a dozen systems se aati hai jinhein tum control nahi karte. Unhein ek store mein force karo toh tumhein record milta hai jiska provenance ab uncertain hai aur index milta hai jiske permissions ab guesswork hain. Dono worse hote hain.

Yeh stance ka exception nahi hai. Yeh uska sabse saaf example hai, aur jis ke liye word *deliberately* rakha gaya tha. Profession ke liye ek governed store. Customer ki duniya ke liye ek connecting layer. Shared discovery index dono pe madad kar sakta hai, aur woh kabhi source classes ko flatten nahi karna chahiye ya shared authority store nahi banana chahiye. Har governed item apni canonical identity rakhta hai, aur governing record use se pehle confirm karta hai.

Onyx Community Edition connectors, indexing, retrieval, citations, agents, aur actions seekhne ke liye kaafi hai. Yeh akele **nahi** kaafi hai different users ke liye production permission fidelity demonstrate karne ke liye.

Onyx ki khud ki documentation permission-sync connectors list karti hai, jo external systems se user permissions inherit karti hain, sath mein user groups, RBAC, aur group-based permissions, jo Enterprise Edition features hain. Jo deployments ko external systems se permission inheritance chahiye unhein Enterprise pe move karne ki wajah naam di gayi hai.

Yeh zaroori hai kyunki *permission before the model* is page ke aath invariants mein se aik hai. Toh student labs public, synthetic, ya class-authorized sources use karte hain, aur permission architecture ya khareeda jaata hai ya alag implement hota hai pehle ke koi bhi real corpus connect ho. [Building the Context Layer](/docs/context-layer-crash-course) mein students woh gate khud likhte hain aur usay us role se test karte hain jiska sahi jawab kuch nahi hai.

**Feature list proof nahi hai.** Production deployment ko demonstrate karna chahiye ke chosen platform asal mein required identity, permission, synchronization, audit, aur isolation controls enforce karta hai, aur demonstration neeche diya gaya permission test hai. Students public, synthetic, ya class-authorized data ke saath kaam karte hain jab tak pass na ho.

## Kab zaroorat nahi hoti

**Sukhun mein**

Har project ko is layer ki zaroorat nahi. Bahut jaldi build karna mahine barbad kar sakta hai.

Yeh section batata hai kaise pata lagaaye ke tumhare project ko chahiye ya nahi.

Har vertical ko is layer ki zaroorat nahi, aur bahut jaldi build karna quarter khone ka real tariqa hai.

Tumhein is ki zaroorat tab nahi jab governed record aur customer ke operational systems mil kar har sawal ka jawab de dein jo outcome ke liye chahiye. Tax practice jiska kaam code, client ki filings, aur ledger se define hai usay kabhi shared drive search karne ki zaroorat nahi pad sakti. Numbers rakhne wale system ki aik MCP connection System of Context nahi hai. Yeh tool hai, aur tumhare Worker ke paas pehle se tools hain.

Tumhein is ki zaroorat tab hai jab *kyun is tarah kiya gaya* ka jawab kisi bhi system ke bahar ho jo koi govern karta hai. Lambay professional rishte yeh naturally paida karte hain: audit firms jinke paas bees saal ke working papers hain, legal practices jinke paas matter files hain, consultancies jinka reasoning decks aur threads mein hai. Jab institutional memory deliverable ka hissa hai, toh layer apni cost ke qabil hai.

Toh tab tak jab engagement is ke liye bulaaye, record ke baad automatically nahi. Aik gate non-negotiable hai: **System of Context tab tak mat banao jab tak tumhara thin slice khatam na ho aur tumhara pehla Worker usay cite na kare.** Connecting layer jismein governed kuch bhi na ho jodne ke liye woh search box hai jismein ambitions hain.

## Ayesha apna pehla customer jodti hai

Uske paas uska slice hai. Aik outcome, poora covered: working paper, sort record, invariants, uski aunty ka derived reflex, checker, evaluation set. Chicago firm ke partner ne uska working-paper page padha, kaha ke uske juniors har file mein chaar ghantay lagate hain, aur pehla engagement sign kar liya. Ab woh uski firm ke andar hai, aur jo record usne banaya woh suddenly kamre mein sab se chhoti cheez hai.

Firm ke paas SharePoint mein baees saal ke working papers hain. Engagement letters email mein hain. Unusual treatments ke peechay ka reasoning chat mein hai, aur ek senior manager ke dimagh mein jo aksar travel karta hai. Trial balance us system mein hai jo har ghanta badalta hai. Uska Worker standards bilkul jaanta hai aur yeh sab nahi jaanta.

**Woh routing authority pehle karta hai pehle ke kuch bhi jode.** *Standard going-concern evidence ke liye kya require karta hai?* Yeh uska Vertical System of Record hai: governed, cited, versioned, unchanged. *Account 1200 mein abhi closing balance kya hai?* Yeh accounting system hai, typed query se, aur woh rule likhti hai jo baad mein usay bachayega. Yeh number kabhi index nahi hota. Jab colleague suggest karta hai ke trial-balance exports ko index karein taake Worker "bas dhoond le" toh woh mana karti hai, aur ek jumle mein keh sakti hai kyun: embedded balance us number ki photograph hai jo pehle se hil chuka hai. *Henderson lease ko 2023 mein finance lease kyun treat kiya?* Yeh SharePoint hai, aur yeh poora wajah hai ke is firm ko context layer ki zaroorat kyun hai. Memo hai. Iska author chala gaya. Koi governed system usay nahi rakhta, aur bina uske uska Worker defensible current-year paper prepare nahi kar sakta.

**Phir woh permissions test karti hai pehle ke answers test kare.** Woh first-year junior ke taur pe login karti hai aur firm ki compensation policy ke baare mein broad question poochti hai. Sahi result kuch nahi hai, kyunki uss junior ko SharePoint mein woh documents nahi khol sakte. Woh engagement manager ke taur pe test karti hai, aur partner ke taur pe. Jab tak teeno woh na laayein jo har ek ko dekhne ki permission hai, tab tak woh doosra source nahi jodti. Uski aunty ka rule, bees saal ke practice se, woh hai jo woh follow karti hai: control ki value tab pata chalti hai jab usay pehle test karte ho.

**Phir woh engagement ke design record mein ek line likhti hai**, aur yeh woh jumla hai jiske liye yeh poora page hai. *Firm ka apna material is client ke baare mein evidence hai. Profession ke requirements record se aate hain. Worker kabhi dono ko mix nahi karega, aur har jawab jo dikhayega woh batayega ke kaun sa kya hai.*

Chhe hafte baad woh line apni value sabit karti hai, aur yeh answer ki jagah conflict ke taur pe mil-ti hai. Worker lease classification prepare karta hai. Yeh governed record se standard cite karta hai. 2023 ke memo ko alag attach karta hai, labeled as firm ki prior treatment. Aur phir woh karta hai jo search tool kabhi nahi karta: yeh report karta hai ke dono disagree karte hain, batata hai har ek kya kehta hai, batata hai kaunsa authority test lagaya gaya, aur partner ke paas escalate karta hai jo settle kar sakta hai. Woh padhta hai, agree karta hai, aur treatment correct karta hai jo uski firm ne teen saal se repeat kiya hai.

Ordinary similarity search yeh akele nahi banata. Ise design karna aur test karna padta hai. Search tool memo dhundhta hai, relevant dhundhta hai, aur us se jawab deta hai. Dono outcomes ka farq poore page ka farq hai.

## Failure modes

**Sukhun mein**

Log aksar sochte hain ke yeh layer paanch cheezon mein se aik hai. Yeh koi bhi nahi hai.

Yeh document dump nahi, vector database nahi, model ki memory nahi, universal source of truth nahi, aur permissions ke tariqqa nahi. In mein se har ek real project hai jo ghalat hua.

Gyarah tarike hain jismein ghalat hota hai. Inhein pehchanna seekho, aur tum har ek ko itni jaldi dekhoge ke rok sako.

Gyarah tarike hain jismein ghalat hota hai, taake tum har ek early pehchan sako:

1.  **Flat index.** Har source searchable, har result equally authoritative treat. Ilaaj [authority routing](#authority-is-scoped-across-many-records), retrieval se pehle settled.
2.  **Flattened portfolio.** Kai governed records aik pool mein daal diye, toh sales rule accounting question ka jawab deta hai aur pichle saal ki policy is saal ki se upar hai. Ilaaj [federation](#the-portfolio-not-the-pair): pehle profession se route karo, aur kuch bhi merge mat karo.
3.  **Copy pe rely karna.** Synchronized governed page jo authority ke taur pe use ho raha hai, jo bhi version index mein hai. Ilaaj [discovery confirmation nahi](#governed-knowledge-is-discovered-then-confirmed): search hit pointer hai, aur governing record confirm karta hai.
4.  **Stale answer.** Synchronized copy jo us fact ke liye use ho raha hai jo live fetch hona chahiye tha. Ilaaj [indexed-or-live decision](#current-state-is-asked-for-every-time) har field ke liye jo outcome badal sakta hai.
5.  **Pehle retrieve, baad mein chhupao.** Forbidden content model context mein filter se pehle aata hai. Ilaaj [permission model se pehle](#permission-comes-before-the-model), aur action se pehle bhi.
6.  **Model chupke se source ban jaata hai.** Retrieval kuch nahi deta, aur model jo usay lagta hai us se jawab deta hai, form mein grounded se alag nahi. Ilaaj [model source nahi](#the-model-is-not-a-source-either): missing evidence packet mein field hai, aur reflex jo apna governing source nahi dhoond pata escalate karta hai.
7.  **Prompt integration layer ban jaata hai.** Lamba prompt jo model ko batata hai das systems ko kaise interpret kare, jabke connections, identifiers, aur checks kabhi implement nahi hue. Ilaaj connectors, tools, schemas, aur deterministic policy.
8.  **Citations bina provenance ke.** Jawab jo page pe link karta hai lekin authority class, version, scope, ya owner kho deta hai. Ilaaj pura [provenance envelope](#provenance-travels-with-every-item).
9.  **Blended conflict.** Do disagreeing sources jo aik fluent sentence mein summarize ho gaye. Ilaaj [conflict preservation](#conflict-is-a-result-not-a-retrieval-failure), authority testing, aur escalation.
10. **Shadow record.** Context platform transactions ka apna copy rakhta hai, aur chupke se woh jagah ban jaata hai jis pe log source se zyada bharosa karte hain. Ilaaj source-owned live retrieval aur governed write-back.
11. **Product-shaped doctrine.** Architecture jo jo bhi vendor lab mein hai uske taur pe define hoti hai. Ilaaj [open interfaces](#the-open-reference-implementation) aur replaceable components.

## Definition of done

System of Context Worker ko serve ke liye ready hai jab har box checked ho. Customer ki information owner permission boxes khud check karta hai.

-   Thin slice khatam hai aur Worker pehle se cite karta hai, is layer se pehle.
-   Har connected source ke paas register row hai jismein hai ke kya govern karta hai aur kya nahi (Template 1 complete).
-   Outcome ke liye har sawal ke paas routed authority aur path hai (Template 2 complete).
-   Jab customer ek se zyada vertical chalata hai, routing pehle profession resolve karta hai, aur kisi vertical ka methodology apni seam se paar nahi lagta.
-   Boundary contract likha hai aur customer ne padha hai (Template 3 complete).
-   Koi operational state index nahi hai. Har field jo outcome badal sakta hai live fetch hota hai, stated maximum tolerated lag ke sath.
-   Governed knowledge discovery ke liye synchronize ho sakta hai, aur har synchronized item apni canonical identity, domain, authority class, aur version rakhta hai.
-   Koi governed cheez sirf index se rely nahi hoti. Applicable record Worker ke cite karne se pehle confirm karta hai.
-   Jab customer kai verticals chalata hai, packet mein har finding us record ka naam rakhta hai jisse aaya.
-   Har item ke access rules uske sath travel karte hain, aur query time pe re-check hote hain.
-   Permission test set har role pe pass hota hai, kam se kam aik test ke sath jiska sahi jawab kuch nahi hai (Template 4 complete).
-   Packet mein har item pura provenance rakhta hai, aur summarizing utarta nahi.
-   Conflict policy likhi hai, aur conflicts evidence organized hai escalate hote hain.
-   Reflex jo apna governing source nahi dhoond pata escalate karta hai, aage nahi badhta, aur yeh test hai.
-   Reviewer har conclusion ko record se rebuild kar sakta hai, model se baghair.
-   Padh, recommend, prepare, aur execute alag grants hain, tool boundary pe enforce hote hain.
-   Har perform action us system mein record hota hai jiske paas result hai, aur conclusion governed case record mein jaata hai.
-   Source outage ke liye named fallback aur escalation path hai high-risk kaam ke dauran.
-   Is instance se kuch bhi shared vertical mein nahi gaya promotion review ke baghair.
-   Named technical, domain, security, aur business owners deployment ke liye hain.
-   Customer ki information owner ne connected sources aur permission results approve kiye hain.

Evaluation set mein successful searches se kai zyada hona chahiye. Ek relevant lekin non-authoritative message test karo. Ek retired policy jo wording bilkul match karte hai. Galat customer ki document. Galat jurisdiction. Live value jo indexing ke baad badal gayi. Reader bina source permission ke. Worker bina action permission ke. Do applicable sources jo conflict karte hain. Zaroori source jo missing hai. Aur source outage high-risk action ke dauran.

Is list ko do baar padho, jaise aakhri padho. Yeh layer ki definition of done hai. Yeh tumhari **definition of safe to deploy** bhi hai, kyunki oopar har unchecked box ek tariqa hai ke helpful system galat insaan ko sahi document de.

## Appendix: aik cross-domain request, end to end

Is page ka sab kuch aik request pe chalta hai, Ayesha ki firm mein, engagement ke chhe mahine baad. Firm ke paas ab accounting Vertical System of Record hai, uski, aur sales ka kisi aur se khareeda. Yeh aik baar padho toh poora page in aath steps ka description hai.

Partner client se message forward karta hai aur poochta hai: *kya hum is renewal pe twenty percent discount approve kar sakte hain aur revenue June mein book kar sakte hain?*

**Step 1. Identify.** Partner pooch raha hai. Customer client firm hai. Case renewal contract hai. Task recommendation hai, approval nahi. Jurisdiction aur reporting basis customer instance se aate hain. Intended action *prepare aur recommend* hai, *approve* kabhi nahi.

**Step 2. Decompose aur route.** Aik jumla, kai sawal. Kya discount commercial authority ke andar hai? Sales. Kya renewal terms enforceable hai? Legal. Revenue kab recognizable hai? Accounting, jo Ayesha ka apna record hai. Client ne asal mein kya agree kiya? Koi record govern nahi karta, toh supporting context hai. Kya last invoice post ho chuki hai? Traditional record, aur sirf live.

**Step 3. Teen modes mein retrieve.** Sales record ka discount rule aur Ayesha ka recognition reflex discovery index mein milta hai, phir har record ke against confirm hota hai pehle ke kuch cite ho. Client ke messages aur purana renewal file SharePoint se aate hain, permission-checked. Current billing state aur contract ka active version live fetch hota hai, kyunki stale value yahan jawab badal dega.

**Step 4. Filter.** Partner sab dekh sakta hai. Agar first-year junior ne same sawal poocha hota toh pricing history nahi aati, kyunki permission document ke sath travel karti hai.

**Step 5. Assemble.** Aik packet. Governing rules, har ek apna record batata hai. Current facts, time-stamped. Client ke messages, supporting context labeled. Aik missing item: second deliverable ke liye sign kiya gaya acceptance nahi. Permission envelope: sirf prepare aur recommend.

**Step 6. Conflict notice karo.** Sales record is contract size pe twenty percent permit karta hai. Ayesha ka accounting record kehta hai revenue June mein recognizable nahi hai bina acceptance evidence ke. Dono sahi hain. Dono alag sawalon ka jawab dete hain, aur yeh *resolved by scope* hai, ek record ke doosre se upar rank hone se nahi.

**Step 7. Soch aur recommend.** Worker discount approve karne ki, aur June recognition ke khilaf recommend karta hai. Pehle ke liye sales rule cite karta hai aur doosre ke liye standard. Woh aik document name karta hai jo doosra jawab badal dega. Dono ko aik single cheerful yes mein mix nahi karta.

**Step 8. Act aur record.** Partner discount approve karta hai. Sales record validate karta hai ke approval authority ke andar hai. CRM likhta hai aur record karta hai kisne approve kiya. Recognition question khula rehta hai, aur conclusion uske evidence ke sath governed case record mein jaata hai, jismein reviewer usay bina model ke puchhe rebuild kar sakta hai.

Aath steps dobara padho aur notice karo ke kya kabhi nahi hua. Koi record doosre mein merge nahi hua. Koi indexed copy cite nahi hua. Koi live number index se jawab nahi diya gaya. Partner ko jo pehle se nahi dikh raha tha woh nahi aaya. Disagreement smooth nahi hua. Aur layer jo sab mumkin banata hai usne koi professional judgment nahi kiya aur koi authoritative fact apna nahi banaya.

## Architecture aik jumle mein

Poora page aik rule mein compress hota hai:

> **Bohat saari domains. Bohat saare Vertical Systems of Record. Aik System of Context jo unhein baghair flatten kiye jodta hai. Records sach rakhte hain, layer relevance assemble karta hai, Worker sochta hai aur permission ke andar kaam karta hai, aur evidence us record mein laut-ta hai jiske paas result hai.**

Pehle sach banao, Agent Factory record aur apne vertical record ke sath. Doosra context jodo, customer ke systems, identities, permissions, aur provenance ke sath. Task assemble karo, authority, current facts, support, gaps, aur citations ke sath. Worker ko governed tools aur human approval se operate karo. Result wahan record karo jahan sach hai.

## Yahan se kahan jaana hai

Yeh page record aur engagement ke beech hai. [Designing the Vertical SoR](/docs/ecosystem/designing-the-vertical-sor) ne governed truth banaya, aur build course us design ko running software mein badalta hai. Yeh page layer add karta hai jo usay customer ki duniya mein le jaata hai. Hands-on build [Building the Context Layer](/docs/context-layer-crash-course) hai: yeh deploy karta hai Onyx Standard, char source classes jodta hai, tumhare record ko MCP over serve karta hai, aur permission gate prove karta hai. Yeh Mode 2 mein directly [AI Searchable Context](/docs/postgres-ai-crash-course) ke baad hai, jo retrieval layer sikhata hai jis pe tumhara record khud built hai, aur dono milkar scope jump ko visible banate hain: aik ek single Worker ke liye store banata hai, agla poore workforce ke liye corpus banata hai.

Phir fixed order jaata hai. [Skills & Connectors](/docs/skills-connectors-crash-course) MCP sikhata hai, jo agent-facing knowledge aur governed tools ke liye important interface hai, sath mein OpenAPI, connector, aur typed-query paths jo yeh page bhi use karta hai. [Eval-Driven Development](/docs/eval-driven-development-crash-course) proof sikhata hai. Is layer ko apna evaluation set chahiye, conflict cases ke around built: customer ka apna material ek cheez kehta hai, governed record doosri, aur passing answer woh hai jo dono dikhata hai aur batata hai kaunsa govern karta hai. [Building a Digital FTE](/docs/digital-fte-crash-course) Worker banata hai jo sab se padhta hai, aur [Human-Agent Teams](/docs/human-agent-teams-crash-course) us Worker ko logon ke sath rakhta hai jo uske jawabon pe bharosa karenge ya nahi.

Aik aakhri framing, jab yeh layer us record ke barabar plumbing lage jis pe mahine lagaye.

Yeh plumbing hai, aur yeh usi ki point hai. Koi plumbing nahi khareedta, aur har building mein hai. Asset jo tum client se client tak carry karte ho woh governed profession hai, plus Layer 3 pattern jo usay jodta hai. Fitting woh hai jo us asset ko us company ke andar usable banata hai jis ne kabhi suna nahi. Vertical Forward Deployed Engineer jo record ko customer ki real information se nahi jod sakta woh khoobsurat kitab haath mein hai kamre mein jismein koi shelf tak nahi pahunch sakta.

Jo woh bhi honest wajah hai ke yeh page Ecosystem mein last hai. Record tumhein hire kiye jane ke qabil banata hai. Connecting layer tumhein rakhe jane ke qabil banata hai.

## Flashcards — Apni Samajh Check Karo

<Flashcard question="System of Context kya hai?" answer="Woh layer jo sources ko jodta hai aur current task ke liye zaruri cheezein assemble karta hai. Yeh apna koi sach nahi rakhta, sirf delivery karta hai." />

<Flashcard question="System of Record aur System of Context ka kya farq hai?" answer="System of Record sach rakhta hai — facts aur rules. System of Context decide karta hai kya pahunchta hai — woh routing, filtering, aur assembly karta hai." />

<Flashcard question="Authority routing kya hai?" answer="Ye decide karna ke har sawal ka kaunsa source governing hai — pehle retrieval se pehle, baad mein nahi." />

<Flashcard question="Context packet kya hai?" answer="Ek temporary, cited bundle jo ek task ke liye assembled hota hai — governing rules, current facts, supporting context, conflicts, missing evidence, permissions, aur citations." />

<Flashcard question="System of Context ka aik qanoon kya hai?" answer="Authority kabhi nahi hoti. Layer citations le ke chalta hai record ki taraf, aur na layer aur na model kabhi cited source ban-ta hai." />

<Flashcard question="Model kyun source nahi hai?" answer="Model ke paas weights hain, source nahi. Koi register row nahi, koi version nahi, koi authority class nahi. Plausibility provenance nahi hai." />

<Flashcard question="Chunking kyun governance ghaib karta hai?" answer="Generic indexing pipeline sentences preserve karta hai lekin 12 controls (stable ID, version, jurisdiction, etc.) chhod deta hai. Worker phir nahi bata sakta ke kya govern karta hai." />

<Flashcard question="Permission model se pehle kyun aati hai?" answer="Reader ko jo nahi dikhna chahiye woh model context mein kabhi aana nahi chahiye. Model context mein chhupa passage chhupa nahi hai." />

<Flashcard question="Conflict ke kitne outcomes hain?" answer="Teen: Resolved by scope (alag sawalon ka jawab), Resolved by authority (ek source govern karta hai), Unresolved (escalate karo evidence organized hai)." />

<Flashcard question="Packet ke char rules kya hain?" answer="1) Authority inherit karta hai, 2) Permissions inherit karta hai, 3) Expires hota hai, 4) Evidence laut-ta hai record mein." />

<Flashcard question="System of Context kab zaruri nahi hai?" answer="Jab governed record aur customer ke systems mil kar har sawal ka jawab dein. Ek MCP connection System of Context nahi hai — yeh tool hai." />

<Flashcard question="Onyx kya hai?" answer="System of Context ki open reference implementation. Students isay install, read, aur dismantle kar sakte hain." />

<Flashcard question="Chunking ka ilaaj kya hai?" answer="Har search hit ko pointer treat karo, answer nahi. Har synchronized item apni canonical identity rakhe taake confirmation step ke paas check karne ke liye ho." />

<Flashcard question="Packet kyun temporary hai?" answer="Kyunki 10 minute baad facts badal sakte hain, doosre colleague ko different permission hai, aur doosre task ko different authority route chahiye." />

<Flashcard question="FDE AF Model mein System of Context kahan baithta hai?" answer="Layer 3 (vertical ecosystem) pattern tayyar karta hai bina customer data ke. Layer 4 (customer instance) customer ke actual systems jodta hai." />
