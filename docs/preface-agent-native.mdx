# Preface: Sahi Taraf — Line ke Us Paar

Jab tak markets ne react kiya, software ki value mein ek trillion dollar ka nuqsan ho gaya. Is liye nahi ke kuch toot gaya. Is liye ke kuch finally register hua: **workers ki ek nai class aa gayi thi**, aur market ne purani class ki qeemat us hisaab se lagayi.

Yeh kitaab un logon ka naqsha hai jo us repricing ki doosri taraf hain — jo woh workers banayenge, deploy karenge, aur unke maalik honge, unki jagah replace hone ki bajay.

## Kya Hua Tha?

**4 February 2026** ko global software stocks mein [2022 ke baad sabse bura stretch](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/us-software-stocks-hit-anthropic-154249835.html) aaya. Software aur services sector se **$1 trillion** ki market value chhi consecutive sessions mein mita di gayi. Traders ne ise [**"SaaSpocalypse"**](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-04/what-s-behind-the-saaspocalypse-plunge-in-software-stocks) kaha.

**Wajah:** Anthropic ne Claude Cowork ke liye [11 open-source plugins release kiye](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/30/anthropic-brings-agentic-plugins-to-cowork/) — legal, finance, sales, marketing, aur data analysis ke liye. Ek plugin NDAs triage kar sakta tha, compliance track kar sakta tha, aur contracts review kar sakta tha.

Market ka jawab fauran aaya:
- Thomson Reuters: **-16%**
- RELX: **-14%**
- Salesforce aur ServiceNow: **-7% each**
- Sirf ek legal plugin ne **$285 billion** mita diye ek hi trading session mein

![Anthropic Hit List](/img/anthropic-hit-list.png)

Paigham saaf tha: **Autonomous agents ab woh complex professional kaam kar sakte hain jo $200/month software subscriptions ko justify karta tha.** Enterprise software mein "seat" ke liye pay karne ka zamana khatam ho raha hai.

March tak, Time Magazine ne Anthropic ko [**"duniya ki sabse disruptive company"**](https://time.com/article/2026/03/11/anthropic-claude-disruptive-company-pentagon/) kaha.

---

## Phir Aur Bura Hua

Teen hafte baad, 23 February ko, [Citrini Research ka 7,000-alfaz ka hypothetical](https://www.citriniresearch.com/p/2028gic) viral ho gaya, aur [Dow ek session mein 800 points gir gaya](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/stock-market-citrini-research-ai-downturn-f5c1ca20).

Yeh prediction nahi tha — yeh June 2028 ka ek thought experiment tha jo explore karta tha ke jab AI agents white-collar knowledge workers ko scale par displace karein to kya hoga.

- Datadog, CrowdStrike, Zscaler: **-9% each**
- IBM: **-13%** (2000 ke baad sabse bura din)

Citrini ka woh line jo us lamhe ko capture karta hai:

> *"Poori modern economic history mein, human intelligence scarce input raha hai. Ab hum us premium ka unwind dekh rahe hain."*

---

## Phir Aur Bada Hua

Citrini selloff ke saat hafte baad, mid-April 2026 mein, Bloomberg ne report kiya ke venture capitalists **$800 billion** par term sheets de rahe hain — February ke $380B se zyada double.

Comparison jo matter karta hai:

**$800B par, Anthropic ki value India ki 6 sabse badi IT services companies ke combined market cap se 3.2 guna zyada hai** — TCS, Infosys, HCLTech, Wipro, LTIMindtree, aur Tech Mahindra — jo milke **19 lakh log employ karti hain** aur $100 billion revenue generate karti hain. Anthropic ke sirf **1,000 employees** hain.

![Anthropic Market Cap Comparison](/img/anthropic-market-cap.png)

Market yeh nahi keh raha ke Anthropic ek zyada valuable company hai. Woh keh raha hai ke **Anthropic repricing ki sahi taraf hai** — aur woh 19 lakh jobs galat taraf hain.

## Phir Capable Hua

April mein, Anthropic ne **Claude Mythos** release kiya [**Project Glasswing**](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-14/anthropic-attracts-investor-offers-at-a-800-billion-valuation) ke zariye:

- Har major OS aur browser mein **hazaron zero-day vulnerabilities** khud dhundhein
- **27 saal purana OpenBSD bug** aur **17 saal purana FreeBSD flaw** pakda
- Expert-level cybersecurity tasks mein **73% score**
- **32-step simulated corporate network attack** end-to-end solve kiya

Anthropic ne Mythos ko broad release ke liye bahut khatarnak samjha — sirf select security partners ko access diya.

---

## Phir Industrial Ho Gaya

May 2026 mein, Financial Times ne report kiya ke Anthropic **$50 billion raise** weigh kar raha hai **~$900 billion pre-money** par — post-money **$1 trillion** ke qareeb.

Infrastructure commitments:
- Google Cloud ko **$200 billion** (5 saal mein)
- SpaceX Colossus 1 ke **220,000+ NVIDIA chips**
- Amazon ke saath **5GW agreement**
- Microsoft aur Nvidia ke zariye **$30B Azure capacity**

Google ne **$40 billion equity** Anthropic mein commit ki.

**Jo cheez industrial scale par manufacture ho rahi hai woh model nahi hai — woh workforce hai jo us par chalti hai.**

## Peeche Mud Kar Dekhein: Pehle Se Signal Tha

Yeh sab kuch hona koi hairat ki baat nahi thi.

June 2023 mein, Thomson Reuters ne [Casetext ko $650 million mein khareeda](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/26/thomson-reuters-buys-casetext-an-ai-legal-tech-startup-for-650m-in-cash/), jiska CoCounsel AI assistant ne complex legal evaluations mein **97% score** kiya tha.

Yeh acquisition technology ke liye nahi tha — yeh **"encoded legal expertise"** ke liye tha: woh capability jo pehle costly human professionals chahiye hoti thi.

CoCounsel ek proof of concept tha SaaSpocalypse se teen saal pehle — jab yeh pattern poori market par nazar aaya.

**"$800B ki bid manufacturing side ki qeemat hai."**

## Market Kya Price Kar Raha Hai?

Paanch signals saaf hain:

| Event | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| **SaaSpocalypse** | Autonomous agents ab woh professional kaam kar sakte hain jo seat-based software ke liye charge hota tha |
| **Citrini** | Disruption software se aage broader knowledge work tak jayegi |
| **$800B bid** | Market aaj un agents banane wali company ke liye pay karne ko tayyar hai |
| **Mythos** | AI workers ab woh kaam kar sakte hain jo koi human team match nahi kar sakti |
| **Compute buildout** | Workforce national infrastructure ki scale par manufacture ho rahi hai |

**Ek thesis:** Value un logon se shift ho rahi hai jo software use karte hain — un logon ki taraf jo un agents ke maalik hain jo us par chalte hain.

---

## Ab Build Bottleneck Nahi Hai

AI workers banane ka primary interface ab natural language hai: English, Urdu, Spanish, jo bhi zaban mein tum sochte ho. Tum job describe karo; agent solution assemble kare. Domain experts jo bina programming background ke hain, is tareeqe se production AI workers ship kar rahe hain. "Agentic coding" woh discipline hai jo yeh mumkin banata hai; neeche Darwaza 3 is ka sabse tez hands-on entry point hai.

---

## Tumhare Liye Kya Matlab Hai?

Agar tumhari job ya tumhari clients ko jo value tum dete ho woh legacy software navigate karne par depend karti hai — tum displace ho rahe ho.

Wohi shift jo seats ko worthless banati hai — kuch aur ko extraordinarily valuable banati hai: **encoded domain expertise**.

Jo bhi knowledge tum rakhte ho — contracts, audits, sales, supply chains, compliance, patient triage — wahi AI workers ko useful banane ke liye chahiye. Agla decade jeetne wali companies woh expertise human heads se nikal kar agents mein encode karengi.

**Yahi yeh kitaab sikhati hai.**

Un workers ko hum **Digital FTEs** kehte hain: role-based, supervised, spec-driven AI agents jo real organizations mein real kaam karte hain. Chatbots nahi. Demos nahi. **Yeh production ka naya factor hain.**
