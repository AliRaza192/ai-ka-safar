# Sales Catalog: AI Workers Bechne ke Tareeqe

## Yeh Document Kahan Fit Hota Hai

[Agent Factory Thesis](/docs/thesis) architecture define karti hai. [AI Worker Catalog](/docs/worker-catalog) define karta hai ke us architecture ke andar kya banta hai. **Sales Catalog** define karta hai ke AI-native company deals kaise close karti hai.

## Is Document Ko Kaise Padhein

Yeh document aik tool hai, kahani nahi. Mukhtalif parne wale isse mukhtalif tareeqon se istemal karenge.

**Agar tum enterprise sales ya revenue operations mein naye ho.** Shuru karo _Appendix A: Glossary_ se jo end mein hai. Aik baar par lo taake lafzon se aqeedat ho jaaye. Phir Executive Summary ko ahista se paro. Jab motions par pahuncho, to har motion ke shuru mein sirf _Simple Lafzon Mein_ wala paragraph paro — pehli baar mein Mechanism, Example, aur Risk sections skip karo. Jab depth chahiye tab wapas aana.

**Agar tum founder, head of sales, ya revenue leader ho jo apna motion design kar rahe ho.** _Seller Diagnostic_ aur _Strategic Fit Matrix_ istemal karo taake dekh sako kaunse motions tumhari stage aur buyer ke liye fit hain. Sirf woh do teen motions poora paro. Baqi ko tab tak skip karo jab tak zaroorat na ho.

**Agar tum investor ya experienced operator ho.** Document tumhare liye bana hai. Upar se neeche paro. Motions buyer-led se vendor-led aur outcome-led se partner-led ki taraf sequence ki gayi hain — jahan zyada tar early-stage AI companies shuru karti hain aur jahan serious revenue scale hota hai.

**Aik baat jargon ke baare mein.** Yeh document B2B sales, RevOps, aur AI deployment ke business aur technical lafzon ka istemal karta hai. Jab pehli baar koi specialized term aaye, to woh aam taur par qareeb mein ya parentheses mein plain language mein samjhaya gaya hota hai. _Appendix A: Glossary_ kisi bhi term ke liye quick reference hai jo tumhe uljha rahi ho. Har term jaanne ki zaroorat nahi hai document follow karne ke liye.

## Executive Summary

Sales Catalog 2026 aur uske baad AI-native products ke saath deals close karne ki recipe book hai. AI Worker bechne ke bohot tareeqe hain, aur sahi tareeqa tumhari stage, buyer, product complexity, aur distribution ki depth par depend karta hai. Yeh document twelve motions ko naam deta hai, unhe char families mein organize karta hai, aur batata hai kaunsa tumhari situation ke liye fit hai.

**Char families** — har qisam ki motion pehle kis cheez par compete karti hai.

_Buyer-led motions_ (Motions 1-4) tab kaam karti hain jab buyer khud discover karta hai, khud evaluate karta hai, aur khud khareedta hai. Seller ka kaam hai findable, frictionless, aur credible hona. Seller sales cycle nahi chalata; buyer chalata hai.

_Vendor-led motions_ (Motions 5-8) tab kaam karti hain jab seller deal initiate karta hai aur orchestrate karta hai. Seller ka kaam hai precise targeting, value articulation, aur procurement navigation. Seller sales cycle chalata hai.

_Outcome-led motions_ (Motions 9-10) tab kaam karti hain jab deal access ki bajaye results par structured hoti hai. Seller ka kaam hai measurement, attribution, aur consistent delivery. Buyer sirf value created ke liye pay karta hai.

_Partner-led motions_ (Motions 11-12) tab kaam karti hain jab third parties purchase drive karti hain. Seller ka kaam hai alliance management — partners ko itna successful banana ke woh tumhare liye bechte rahein.

**Paanch sales assets** — har motion kya capture karne ki ladai lad rahi hai.

_Pipeline_ qualified opportunities ki reliable, repeatable supply hai jo sales process mein flow hoti hai. Har successful motion ise produce karti hai; zyada tar failed motions nahi karti.

_Velocity_ hai ke opportunity kitni jaldi closed revenue mein convert hoti hai. Tez cycle ka matlab hai same team se har quarter mein zyada deals.

_Deal economics_ hain revenue per deal guna gross margin. Jo motion $1M deals 80% margin par produce karti hai woh alag revenue league mein hai jo $10K deals 30% margin par produce karti hai.

_Retention_ hai net revenue retention — customer apna spending expand karta hai, rakhta hai, ya ghata hai? SaaS mein, 130% se upar NRR category leader ko define karta hai. AI mein, math badal raha hai kyunki outcome-based revenue usage ke saath naturally grow hoti hai.

_Trust_ hai buyer ka tumhari team, product, aur operational discipline par earned confidence. Trust banane mein saal lagte hain aur khone mein minute.

Sab se mazboot motions aik saath teen ya zyada assets capture karti hain. Agent era mein revenue strategy ka kaam hai pehle kaunsa asset capture karna hai choose karna, phir baqi ko sequence karna.

**Scope ki aik baat.** Yeh catalog primarily **B2B markets** par focus karta hai — doosre businesses ko bechne wale AI Workers aur AI-native software, directly individual consumers ko nahi. Consumer-facing AI sales (mobile app store, advertising-led, subscription-led) mukhtalif rules follow karti hain aur yahan primary subject nahi hain, halanke kai motions — Self-Serve PLG, Marketplace-Led, aur Community-Led — dono contexts mein cleanly apply hoti hain.

**Maturity spectrum.** Is catalog mein har motion Proven, Emerging, ya Speculative tag hai, based on is baat par ke kitni AI-native companies isse aaj successfully chal rahi hain.

* _Proven_ motions ke multiple at-scale companies aaj operate kar rahe hain, confirmed revenue aur documented playbook ke saath. Mechanics ache se samjhe gaye hain.
* _Emerging_ motions funded companies dwara 2026 mein chalayi ja rahi hain, lekin zyada tar outcomes abhi pending hain aur canonical winner abhi nahi nikla.
* _Speculative_ motions un buyer behaviors ya contracting structures par depend karti hain jo abhi scale par nahi hain, lekin jaldi ban sakte hain.

Maturity quality ke barabar nahi hai. Proven motions safe hain; Emerging motions bara upside deti hain; Speculative motions un teams ko sab se bara upside deti hain jo baqi market aane se pehle sahi position le lete hain.

## Beginner ke Liye 10 Minute Version

### Sales Motion Kya Hai?

Sales motion woh specific tareeqa hai jisse company apna product bechti hai — kaun baat shuru karta hai, deal close hone mein kitna waqt lagta hai, pricing kya hai, aur kaun actually selling karta hai.

### Char Families

1. **Buyer-led motions (1-4):** Buyer khud product dhundta, evaluate karta, aur khareedta hai — bina salesperson ke
2. **Vendor-led motions (5-8):** Tumhari team contact karta hai aur deal close karta hai
3. **Outcome-led motions (9-10):** Buyer tab pay karta hai jab AI real result deliver kare
4. **Partner-led motions (11-12):** Third parties tumhara product apne clients ko bechte hain

### Motion Chunne ka Aasan Tareeqa

Do sawaal: *Mera product per customer per year kitna costa hai?* aur *Deal close hone mein kitna waqt lagta hai?*

- Kam price + short cycle = buyer-led (PLG ya Marketplace)
- Medium price + medium cycle = founder ya outbound
- Bada price + lamba cycle = enterprise field ya FDE
- Measurable outcomes = pay-per-outcome

## Seller Diagnostic: Aath Sawaalat

Motion chunne se pehle, neeche aath dimensions par khud ko honestly score karo. Har row jis motion ki taraf point karti hai woh us condition ke saath sab se zyada aligned hain. Jo team teen ya char par High score karti hai woh aam taur par jaldi do teen candidate motions par narrow hoti hai.

1. **Founder selling capacity.** Kya founder abhi personally deals close kar raha hai? _Haan → Founder-Led, FDE. Nahi, sales team mojood hai → Enterprise Field, AI-Outbound, Channel._

2. **Product complexity.** Kya product ko evaluate karne se pehle buyer education ki zaroorat hai? _Kam → PLG, Marketplace, Open-Source. Moderate → Founder-Led, AI-Outbound. Zyada → Enterprise Field, FDE, Value-Based._

3. **Time-to-value.** Buyer pehli interaction ke baad kitni jaldi meaningful value experience karta hai? _Minute → PLG, Marketplace. Din-hafte → Open-Source, AI-Outbound, Pilot. Mahinay → Enterprise Field, FDE, Value-Based._

4. **Outcome measurability.** Kya tumhare product se buyer ki success cleanly measure ho sakti hai? _Zyada → Pay-Per-Outcome, Value-Based. Kam → PLG, Enterprise Field, Channel (access ke roop mein priced)._

5. **Buyer technical sophistication.** Primary buyer technically kitna literate hai? _Developer / engineer → Open-Source, PLG, Marketplace. Business / operator → Founder-Led, AI-Outbound, Enterprise Field. Executive / procurement-led → Enterprise Field, FDE, Value-Based, Hyperscaler._

6. **Procurement friction.** Buyer ka typical procurement cycle kitna lamba hai? _Din-hafte → PLG, Marketplace, Open-Source. Mahinay → Founder-Led, AI-Outbound, Enterprise Field (pilot phase ke saath). Quarter → Enterprise Field, FDE, Channel, Value-Based._

7. **Channel aur partner ecosystem.** Kya third parties pehle se tumhare target buyer ko adjacent tareeqon se serve kar rahe hain? _Haan, deep → Channel, Hyperscaler, SI partnership. Haan, light → Marketplace, Community. Nahi → Founder-Led, AI-Outbound, Enterprise Field._

8. **Capital aur patience.** Tumhari team significant revenue ki zaroorat se pehle kitna waqt operate kar sakti hai? _6 mahine se kam → PLG, Marketplace, Open-Source. 6-18 mahine → Founder-Led, AI-Outbound. 18+ mahine → Enterprise Field, FDE, Value-Based._

Diagnostic tumhe nahi batata kaunsi motion sahi hai. Woh batata hai kaunsi motions tumhare liye _available_ hain tumhari starting position ko dekhte hue. Upar ki matrix aur neeche ki deep sections batati hain ke available motions mein se kaunsi sharpest hai us buyer ke liye jise tum bech rahe ho.

## Buyer Maturity Curve aur Timing

Is catalog mein har motion buyer ke apne AI journey mein aik window hai. Jis buyer ne kabhi AI deploy nahi kiya woh mukhtalif tareeqe se khareedta hai jo buyer se jo production AI scale par chalata hai. AI-native buyer ke liye jo motions zabardast kaam karti hain woh AI-curious buyer ke liye ajeeb lagti hain, aur iska ulat bhi.

Teen stages buyer maturity curve ko define karti hain.

**Stage 1 — AI-Curious.** Buyer AI mein interested hai lekin production mein deploy nahi kiya. Procurement AI ko exotic treat karta hai; legal ko scratch se AI-specific language draft karna padta hai; security ke paas koi AI vendor template nahi hai. Buyer pilots, references, aur aik sponsor chahta hai jo internally vouch kar sake. Sales cycles slow hain kyunki har pehli baar objection uthaya ja raha hai. Sab se behtar motions: Founder-Led, FDE, Enterprise Field paid pilot phase ke saath.

**Stage 2 — AI-Piloting.** Buyer ne experiments chalaye hain. Andar aik AI champion hai (aksar VP of Engineering, Chief Data Officer, ya AI-curious COO) jis ne kam se kam aik model production mein ship kiya hai. Procurement ke paas basic AI vendor template hai. Sales cycles tez hain (3-6 mahine) kyunki buyer jaanta hai kya poochna hai. Sab se behtar motions: Enterprise Field, AI-Augmented Outbound, Channel.

**Stage 3 — AI-Native.** Buyer AI ko core infrastructure treat karta hai. AI-native team hai, AI procurement playbook hai, aur buyer outcome-based pricing options expect karta hai. Sales cycles clear-fit products ke liye tez ho sakte hain (hafte) lekin procurement scrutiny intense hai — security review rigorous hai, competitive benchmarking thorough hai, aur buyer outcomes par negotiate karega, sirf price par nahi. Sab se behtar motions: Pay-Per-Outcome, Value-Based, Hyperscaler Co-Sell, PLG (departmental adoption ke liye).

Geography curve ko tez ya der kar deta hai. Silicon Valley, Seattle, Boston, New York, London, Toronto, Berlin, Bangalore, aur Singapore 2026 mein predominantly Stage 2 ya Stage 3 markets hain — in ecosystems ke buyers ne AI experiments chalaye hain, internal AI procurement templates likhe hain, aur outcome-based pricing options demand karna shuru kar rahe hain. Dunya ka baqi hissa do teen saal peeche hai: Europe ke zyada tar hisson, Latin America, Middle East, Africa, aur Southeast Asia ke enterprise buyers abhi solidly Stage 1 mein hain, Stage 2 ki taraf move kar rahe hain.

Founders ke liye global selling ka yeh matlab hai ke same product ko mukhtalif markets mein mukhtalif motions ki zaroorat hai. Aik self-serve PLG product San Francisco aur São Paulo mein same tareeqe se becha ja sakta hai — buyer user hai, aur user ki sophistication geography par depend nahi karti. Lekin San Francisco ke AI-native buyers ke liye calibrated enterprise field motion Karachi, Lagos, ya Jakarta mein fail hogi — nahi kyunki local buyer kam sophisticated hai, balke unki AI procurement kam mature hai. Seller ko motion slow karna padega: zyada education, zyada references, lambay pilots, procurement mein zyada madad.

Ulta mismatch bhi utna hi costly hai. Stage 1 buyers ke liye calibrated motion (heavy hand-holding, lambay pilots, founder-har-call par) Stage 3 markets mein behooda mehngi hai aur kamzori signal karti hai. Stage 3 buyer outcome-based pricing aur char-hafte ki procurement cycle chahta hai. Unhe Stage 1 motion bechna seller ko unsophisticated, slow, aur shayad integration effort ke qabil nahi batata hai. Global selling karne wale founder ko jaanna chahiye ke unka buyer _aaj_ kaunsi stage mein hai, nahi ke unke reference customers kaunsi stage mein hain. Stage 3 buyers ke liye calibrated motion Stage 1 buyers mein fail hogi, aur iska ulat bhi.

![Buyer Maturity Curve](/img/buyer-maturity.png)

| Stage | Kya Hai | Best Motions |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **AI-Curious** | AI deploy nahi kiya production mein | Founder-Led, FDE, Enterprise Field |
| **AI-Piloting** | Experiments run kiye hain | Enterprise Field, AI-Outbound, Channel |
| **AI-Native** | AI core infrastructure hai | Pay-Per-Outcome, Value-Based, Hyperscaler |

## A. Buyer-Led Motions

### Motion 1 — Self-Serve PLG (Product-Led Growth)
*Proven. Beginner: Easy.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Vending machine — buyer swipe karta hai, product milta hai. Koi salesperson nahi, koi contract nahi chhoti subscriptions ke liye. Self-Serve PLG bilkul yahi hai: product khud selling karta hai. Buyer sign up karta hai, minutes mein value experience karta hai, aur khud upgrade karta hai jab usage paid tier justify kare.

Yeh tab kaam karta hai jab product aik single user ko fori, obvious value deliver kare bina organizational coordination ke. Cursor (AI code editor) aik saaf misaal hai: engineer sign up karta hai, AI assistance ke saath code likhta hai, pehle session mein value dekhta hai, aur free-tier limits hit hone par upgrade karta hai. Linear, Notion AI, aur ElevenLabs sab is motion ki variants chalate hain.

*Founding motion ke liye sab se behtar jahan fori single-user value ho. Aksar Enterprise Field ke saath pair hota hai jab company scale karti hai — PLG → Enterprise hybrid 2026 mein sab se common motion sequences mein se aik hai.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Curiosity aur value ke darmiyan friction khatam karo. Sign-up flow mein har qadam jo value produce nahi karta usse hatao. Product khud sales pitch hai, demo hai, aur close hai.

**Kab use karein:** Jab target buyer user ho (alag procurement role nahi), jab value fori ho (minute, hafte nahi), aur jab price point corporate-card thresholds se neeche ho (aam taur par under $200/seat/month, halanke yeh barh raha hai jab AI products clear ROI dikha rahe hain).

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** PLG kaam karta hai kyunki yeh traditional B2B sales funnel ko invert karta hai. Sales-qualified leads ko salesperson ko push karne ki bajaye, product qualified buyers produce karta hai jo khud ko paid conversion tak pull karte hain. CAC product investment (onboarding, activation, in-product upgrade prompts) par dominate hota hai headcount par nahi. Margin zyada hai kyunki sales team ki payment nahi hai. Constraint conversion-to-paid hai: zyada tar PLG products free users ko 2-5% paid mein convert karti hain.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _FocusFlow socho_, aik $20-per-month AI app jo kisi ko bhi email organize karne mein madad karta hai. Aik user lunch par sign up karta hai, inbox connect karta hai, aur paanch minute mein productively use kar raha hota hai. Woh pehle hafte mein sau AI summaries per day ki free-tier ceiling hit karta hai aur paid mein upgrade karta hai — koi salesperson shamil nahi. FocusFlow ki revenue isliye barhti hai kyunki product khud free users ko paying mein convert karta hai, nahi kyunki kisi ne unhe call kiya.

**Pakki misaal (Confirmed examples):** Cursor ka raasta free-tier code editor se paid subscriptions se enterprise contracts tak. Linear, Notion AI, Perplexity Pro. ElevenLabs voice generation ke liye.

**Primary Risk:** Conversion-to-paid stall hoti hai. Product adoption milta hai lekin users kabhi upgrade nahi karte. _Hal:_ upgrade trigger ko product mein directly design karo. Free tier ki usage ceiling itni tight honi chahiye ke genuine power users pehle hafte mein hit karein, saal mein nahi.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Self-serve plateau. Motion departmental adoption ke liye kaam karti hai lekin company ki enterprise expansion (security review, multi-seat negotiation, custom contracts) aik sales motion chahti hai jo team ne nahi banayi. _Hal:_ pehla enterprise seller tab hire karo jab product-led growth enterprise-scale prospects produce kare, uske baad nahi. "Buyer-led" se "vendor-led" ki transition deliberate motion design chahti hai.

**Pehla qadam:** Aik free tier banao jo sign-up ke paanch minute mein genuine value ka moment create kare. Us moment ke bina, koi bhi PLG mechanic matter nahi karta.

### Motion 2 — Marketplace-Led
*Proven. Beginner: Easy.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Busy bazaar mein stall kiraye par lena — crowd pehle se hai. Marketplace-Led selling ka matlab hai apna AI product aik established platform ke app store mein list karna (Salesforce AppExchange, Shopify App Store, Microsoft AppSource, ChatGPT Apps directory, Atlassian Marketplace). Marketplace discovery, billing, aur initial trust signal handle karta hai. Tum product handle karte ho.

Yeh motion AI products ke liye uniquely powerful hai kyunki buyer aksar platform ke andar hota hai jab woh decide karta hai ke AI feature chahiye. Salesforce admin ko agar AI lead-scoring tool chahiye to woh Google se pehle AppExchange par search karega.

*Founding distribution motion ke liye sab se behtar ya established products ke liye complementary channel. Aksar scale par company ki aklauti motion nahi hoti.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Platform ki distribution, billing, aur trust apparatus inherit karo. Direct customer acquisition costs ki bajaye revenue share ya listing fees ke zariye iski qeemat chukao.

**Kab use karein:** Jab tumhara AI product platform ke existing user base mein cleanly fit ho, jab platform ke customers tumhare target customer se match karein, aur jab platform ki revenue share tumhari direct customer acquisition cost se neeche ho.

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** Marketplace aik saath teen founder problems hal karta hai: discovery (buyer platform mein shopping karte hue tumhe dhundhta hai), billing (platform credit cards, invoicing, aur tax handle karta hai), aur trust (platform ka vetting process khud trust signal hai). Trade-off platform ki revenue share (aam taur par 15-30%) aur platform ki policy risk hai (woh terms change kar sakte hain ya competing first-party feature launch kar sakte hain).

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _NoteSnap socho_, Salesforce ke liye aik AI summarization app. NoteSnap Salesforce AppExchange mein list hota hai. AppExchange par productivity tools browse karta hua Salesforce admin NoteSnap dhundhta hai, install karta hai, free trial mein value dekhta hai, aur paid mein convert hota hai — sab Salesforce ke apne billing system ke zariye. NoteSnap ne kabhi outbound campaign nahi chalaya; platform ne customer acquisition kiya.

**Pakki misaal (Confirmed examples):** Salesforce AppExchange aur Shopify App Store ki successful companies ki lambi list. 2026 mein Claude Apps aur ChatGPT Apps ke zariye shipping productivity agents. Meta aur TikTok ad platforms mein embedded AI-native ad-creative tools.

**Primary Risk:** Platform risk asymmetric aur existential hai. Policy change, take-rate increase, ya first-party feature launch marketplace-led business ko raat mein khatam kar sakta hai. _Hal:_ platform ke bahar aik direct relationship layer maintain karo — apni email list, community, ya data export pathway — taake platform termination recoverable ho fatal nahi. Plan karo ke platform tumse eventually compete karega.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Platform dependency. Jab zyada tar revenue aik platform se aati hai, to platform ke pricing ya policy changes tumhari poori economics ko hilaa sakti hain. _Hal:_ revenue ko multiple channels mein distribute karo aur direct relationship hamesha build karo.

**Pehla qadam:** Aik platform chuno aur usmein first-class citizen bano (top-rated, deeply integrated, frequently updated) doosre par list karne se pehle.

### Motion 3 — Open-Source-Led
*Proven. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Recipe free do, catering charge karo. Open-Source-Led selling ka matlab hai apne product ke core ko open-source code ke roop mein publish karna jo koi bhi developer parh sakta hai, modify kar sakta hai, aur chala sakta hai. Jitne zyada developers isse istemal karein, tumhari reputation barhti hai, contributors project mein shamil hote hain, aur code behtar hota hai — bina marketing team ki payment ke. Phir tum paid version bechte ho jismein woh cheezein hain jo companies ko chahiye: managed hosting, security features, audit logs, support contracts, regulated-environment certifications.

Yeh motion khas taur par AI infrastructure ke liye powerful hai: agent frameworks, evaluation tools, aur developer libraries jahan developer mindshare primary moat hai.

*Founding motion ke liye sab se behtar infrastructure-shaped products ke liye. Aksar Channel ya Hyperscaler Co-Sell ke saath pair hota hai jab commercial offering mature ho.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Open-source project ko globally distributed marketing function ke roop mein istemal karo. Community project ko evangelize karti hai; users ka aik hissa paying customers ban jaata hai jab unhe operational, security, ya scale needs hoti hain jo open project handle nahi karta.

**Kab use karein:** Jab target buyer technical ho (developers, ML engineers, infrastructure teams), jab credible reason ho ke company managed version ke liye pay karegi (hosting, compliance, support) khud open version chalane ki bajaye, aur jab team community banane ke liye multi-year arc mein open-source development sustain kar sake.

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** Open-source-led kaam karta hai kyunki developer adoption enterprise adoption se aage chalti hai — engineers apni machines par open project ke saath experiment karte hain, phir andar advocate karte hain jab company ko related need hoti hai. Pre-sales relationship koi bhi salesperson hire hone se pehle ban jaati hai. Constraint open-to-commercial conversion hai: bohot se open-source projects technology kharab hone ki wajah se nahi fail hoti, balke kyunki founders ne free ke upar kabhi clear paid product nahi banaya.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _AgentCore socho_, aik open-source framework jo developers ko AI agents banane deta hai. Hazaron developers AgentCore free mein download karte hain, project mein contribute karte hain, aur apps banate hain. AgentCore ka commercial offering, AgentCore Cloud, enterprises ko managed hosting, single sign-on, audit logs, aur compliance certifications ke liye charge karta hai. Free project adoption drive karta hai; paid version un customers ko capture karta hai jinhe enterprise features chahiye.

**Pakki misaal (Confirmed examples):** LangChain, Continue, n8n, Cline, aur widely-adopted open cores ke upar commercial managed versions ship karne wali agent frameworks ki lambi list. Yeh pattern current agent ecosystem mein sab se prominent mein se aik hai.

**Primary Risk:** Open-to-commercial conversion fail ho jaye. Open project bohot popular hai lekin commercial revenue kamzor hai. _Hal:_ pehle se decide karo ke tum kya _kabhi_ nahi denge — aam taur par managed hosting, single-sign-on, audit logs, advanced security, aur enterprise support. Line ko publicly commit karo. Volunteers aur contributors faith kho dete hain agar line hilti hai.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Hyperscaler appropriation. AWS, Azure, ya GCP tumhare open project ko apni managed service mein wrap karke tumse zyada distribute kare. _Hal:_ hyperscaler partner programs ke saath pehle se kaam karo, ya aik license (BSL, SSPL) istemal karo jo explicit license ke bina cloud-provider commercial use ko limit karta hai.

**Pehla qadam:** Open project ko genuinely useful state mein ship karo commercial offering ke baare mein baat karne se pehle. Communities bait-and-switch ko door se soongh sakti hain.

### Motion 4 — Community-Led
*Proven. Beginner: Easy.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Kuch bechne se pehle mashoor ho jao. Community-Led ka matlab hai audience banana — Discord server, YouTube channel, Substack, tutorial library, public-build-in-public X account — paid product ship karne se kaafi pehle. Jab tak tum ship karo, audience pehle se mojood hai. Woh tumhare product ka wait kar rahe the. Woh khareedenge, evangelize karenge, aur early-version flaws maaf karenge kyunki woh tumhari success mein personal investment mehsoos karte hain.

Yeh motion technical audiences ke liye Open-Source-Led se aur kuch AI tools ke liye Marketplace-Led se overlap karti hai, lekin iski distinctive feature founder ya team ki public identity hai entry point ke roop mein.

*Founding motion ke liye sab se behtar products ke liye jin ki strong creative identity ho. Founder ki personal reach se aage scale karna mushkil hai bina community ko deliberately structured brand asset mein convert kiye.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Pehle audience banao, phir product. Distribution ko quarterly expense ki bajaye long-term moat samjho.

**Kab use karein:** Jab founder ya team target buyer ke ecosystem mein credibly content produce kar sake (developer-influencer dev tools ke liye, creator-influencer creator tools ke liye, operator-influencer vertical SaaS ke liye), jab embed hone ke liye koi established marketplace ya platform na ho, aur jab team serious revenue se pehle 12+ mahine audience building mein patiently invest kar sake.

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** Community-led kaam karta hai kyunki audience khud ko pre-qualify karti hai. Jo log developer-influencer ko do saal follow karte hain aur phir us influencer ka product khareedte hain woh paid acquisition ke cold lead se 10-20× zyada convert hone ke chances rakhte hain. Trust, banane ka sab se mehnga sales asset, product launch se upar stream mein ban jaata hai.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _VideoMaker socho_, aik AI video editing tool. Iske founder ne YouTube aur TikTok par do saal tutorials post kiye, product launch karne se pehle pachaas hazar followers ki audience banayi. Launch day tak audience wait kar rahi thi. Pehle hazaar paid customers seedha uski existing followers se aaye; launch-week revenue zyada tar pre-launch products ki pehle saal ki kamai se zyada thi.

**Pakki misaal (Confirmed examples):** Tiago Forte ka Building a Second Brain franchise (productivity), Lenny Rachitsky ka product-management substack-to-software trajectory, aur AI-creator personalities jo apni audiences ko tools launch karte hain. Yeh pattern companies se zyada individuals se shaped hai.

**Primary Risk:** Community dilution jab scale karo. Early audience intimacy aur direct founder access ko value deti hai; jab company scale karti hai, woh intimacy na-mumkin ho jaati hai. _Hal:_ community ko brand asset mein badlo (named programs, public events, tier rewards) hamesha personal founder presence par depend karne ki bajaye.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Content-to-product gap. Audience content ke liye aayi thi, product ke liye nahi. Agar product content ki quality se match nahi karta, to community ka trust toot jaata hai. _Hal:_ product development ko content journey ka hissa banao — public build karo, audience ko shamil karo, early feedback loops banao.

**Pehla qadam:** Product bechne se pehle content produce karna shuru karo. Bina commercial pitch ke barah mahine consistent content, minimum entry price hai.

## B. Vendor-Led Motions

### Motion 5 — Founder-Led Sales
*Proven. Beginner: Easy.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Chef khud guests bhi serve kare. Founder-Led Sales bilkul yahi hai: founder personally pehle 5-50 deals hand-close karta hai sales team hire karne se pehle. Koi doosra tareeqa nahi seekhne ka ke buyer ko asal mein kya value hai, kya objections aati hain, aur asal sales playbook kaisa dikhta hai. Founder jo yeh step skip karta hai aur VP of Sales bahut jaldi hire karta hai woh aik sales motion ship karta hai jo validate nahi hui aur aik team jo improvise nahi kar sakti.

*Founding sales motion ke liye sab se behtar kisi bhi complex B2B AI product ke liye. 6-18 mahine ke andar AI-Augmented Outbound, Enterprise Field, ya doosre vendor-led motion mein transition ki planning karo — founder bandwidth binding constraint hai.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Founder team mein aik lauta shakhs hai jis ke paas product context aur strategic discretion dono hain non-standard deals close karne ke liye. Pehle phase ke liye founder istemal karo, phir founder ne jo seekha usse repeatable playbook mein convert karo sellers hire karne se pehle.

**Kab use karein:** Hamesha, B2B AI-native companies ke liye pre-Series A ya sales motion ki evolution mein pehle. Bhi companies jo primarily PLG motion chalati hain unhe early enterprise deals ke liye founder-led sales se faida hota hai.

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** Founder-Led kaam karta hai kyunki har early deal partially custom hai — buyer young company par risk le raha hai, aur founder aik lauta shakhs hai jo authoritatively keh sakta hai "haan, yeh kar sakte hain" aur yeh sach ho. Constraint founder bandwidth hai: founder aam taur par har quarter 3-5 deals close kar sakta hai jabke product bana raha hota hai, capital raise kar raha hota hai, aur team recruit kar raha hota hai. Uske baad, motion founder par bottleneck ho jaati hai.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _LegalDraft socho_, aik AI legal-research tool. Founder, aik former corporate attorney, personally apni purani law firm ke tees doston ko call karti hai. Woh product demo karti hai, har call par pricing negotiate karti hai, aur pehle pandra customers khud sign karti hai — koi bhi salesperson hire kiye bina. Woh in pandra conversations mein yeh bhi discover karti hai ke feature jise woh killer use case samajh thi thi barely valuable hai, aur boring feature jo woh almost kaat deti woh hai jiske liye customers actually pay karte hain.

**Pakki misaal (Confirmed patterns):** 2025-2026 mein zyada tar successful B2B AI-native startups, chahe woh eventually kaunsi bhi motion par scale karein, pehle 5-50 customers ke liye founder-led sales chalayi. Harvey, Sierra, Glean, aur Hebbia sab is pattern mein fit hoti hain.

**Primary Risk:** Founder bottleneck. Founder sales meetings mein bahut zyada hai ke product bana sake ya company chala sake. _Hal:_ motion ko explicitly sunset karo. Pehle 30-50 deals ke baad, founder ko sales playbook likhna chahiye, agle 50 deals nahi chalane chahiye.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Handoff fail ho jaye. Pehla sales hire founder jo kiya woh replicate nahi kar sakta, kyunki founder ki selling mein unwritten product commitments, pricing improvisation, aur personal relationships shamil thin. _Hal:_ har commitment, har pricing exception, aur har deal structure ko waise hi document karo jaise woh ho raha hai. Handoff document founder-led sales ke doran banta hai, uske baad nahi.

**Pehla qadam:** Founder ko personally next deal close karna chahiye. Baad mein jo bhi aaye woh us aik deal mein seekhi gayi cheezon par bana hai.

### Motion 6 — AI-Augmented Outbound
*Emerging. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** 50 ka SDR team 5 mein compress karo. AI-Augmented Outbound AI agents ka istemal research, drafting, aur follow-up kaam ke liye karta hai jise historically sales development representatives ki fauj ki zaroorat thi — aik choti human team ko live conversations aur demos ke liye chhodta hai jo AI abhi acha nahi kar sakta. Traditional SDR tees personalized emails roz bhejta tha. AI-augmented SDR teen hazaar bhejta hai.

Yeh woh sales motion hai jise AI ne khud mumkin banaya hai. Do saal pehle, underlying technology kaafi nahi thi. Aaj, well-tuned AI-augmented outbound best human SDR teams ki personalization quality se match ya exceed karta hai — headcount ke aik chotay se hisse mein, human SDR important conversations mein loop mein.

*Primary motion ke liye mid-market vendor-led GTM ke liye sab se behtar. Kisi bhi vendor-led motion ke upar augmentation layer ke roop mein bhi kaam karti hai. Mechanics underlying AI tooling ke saath tezi se evolve ho rahe hain.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** AI agents se upper-funnel research, drafting, aur follow-up kaam karwao. Aik choti human team rakho jo bottom-funnel handle kare (live conversations, demo coordination, deal navigation) jahan AI abhi human judgment ko replace nahi kar sakta.

**Kab use karein:** Jab target buyer email ya LinkedIn se reachable ho (mid-market tech buyers ke liye typical, executive-level enterprise buyers ke liye kam reliable), jab product 30-minute call mein demonstrate ho sake, aur jab team ke paas RevOps maturity ho AI ke prompts aur behavior ko instrument aur tune karne ki.

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** AI-augmented outbound kaam karta hai kyunki traditional outbound mein limiting factor hamesha scale par personalization quality thi. Humans roz 30 personalized emails likh sakte thay; AI 3,000 likh sakta hai. Constraint outreach volume se deliverability, response quality, aur human SDR ki capacity mein shift hoti hai increased response rate handle karne ki. Early teams jo yeh motion achi tarah chalati hain pure human outbound par meaningful pipeline efficiency improvements report karti hain — typical claims 2-4× range mein hain — halanke independent benchmarks scarce hain aur comparison heavily is baat par depend karta hai ke "pipeline" kya measure ho rahi hai.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _SalesScope socho_, aik B2B AI tool. Iski paanch SDR team aik AI agent ka istemal har hafte das hazar prospects research karne, har ek ke liye personalized outreach draft karne, aur automatically follow-up karne ke liye karti hai. Human SDRs sirf live conversations handle karte hain jo result hoti hain. Team manual outreach wali comparable pachaas SDR team se har mahine zyada meetings book karti hai — aur daswein headcount cost par.

**Pakki misaal (Emerging analogues):** Apollo, Clay, Salesloft AI, Outreach AI, aur 2025-2026 mein ship hone wali AI-native sales engagement platforms ki wave. Bohot se AI-native vendors yeh motion apni primary outbound engine ke roop mein chalate hain.

**Primary Risk:** Buyer fatigue AI-generated outreach se. Jaise AI-augmented outbound phailta hai, buyers isse pehchaanna aur ignore karna seekhte hain. _Hal:_ research aur drafting ke liye AI istemal karo, lekin actual sending aur follow-up ke liye human SDRs ko conversation mein rakho. "AI-generated, AI-detected, ignored" raasta haqeeqi hai aur barh raha hai.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Compliance aur deliverability. Scale par AI-augmented outbound ESP (email service provider) penalties trigger kar sakta hai ya jurisdictional rules tod sakta hai. _Hal:_ deliverability infrastructure mein invest karo aur regional regulations ko sakhti se follow karo.

**Pehla qadam:** Apni mojooda SDR team ki actual time allocation audit karo. Agar woh research aur drafting mein 40% se zyada waqt guzaarti hai, to AI-augmented outbound ka clear leverage maujood hai. Agar woh zyada tar waqt live calls aur meeting coordination mein guzaarti hai, to leverage chota hai.

### Motion 7 — Enterprise Field Sales
*Proven. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Dealership customer ke office mein — $1M ki car bech rahe ho. Enterprise Field Sales traditional B2B sales motion hai: account executives saalana $1-5M quotas carry karte hain, multi-stakeholder deals 3-9 month cycles mein close karte hain, jahan executive champions, technical evaluators, security reviewers, legal, aur procurement sab ko navigate karna padta hai.

Yeh motion sab se zyada old-school enterprise software (Oracle, SAP, Salesforce) se associated hai. Yeh aklauti motion bhi hai jo reliably six-figure AI deals scale par produce karti hai.

*Primary motion ke liye sab se behtar products jo six-figure deals target karti hain. Aksar destination motion hai jismein PLG, Founder-Led, aur AI-Augmented Outbound companies scale karte hue graduate hoti hain.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Buyer ki procurement complexity ko sales-team specialization se match karo. Jahan buyer ke paas CFO hai jo spend approve karta hai, CIO hai jo architecture approve karta hai, security team hai jo vendor approve karti hai, aur legal team hai jo contract approve karti hai — seller ko equivalent specialists chahiye.

**Kab use karein:** Jab deal sizes saalana $100K se upar hon, jab buyer formal procurement wali bari organization ho, jab product ko evaluate karne ke liye 30-minute se zyada conversation ki zaroorat ho, aur jab team ke paas lambi sales cycle ko support karne ke liye 18+ mahine ka capital ho.

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** Enterprise field sales kaam karta hai kyunki bari organizations bad-vendor risk minimize karne ke liye designed processes ke zariye khareedti hain. Seller ka kaam hai us process ko navigate karna — andar champions banana, security documentation dena, contracts negotiate karna — mahino mein jo organization ko purchase formally approve karne mein lagte hain. Lagbhag har enterprise AI deal paid pilot ke baad production contract ke roop mein structured hoti hai; pilot alag motion nahi balke is motion ka standard entry phase hai.

Constraint sales-team CAC hai. Fully-loaded enterprise account executive — salary, commission, benefits, sales-engineering allocation, tooling — aam taur par saalana mid-six-figures mein chalta hai aur full quota tak ramp hone mein chay se nau mahine lagte hain. Jab tak woh ramp complete na ho, har AE bina offsetting revenue ke cost hai. Companies jo bahut jaldi, playbook validate hone se pehle, bahut se AEs hire karti hain woh capital deals ke close hone se tezi se jalaati hain.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _HRSmart socho_, Fortune 500 HR teams ke liye aik AI tool. Aik deal band karna chay mahine lagta hai. Account executive pehle VP of HR se milta hai, phir CIO se, phir security review (teen hafte), phir legal (chay hafte), phib procurement (char hafte). Deal $400,000 ACV par band hoti hai. AE fully loaded $400,000 saalana cost karta hai aur full quota tak ramp hone mein nau mahine lagta hai. Math tabhi kaam karti hai jab har AE is scale par saalana teen se paanch deals band kare.

**Pakki misaal (Confirmed examples):** Glean, Harvey, Sierra, Writer, aur Cresta mein zyada tar six-figure-and-above AI-native deals enterprise field motions ke zariye chalti hain. In companies mein se har ek ne formal sales organization banayi hai specialized roles ke saath (AEs, SEs, customer success, sales engineering).

**Primary Risk:** Lambay sales cycles capital jalaate hain. Fully-loaded mid-six-figure AE cost par 6-9 month cycle ka matlab hai ke har AE koi bhi deal close hone se pehle meaningful capital consume karta hai. _Hal:_ choti tadaad mein high-value target accounts par concentrate karo (account-based selling) hazaron mid-market companies par outbound spray karne ki bajaye.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Bhaari CAC ratios. Enterprise field motions 18+ mahine ki CAC payback periods produce kar sakti hain, jo venture-backed companies ke liye unsustainable hain jinhe efficiency demonstrate karna hai. _Hal:_ enterprise field ko PLG ya pilot motion ke saath combine karo jo lower-cost initial entry produce kare, phir account ke andar expand karo.

**Pehla qadam:** Aik enterprise seller hire karo aur use 6 mahine tak playbook chalne do doosra hire karne se pehle. Doosre hire ki success pehle hire ne jo seekha us par bani hai, parallel learning par nahi.

### Motion 8 — Forward-Deployed Engineering (FDE)
*Proven. Beginner: Advanced.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Embedded chefs, menus nahi. Apne engineers ko customer ke kitchen mein bhejo aur custom meals banao. Waqt ke saath recipes aisi menu ban jaati hain jo doosre customers ko bech sako. Forward-Deployed Engineering ka matlab hai apne engineers ka (aur ab AI Workers ka) chota team mahino ke liye aik customer ki organization mein embed karna, sirf un ke liye custom solution banake. Har engagement team ko kuch specific seekhata hai ke customer ki industry asal mein kaise kaam karti hai. Teesri ya chauthi deployment tak, tumhare paas itne productized patterns hote hain ke aik self-serve vertical product launch kar sako.

Palantir ne defense aur intelligence mein model invent kiya. Anthropic ki Applied AI team aur OpenAI ka Forward Deployed function aaj lab level par isse chala rahe hain. Sierra aur kai enterprise AI vendors iski variants chala rahe hain.

*Founding motion ke liye sab se behtar enterprise-scale strategic deals ke liye ya mid-stage path ke liye product companies jinhe specific industry mein vertical depth chahiye. Aksar Vertical AI-Native Greenfield mein transition hoti hai.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Engagement khud product discovery hai. Engagement ke doran earn kiye gaye patterns woh moat ban jaate hain jo company ko scale karne dete hain.

**Kab use karein:** Jab target customer strategic enterprise ho (government, bari bank, bari hospital system, bari industrial company), jab buyer ki problem itni complex ho ke generic product hal na kar sake, aur jab team ke paas har early engagement par 6-18 mahine guzaarne ka capital aur patience ho.

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** FDE kaam karta hai kyunki yeh vertical products ka cold-start problem hal karta hai. Pehla customer company ko industry seekhane ke liye pay karta hai; baad ke customers aik product khareedte hain jo pehle engagement ke through harden hua hai. Har engagement kaam ka aik hissa reusable patterns mein convert karta hai; waqt ke saath, reusable se custom work ka ratio palat jaata hai, aur company services-margin economics se software-margin economics mein graduate hoti hai.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _MedAgent socho_, hospital systems ke liye aik AI tool. MedAgent ka pehla customer bari hospital network hai. MedAgent ke teen engineers chay mahine ke liye hospital ke offices mein move karte hain, us hospital ke specific clinical workflows ke liye MedAgent customize karte hain. Hospital engagement ke liye $2M pay karta hai. Engineers ke doran discover kiye gaye patterns — common documentation flows, integration points, clinical-language conventions — productized version mein reusable features ban jaate hain jo MedAgent baad mein doosre hospitals ko kam margins par lekin zyada volume par bechta hai.

**Pakki misaal (Confirmed examples):** Palantir ki defense aur commercial deployments. Anthropic ki Applied AI Team. OpenAI ka Forward Deployed function major enterprise accounts ke liye. Choti AI-native consultancies jo paid pilots chalati hain jo waqt ke saath productize hoti hain.

**Primary Risk:** Service-business gravity. Team ko pehle din se real revenue milti hai custom work bechne se, jo seductive aur addictive hai. Hamesha custom work karte rehna aur kabhi real product ki mushkil jump na karna bahut aasaan hai. _Hal:_ demand karo ke har engagement kam se kam aik reusable pattern produce kare jo next engagement mein ship ho. Custom se productized work ka ratio top operating metric ke roop mein track karo.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Senior engineering bandwidth. FDE company ke sab se senior engineering talent ko mahino ke liye consume karta hai. _Hal:_ saath chalne wali FDE engagements ki tadaad ko limit karo jo company sustain kar sakti hai. Do FDE deployments saath chalana reasonable hai; paanch ka matlab aam taur par sab par quality suffer kar rahi hai.

**Pehla qadam:** Aik strategic customer ko full-margin pricing par embedded engagement ke liye sign karo. Company ka product roadmap partially us customer ke actual workflow se shaped hona chahiye.

## C. Outcome-Led Motions

### Motion 9 — Pay-Per-Outcome (Service-as-Software)
*Emerging. Beginner: Advanced.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Carpenter tab pay karo jab door fix ho, jab woh aaye nahi. Pay-Per-Outcome ka matlab hai buyer tab pay karta hai jab AI Worker actually result deliver kare — resolved support ticket, closed sales meeting, processed insurance claim, drafted legal document. vendor delivery risk leta hai.

Yeh woh pricing model hai jise AI era ne scale par mumkin banaya hai, kyunki AI Worker outputs measurable hain aise tareeqe se jaise software-seat outputs aam taur par nahi thin. Sierra (customer support), Decagon (customer service), EvenUp (legal claims), aur AI-native vendors ki wave 2026 mein outcome-based pricing chala rahe hain.

*Founding motion ke liye sab se behtar jab outcomes cleanly measurable hon, ya mid-stage pricing flip ke liye companies jo seat-based shuru ki thin. Service-as-Software pattern se tightly coupled.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Vendor revenue ko customer value se align karo. Vendor tabhi paisa kamata hai jab customer ko value mile, aur customer ko jitni zyada value mile, vendor utna zyada kamata hai.

**Kab use karein:** Jab AI Worker ka output cleanly attributed aur measured ho sake (resolved ticket resolved ticket hai; drafted contract drafted contract hai), jab unit economics priced outcome level par kaam karein (compute cost + AI quality cost revenue per outcome se neeche ho), aur jab buyer outcome contracts structure karne ke liye enough sophisticated ho (aam taur par AI-native ya AI-piloting buyers).

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** Outcome pricing kaam karti hai kyunki yeh seller ko customer ke software budget ki bajaye customer ke labor budget ke liye compete karne deti hai. Aik mid-market company customer support headcount par customer support software se das guna zyada kharch karta hai. AI vendor jo outcome pricing ke through headcount budget ka aik hissa capture karta hai alag revenue category mein operate karta hai software budget ka aik hissa capture karne wale vendor se.

Pricing math human labor cost se anchored hai, SaaS comparable se nahi. Agar aik customer support representative all-in lagbhag $5 per resolved ticket cost karta hai (salary + benefits + management overhead + workspace), to outcome price ceiling $1-3 per resolved ticket ke aas paas hai — human cost se kaafi neeche taake customer real savings capture kare, vendor ke compute cost se kaafi upar taake gross margin positive ho. Vendor ka compute cost per outcome (aam taur par typical agent tasks ke liye $0.20-0.80, model efficiency behtar hone ke saath tezi se gir raha hai) floor set karta hai; customer ka human cost ceiling set karta hai; price dono ke kahin beech mein hota hai. Vendor ki strategic sawaal hai kitni aggressively gap compress karna hai — wider gap ka matlab har outcome par zyada margin lekin customer adoption slow; narrower gap adoption tez karta hai lekin margin compress karta hai jab compute prices abhi gir rahe hain.

Technical foundation outcome attribution hai. Vendor ko audit-grade telemetry produce karni hogi: har priced outcome ke liye, aik verifiable record ke AI ne kya kiya, kya process kiya, aur result kaise confirm hua. Iske bina, customer disputes ka koi objective basis nahi aur revenue collection quarterly negotiation ban jaati hai. Companies jo yeh motion achi tarah chalati hain outcome-attribution infrastructure ko product ka hissa samajhti hain, accounting overhead nahi — aur isse engineers se staff karti hain, finance analysts se nahi. Jo discipline yeh banata hai woh motion ko durable banata hai; iski kami motion ko fail hone par khatam karti hai.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _TicketBot socho_, aik AI customer-support agent. TicketBot customers ko per seat charge nahi karta. Iski bajaye, customer $0.50 pay karta hai har support ticket ke liye jo TicketBot khud resolve karta hai (bina human ko escalate kiye). Pachaas hazar tickets per month wale customer ka $25,000 monthly bill hoga — lekin sirf agar TicketBot actually tickets resolve kare. Agar TicketBot incoming tickets ka sirf teesaa fisaa resolve karta hai, to bill uska teesaa hissa hai. Customer ka CFO isse pyaar karta hai; customer ki procurement team ko seekhna padega ke contract kaise structure karein.

**Pakki misaal (Confirmed examples):** Sierra ki per-resolution pricing AI customer service ke liye. Decagon ke outcome-based contracts. EvenUp ki per-claim pricing personal-injury legal work ke liye. Yeh pattern 2026 mein sab se actively-expanding pricing structures mein se aik hai.

**Primary Risk:** Early years mein negative gross margin. Agar AI quality abhi kaafi nahi hai, to vendor failed work ka compute aur human-fallback costs pay karta hai lekin koi revenue nahi milti. _Hal:_ aik price-per-outcome set karo jismein early-quality issues ke liye margin buffer shamil ho, aur quality behtar hone ke saath tighter pricing par graduate karo. Bohot se vendors pehle 12-18 mahine near-zero gross margin par operate karte hain jab tak quality stabilize na ho.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Attribution disputes. Buyer claim karta hai AI ne outcome produce nahi ki (ya buyer ke apne staff ne produce kiya). _Hal:_ pehle din se outcome-attribution telemetry mein invest karo. Vendor ko irrefutable, audit-quality evidence chahiye ke kaunse outcomes AI Worker ne produce kiye.

**Pehla qadam:** Aik outcome chuno (sab se saaf, sab se measurable) aur price karo. Ek saath multiple outcomes price karne ki jaldi mat karo jab tak pehle ki economics proven na ho jaayein.

### Motion 10 — Value-Based Engagement
*Speculative. Beginner: Advanced.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** McKinsey style — sirf tab pay karo jab savings create hon. Aik consulting firm hire karna socho jo sirf unki create ki gayi savings ka percentage pay kare — McKinsey ya BCG style engagement, lekin jahan deliverable slide deck nahi balke live AI Worker hai, aur pricing seedha customer ke measured P&L improvement se tied hai. Value-Based Engagement ka matlab hai bari strategic deals ko business value created ke percentage ke roop mein structure karna, jahan rates deal complexity aur buyer sophistication ke hisaab se widely vary karti hain. Aam un AI deployments ke liye jo sau karod dollars ke P&L lines ko touch karti hain.

Yeh motion speculative hai kyunki yeh buyer ki willingness par depend karti hai ke formal taur par value-based contracting commit kare, jo zyada tar enterprise procurement organizations abhi structure nahi kar paate. Yeh 2026 mein mostly bespoke deals ke roop mein mojood hai AI-native vendors aur forward-leaning enterprise customers ke darmiyan.

*Realistic sirf strategic-deal scale (&gt;$1M ACV) par aur sirf AI-native buyers ke saath. Early-stage companies ke liye viable motion nahi; aksar Enterprise Field ya FDE ke upar layer hoti hai sab se bari deals ke liye.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Deal ko customer ke measurable economic outcome ke function ke roop mein price karo. Vendor customer ke gain ke proportion mein upside leta hai, aur (kuch structures mein) downside leta hai agar gain threshold se neeche gir jaaye.

**Kab use karein:** Jab customer ka executive sponsor value-based contracting commit karne ka authority rakhta hai (aam taur par sirf C-suite level par), jab created value cleanly AI Worker ko attribute ho sake (doosri initiatives se confounded na ho), aur jab deal size itna bara ho ke contracting complexity justify kare.

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** Value-Based Engagement tab kaam karti hai jab dono parties agree kar sakein ke value ka kya matlab hai aur kaise measure karna hai. Structure vendor incentives ko customer outcomes se zyada tightly align karti hai kisi bhi aur pricing model se — vendor ki revenue customer ke measurable gain ke proportion mein barhti hai, conventional vendor-buyer adversarial dynamic hataati hai jahan vendor access ke liye charge karna chahta hai aur buyer results ke liye pay karna chahta hai.

Contract structure seat- ya outcome-based pricing se materially zyada complex hai. Aik typical agreement ke char components hote hain. _Baseline measurement period_ (deployment se pehle aam taur par 30-90 din) establish karta hai ke customer ke metrics AI Worker ke bina kaise dikhte thay. _Value-share formula_ define karti hai ke vendor gain ka kaunsa hissa capture karta hai — aam taur par aik percentage jo deal complexity aur buyer sophistication ke hisaab se vary karti hai. _Ceiling aur floor_ dono upside (taake vendor customer ke executives se zyada na kamaye jo internally defend kar sakein) aur downside (taake vendor product deploy karne ke liye customer ko pay na kare) cap karta hai. Aur _audit rights_ vendor ko customer ki reporting ko verify karne ki ability deti hain billing drive karne wale metrics par — audit rights ke bina, customer ki procurement organization pehle true-up cycle mein measured value ko under-report karegi jab contract us point par pahunche.

Constraint contracting maturity hai. Zyada tar enterprise procurement organizations abhi value-based deals ko scale par structure karne ke liye equipped nahi hain; legal, finance, aur operations sab ko representatives chahiye jo model samjhein aur non-standard contract terms commit karne ka authority rakhein. Isliye in deals ko aam taur par C-suite level par executive sponsor ki zaroorat hoti hai — sirf woh authority procurement organization ke default ko override kar sakta hai "hum deals aise nahi structure karte." Sponsor ke bina, proposal mid-organization mein indefinitely stall hota hai, technical merit chahe jo bhi ho. Motion 10 chalane wale sellers apni early energy zyada tar executive sponsor dhundne aur recruit karne mein lagate hain; baqi motion sponsor ke mandate ke khilaf execution hai.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _CashFlow socho_, hedge funds ke liye aik AI tool. $50B fund CashFlow deploy karta hai, aur 12-month measurement period mein, trading efficiency mein $40M saalana improvement deployment ko attribute karta hai. CashFlow ka contract baseline se upar measurable improvement ke pandra fisaa par structured hai: fund contract ki muddat ke liye saalana $6M pay karta hai. Deal negotiate karne mein nau mahine lage, fund ke CIO aur CFO ko personally approve karna pada, aur sirf isliye procurement se guzar saka kyunki executive sponsor ne push kiya.

**Pakki misaal (Emerging analogues):** Kuch Anthropic Applied AI engagements strategic enterprise customers ke saath. Kuch Palantir deployments mission outcomes ke around structured. Financial services, healthcare, aur consulting firms mein forward-leaning AI deployments. Yeh pattern itna young hai ke canonical exemplar nahi hai.

**Primary Risk:** Attribution disputes. Customer claim karta hai AI ne value produce nahi ki (ya customer ke apne initiatives ne ki). _Hal:_ engagement shuru hone se pehle baseline measurement period establish karo. Post-deployment metrics ko hypothetical counterfactual se nahi balke pre-deployment baseline se compare karo.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Lambay contracting cycles. Value-based contracts negotiate karne mein 6-12 mahine lag sakte hain, jis doran team bina revenue ke relationship mein invest kar rahi hoti hai. _Hal:_ value-based engagement ko paid pilot phase ke saath combine karo jo production contract negotiate hote hue revenue produce kare.

**Pehla qadam:** Proposal mein invest karne se pehle executive sponsor dhundo jis ke paas value-based contracting commit karne ka authority ho. Us sponsor ke bina, motion mid-organization procurement mein stall hoti hai.

## D. Partner-Led Motions

### Motion 11 — Channel & SI Partnership
*Proven. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Apple Best Buy mein bechta hai. Beverages grocery stores mein bikti hain. Bohot se vendors ke paas bahut se potential customers hain aur kaafi sales reps nahi — unhe kisi aur ko last mile of selling aur deploying karna padta hai. Channel aur Systems Integrator (SI) Partnership ka matlab hai apne AI Workers third parties ke zariye bechna — value-added resellers, consultancies (Accenture, Deloitte, Slalom, BCG, McKinsey), regional systems integrators — jo inhe apne clients ke saath broader engagements ke hissae ke roop mein deploy karte hain.

Yeh motion products ke liye essential hai jin mein significant implementation expertise ki zaroorat hai. Aik AI Worker jo bari bank mein deploy hone mein hafte lagata hai Accenture dwara deploy hone par bechna bohot aasan hai — unke paas pehle se bank ka trust hai, woh pehle se bank ke workflows jaante hain, aur unke paas pehle se contract template hai jo AI services cover karta hai.

*Primary motion ke liye sab se behtar products jin ko implementation expertise chahiye, ya Enterprise Field Sales ke saath complementary channel ke roop mein.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Partners ko implementation, customization, aur ongoing operations ka kaam handle karne do jo AI vendor khud nahi karna chahta. Partners ko margins ya referral fees ke zariye pay karo jo unke long-term success ko incentivize karein.

**Kab use karein:** Jab target customer bari enterprise ho jis ke paas pehle se SI relationships hon, jab product mein significant implementation work ho, aur jab company ke paas 12-18 mahine partner relationships banane ka patience ho jab tak channel meaningful revenue produce kare.

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** Channel-led kaam karta hai kyunki SIs ke paas enterprise buyers par trust hai jo AI vendors ke paas abhi nahi. SI ki recommendation khud sales argument hai. Constraint partner economics hai: SIs ko engagement par 30-50% margin chahiye, jo AI vendor ki pricing flexibility compress karta hai. Companies jo yeh motion achi tarah chalati hain SIs ke saath co-sell karti hain (joint sales calls, joint case studies, joint executive briefings) bajaye iske ke SI ko passive distributor treat karein.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _DocAI socho_, aik AI document-processing tool. DocAI seedha bari banks ko nahi bechta — Accenture ke zariye bechta hai. Accenture ke consultants apne banking clients ke liye DocAI implement karte hain bari digital transformation engagements ke hisse ke roop mein jo pehle se $50M budgets command karti hain. DocAI ko recurring software license revenue milti hai (aam taur par har bank ke liye $500K-$2M); Accenture ko implementation fees milti hain ($5M-$20M per engagement). DocAI ko kabhi seedha bank procurement navigate nahi karna padta — Accenture ke existing relationships woh kaam karte hain.

**Pakki misaal (Confirmed examples):** Fortune 500 accounts mein bechne wale zyada tar enterprise AI vendors direct sales motion ke saath saath channel motion chalate hain. Copilot ke liye Microsoft ka partner ecosystem. Einstein/Agentforce ke liye Salesforce ka SI ecosystem. Regional banks jo AI deploy karte hain zyada tar SI-led engagements ka istemal karte hain.

**Primary Risk:** Partner economics misalignment. Partner ko alternative vendors push karne se zyada paisa milta hai. _Hal:_ partner enablement mein invest karo (training, sales materials, technical support) taake partner ke liye tumhara product bechna operationally aasan ho alternatives se. Partner sales relationship business hai; company jo partner success mein sab se zyada invest karti hai jeetti hai.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Partner conflict direct sales ke saath. Direct sales team aur channel partner same deals ke liye compete karte hain, margins erode karte hain. _Hal:_ pehle din se clear deal-registration rules establish karo. Partners jo deal source karte hain woh uske malik hain; direct sales woh deals handle karta hai jinhe partners reach nahi kar sakte.

**Pehla qadam:** Apni target vertical mein sab se active teen SIs pehchano. Zyada jodne se pehle un mein se do ke saath deep relationships mein invest karo.

### Motion 12 — Hyperscaler Co-Sell
*Proven. Beginner: Medium.*

**Simple lafzon mein:** Cloud provider ke menu par featured hona. Hyperscaler Co-Sell ka matlab hai apna AI product AWS Marketplace, Microsoft Azure Marketplace, ya Google Cloud Marketplace par list karna, aur hyperscaler ki sales organization ke saath partner karna taake deals unke existing customer relationships mein drive kiye ja sakein. Hyperscaler ke account executives tumhara product bechne mein madad karte hain kyunki unhe tumhari deployment se underlying compute revenue milti hai.

Yeh motion AI products ke liye uniquely powerful hai kyunki underlying compute load bara hai — har AI deployment meaningful cloud spend produce karti hai, jise hyperscaler ki sales team grow karne ke liye incentivized hai.

*Primary motion ke liye sab se behtar compute-heavy AI products ke liye jin ka significant cloud footprint ho, ya Enterprise Field Sales ke upar additional channel ke roop mein layered.*

**Kya soch hai (Core idea):** Apni sales motion ko hyperscaler ki sales motion se align karo. Hyperscaler ka AE underlying compute par commission kamata hai; AE ko incentive hai tumhe unke accounts mein laane ka.

**Kab use karein:** Jab product cloud-deployed ho (aam taur par AWS, Azure, ya GCP), jab product hyperscaler ke liye meaningful compute revenue produce kare, jab team ke paas hyperscaler relationships maintain karne ki partnership-management capacity ho, aur jab target buyer pehle se meaningful cloud customer ho.

**Yeh kaise kaam karta hai (Mechanism):** Hyperscaler Co-Sell kaam karta hai kyunki hyperscaler AEs un relationships ke upar baithte hain jo AI vendors aasani se replicate nahi kar sakte — unke paas customer ke saath saalon ka trust hai, woh customer ki procurement preferences jaante hain, aur unke paas technical decision-making ke liye private channels hain. Jab hyperscaler AE apne accounts mein aik AI vendor introduce karta hai, AI vendor aik credibility shortcut inherit karta hai. Constraint hyperscaler-program participation hai: AI vendor ko hyperscaler ke partner program mein invest karna padega (certifications, joint case studies, marketplace listings) co-sell ke liye eligible hone ke liye.

**Kalpanik misaal (Fictional walk-through):** _VoiceTalk socho_, significant cloud compute requirements wala aik AI voice tool. VoiceTalk AWS Marketplace par list hota hai aur top-tier AWS partner banta hai. Fortune 1000 telecom ko bechne wala AWS account executive expanded cloud spend ki discussion ke doran VoiceTalk ka zikr karta hai. Customer VoiceTalk ko apni existing AWS contract mein jodta hai — fresh procurement se guzarne ki bajaye pehle se allocated AWS budget istemal karke. AWS underlying compute revenue kamata hai (jo bari hai — voice AI compute-heavy hai); VoiceTalk ko customer milta hai direct deal ki zaroorat se fraction of the sales cycle mein.

**Pakki misaal (Confirmed examples):** Significant cloud workloads wale zyada tar enterprise AI-native vendors hyperscaler co-sell motions chalate hain. AWS Marketplace, Azure Marketplace, aur GCP Marketplace mein se har ek ke paas 2026 mein growing AI-vendor catalogs hain. Amazon Bedrock par Anthropic ka Claude khud is dynamic ka extreme version hai, jahan hyperscaler seedha AI capability bech raha hai.

**Primary Risk:** Hyperscaler de-prioritization. Hyperscaler ki strategic priorities shift hoti hain, aur tumhara product abhi featured nahi hai. _Hal:_ hyperscaler motion ke saath saath meaningful direct sales motion maintain karo, taake de-prioritization setback ho existential event nahi.

**Doosra khatra (Secondary risk):** Multi-hyperscaler complexity. AWS, Azure, aur GCP par simultaneously bechna partner-management ka kaam teen guna karta hai. _Hal:_ us hyperscaler ko prioritize karo jis ka customer base tumhare target market se sab se zyada overlap karta hai. Baqi tab jodo jab team ke paas bandwidth ho.

**Pehla qadam:** Aik hyperscaler chuno aur doosre par list karne se pehle wahan top-tier partner bano.

## Cross-Cutting Concepts

Kai concepts motions mein baar baar aati hain aur ek baar define honi chahiyein har baar repeat karne ki bajaye.

**Procurement navigation.** Buyer ke formal purchasing process ke through deal ko aagye badhane ka process — security review, legal review, vendor approval, contract negotiation, integration approval, procurement signoff. Procurement navigation din (PLG) ya aath mahine (strategic enterprise) le sakta hai. Yeh vendor-led sales cycles mein variance ka sab se bara source hai. Seller jis ne procurement navigation ko sales motion mein nahi banaya woh baar baar deals ko "legal mein stalled" ya "security review mein atka hua" experience karega — inka matlab hai "seller ne buyer ka process nahi samjha."

AI-native vendors ke liye, teen procurement objections lagbhag har enterprise deal mein aati hain aur seller ki pehli executive conversation se pehle standard sales kit mein jawab dene chahiyein:

_Data privacy aur model training._ Enterprise procurement mein AI deals stall hone ka sab se aam reason buyer ka dar hai ke unki proprietary data vendor ke models ko train karne ke liye istemal hogi — ya worse, underlying foundation-model provider ke saath identifiable form mein share hogi. Sellers ko master service agreement mein clear, written commitment chahiye (marketing page par nahi): customer data model training ke liye istemal nahi hoti, foundation-model providers ke saath identifiable form mein share nahi hoti, aur contract termination par delete hoti hai. Vendors jo yeh objection pre-sales documentation mein handle karte hain unse 30-60 din tez close karte hain jo vendors jo legal counsel ko scratch se surface aur negotiate karne dete hain.

_Hallucination liability._ Jab AI galat ho, zimmedar kaun hai? Regulated industries (healthcare, legal, financial services) mein sellers ko pre-drafted contractual language chahiye jo AI accuracy, warranty limitations, aur material decisions ke liye humans in the loop rakhne ki customer ki zimmedari ko address kare. Is language ke bina, legal review nabbey din ya zyada leti hai jab buyer ka counsel scratch se likhta hai — aur buyer ka counsel aam taur par seller se zyada conservatively likhta.

_Compute residency aur model deployment._ Regulated industries ya non-US jurisdictions ke buyers ke liye, AI kahan chalta hai data kahan rehti hai utna hi matter karta hai. EU mein AWS Bedrock, EU mein Azure OpenAI, on-premises deployments, aur dedicated-tenant model hosting mein demand barh rahi hai. In requirements ke liye deployment story na rakhne wale sellers technical due diligence mein deals haarte hain, legal review shuru hone se pehle hi.

**Trust Ladder.** Worker Catalog mein define: AI Worker ki progression co-pilot se (suggests, human approves) supervised autonomous tak (acts, human reviews aggregate output) full autopilot tak (bina per-task supervision ke acts). Har rung alag pricing model imply karta hai. Co-pilot tool ke roop mein priced hai. Supervised autonomous seat-equivalent ke roop mein priced hai. Full autopilot outcome ke roop mein priced hai. Sales motions jo Trust Ladder ko ignore karti hain buyer ki actual deployment posture ke liye galat pricing tier ke saath end hoti hain.

**Pilot economics.** 2026 mein lagbhag har six-figure AI deal paid pilot se shuru hota hai — time-bounded, scope-limited initial deployment, aam taur par 30-90 din, jo buyer ko production contract commit karne se pehle apne environment mein product validate karne deta hai. pilot alag sales motion nahi hai; yeh aik _structure_ hai jo Enterprise Field, FDE, AI-Augmented Outbound, aur bilkul Pay-Per-Outcome motions ke andar rehti hai. Pilots paid hain (taake free consulting na hon) lekin production contract se chote — aam taur par pilot ke liye $25-100K, phir pilot ke success hone par $100K-$1M production deployment ke liye.

Economics aksar standalone basis par justify nahi hoti: pilots itne tightly scoped hote hain ke team aksar un par paisa khota hai. Economics tabhi kaam karti hain jab pilot significantly higher pricing par production contract mein convert hoti hai. Companies jo production-conversion mechanics ke bina pilots chalati hain — clear conversion clauses, deadline-based pricing tiers, expanded-scope upgrades — unke paas aksar high pilot-revenue aur low production-revenue hoti hai, jo structural problem hai temporary nahi. Do practical disciplines strong pilot operators ko weak se alag karti hain: pilot ke success metrics ko original agreement mein rigorously scope karo (out-of-scope work alag engagement hai, pilot extension nahi), aur contract aise structure karo ke pilot ke end par buyer ya to 30 din ke andar production mein convert kare ya preferred pricing kho de. Clause indefinite pilot extensions ko rokta hai — sab se common pilot failure mode — aur buyer ki procurement organization ko defined timeline par decision lene par force karta hai.

**RevOps stack.** Koi bhi vendor-led motion kaam karne ke liye instrumentation — CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot), sales engagement (Outreach, Salesloft), product analytics (Mixpanel, Amplitude), revenue intelligence (Gong, Chorus), forecasting (Clari, Boostup), customer success (Gainsight, Catalyst). 2026 mein, stack ki har layer ke AI-native versions emerge ho rahe hain — aur unhe integrate karne ki discipline khud meaningful competitive advantage hai. Companies jo RevOps mein under-invest karti hain sab apni motions dark mein chalati hain, har deal se individually seekhti hain deals ke patterns se nahi.

**Outcome attribution.** Technical infrastructure jo prove karne ke liye zaroori hai ke kaunse outcomes AI Worker ne produce kiye versus jo humans ne, doosre systems ne, ya coincidence se produce kiye. Outcome attribution outcome-based pricing aur value-based engagement ke liye foundational requirement hai. Companies jo outcome attribution ke bina outcome pricing ship karti hain customers ke saath chronic disputes mein end hoti hain.

**Outcome-led deals mein compensation.** Outcome-based pricing (Motion 9) aur value-based engagement (Motion 10) aik structural problem create karti hain jo traditional SaaS commission plans handle nahi kar paate. Seat-based deal mein, AE $100K ACV contract close karta hai aur sign hone ke din bookable contract value par commission kamata hai. Outcome-based deal mein, pehle din $100K bookable amount nahi hai — customer $0.50 per resolved ticket, $5 per processed claim, $50 per booked meeting pay karega. Revenue contract term ke doran aati hai, AI Worker perform karne par contingent.

2026 mein teen approaches emerge ho rahe hain, aur zyada tar in motions chalane wali companies in mein experiment karti hain:

_Projected-usage commission._ AE signing par projected annual revenue figure par commission kamata hai, aam taur par customer ke stated volume se derived (maslan, "100,000 tickets per year × $0.50 = $50K projected ACV"). Risk: AEs projected volumes inflate karte hain, aur customers ka aik hissa estimate se dramatically kam actual revenue deliver karta hai. Yeh model chalane wali companies aksar commission claw back karti hain jab actuals projection se significantly neeche gir jaayein — jo deal close hone ke mahino baad gussa conversations produce karta hai.

_Realized-revenue commission._ AE actual collected revenue par commission kamata hai, 60-90 din lag ke saath quarterly paid. AE incentives ko delivery se align karta hai lekin recruiting problem create karta hai: outcome-based deals par kaam karne wale AEs ko close karne ke mahino baad payment milti hai. Seat-based options rakhne wale sellers kahin aur seat-based path choose karenge jab tak realized-revenue plan higher rates se compensate na kare.

_Hybrid._ Zyada tar outcome-led motions chalane wali companies blend istemal karti hain — signing par commission ka aik hissa conservative volume projection par, baqi actual revenue aane par paid. Aam taur par 30% upfront aur 70% trailing common starting point hai, halanke ratio company aur seller seniority ke hisaab se vary karta hai.

Compensation ka sawaal motion ship hone se pehle rarely solve hota hai. Motion 9 ya Motion 10 chalane wali zyada tar companies ne motion mein pehle chay se barah mahine guzaare seekhte hue ke kis commission structure ne sahi seller behavior produce kiya — aur seekhte hue adjust karte rahe. Sahi approach conservative shuru karna hai (realized-revenue par heavy), accept karna ke recruiting mushkil hai, aur zyada upfront commission ki taraf migrate karna jab actual-versus-projected variance predictable ho jaaye.

## Common Motion Failures

1. **PLG without enterprise motion** — Wall hit karte ho jab organizations centralize karna chahti hain
2. **Founder-led jo handoff nahi karta** — Playbook sirf founder ke head mein, sales hire fail hoti hai
3. **Enterprise field mein VP hire bahut jaldi** — Unvalidated playbook ke saath expensive hire
4. **Pay-Per-Outcome without attribution** — Customer disputes, revenue collection quarterly fight

## AI Ne Har Motion Kya Badla

Is catalog mein motions ke ancestors pre-AI sales literature mein hain. Founder-Led Sales, Enterprise Field, Channel, PLG — koi bhi naya nahi hai. Naya woh hai jo AI _har motion ke andar_ karta hai. AI era sales motions ko replace nahi karta balke har aik ke unit economics, role definitions, aur tool stack badal deta hai.

Paanch shifts naam ke qabil hain. Saath mein, woh samjhaate hain ke 2026 mein same nominal motions 2020 mein jitni economics produce karti thin us se dramatically kyun mukhtalif economics produce karti hain.

**1. Seller ab AI-augmented hai.** RevOps stack ki har layer ka ab AI-augmented version hai: research aur prospecting (Clay, Apollo, ZoomInfo AI enrichment ke saath), outbound drafting (Outreach, Salesloft, instantly.ai), conversation intelligence (Gong, Chorus, Avoma — sab heavily AI-instrumented), forecasting (Clari, BoostUp, AI-driven deal scoring ke saath), aur contract review (Ironclad, Spotdraft, AI redlining ke saath). 2020 ki motion aur 2026 ke tool stack ke saath chalane wali sales team har rep par 2-4× activity volume produce karti hai. Matlab: SaaS sales ko pandra saal tak define karne wali SDR-heavy outbound function choti team mein compress ho rahi hai AI se augmented. AI-Augmented Outbound (Motion 6) iska sab se visible expression hai, lekin same dynamic har vendor-led motion ko reshape kar rahi hai.

**2. Compute ab COGS hai.** Traditional SaaS gross margins 75-85% thin, kyunki dominant variable cost customer support thi, infrastructure nahi. AI-native products ki substantially higher variable costs hain kyunki har query, har generation, har tool call frontier-model compute invoke karta hai jiske liye vendor pay karta hai. Early years mein AI-native gross margins aam taur par 50-70% hain, scale ke saath 65-80% tak chadh jaati hain. Yeh badalta hai ke kaunsi motions economically viable hain. Pay-Per-Outcome (Motion 9) isse structurally exposed hai: agar per resolved ticket compute cost $0.40 hai aur per resolved ticket price $0.50 hai, to unit economics kaam karti hain; agar compute cost $0.60 tak chadhe, to company customers ko product use karne ke liye pay kar rahi hai. Access ke roop mein pricing wali motions (PLG, seat pricing ke saath Enterprise Field) compute volatility se insulated hain; outcome ke roop mein pricing wali nahi.

**3. Outcome attribution ab apna discipline hai.** SaaS mein, seller access deliver karta tha aur buyer value measure karta tha. AI mein, seller seedha value deliver kar raha hai — aur us value ko seller ko measure karna padta hai, buyer ko nahi, kyunki buyer aasani se AI-produced outcomes ko human-produced ones se distinguish nahi kar sakta. Yeh aik naya sales-engineering function hai: AI Worker ko instrument karna taake audit-grade evidence produce ho ke kaunse outcomes usne produce kiye, kaunse mein assist kiya, aur kaunse human ne handle kiye. Companies jo outcome attribution ke bina outcome pricing (Motion 9) ya value-based engagements (Motion 10) ship karti hain customers ke saath chronic disputes mein end hoti hain. Yeh naya role — kabhi _AI Outcome Engineer_ ya _AI Sales Engineer_ bulaaya jaata hai — traditional sales engineering aur traditional customer success ke beech baithta hai.

**4. Buyer bhi AI-augmented hai.** Procurement organizations AI agents deploy karna shuru kar rahe hain vendor proposals evaluate karne, security questionnaires summarize karne, aur pre-screen technical reviews chalane ke liye. Sales team jo AI-augmented procurement anticipate nahi karti woh ek team se haarti hai jo karti hai — proposals likh kar jo AI agents cleanly summarize kar sakein, technical documentation structure kar ke jis se AI agents specs extract kar sakein, formats mein pricing kar ke jinhe AI agents compare kar sakein. Woh era khatam ho raha hai jab salesperson buyer ke paas sab kuch parhne ka waqt nahi hone par rely kar sakta tha. Buyer ka AI sab kuch parhta hai.

**5. Motions khud converge ho rahe hain.** PLG enterprise pipeline produce karti hai. Enterprise Field accounts kholta hai jo phir self-serve usage ke through expand hoti hain. AI-Augmented Outbound Founder-Led closing ko feed karti hai pehle deals ke liye, phir Field Sales mein transition hoti hai. Is catalog mein motion taxonomy discrete categories ke roop mein present ki gayi hai kyunki revenue teams aise plan aur staff karti hain. Lekin operation mein, zyada tar successful AI-native companies teen ya char motions simultaneously blend karti hain, jahan AI-augmentation in ke beech connective tissue ke roop mein kaam karti hai. Yeh _Common Hybrid Motions_ dynamic hai jo agla section map karta hai.

Yeh paanch shifts saath mein SaaS transition ke baad se B2B sales economics mein sab se important change produce karti hain. Jo company 2026 ki motion 2020 ki economics ke saath chalati hai woh category mistake kar rahi hai. Jo company 2026 ki motion 2026 ki economics ke saath chalati hai woh apne peers se mukhtalif game mein compete kar rahi hai.

Sales motion woh specific tareeqa hai jisse company apna product bechti hai — kaun baat shuru karta hai, deal close hone mein kitna waqt lagta hai, pricing kya hai, aur kaun actually selling karta hai.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 2 / 7** — Char sales families kaunsi hain?</summary>

**Jawab:**
1. **Buyer-led (1-4):** Buyer khud product dhundta aur khareedta hai
2. **Vendor-led (5-8):** Tumhari team deal close karti hai
3. **Outcome-led (9-10):** Buyer tab pay karta hai jab AI real result deliver kare
4. **Partner-led (11-12):** Third parties tumhara product bechte hain

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 3 / 7** — Motion kaise chunno?</summary>

**Jawab:**
Do sawaal: product per customer kitna costa hai aur deal close hone mein kitna waqt lagta hai?
- Kam price + short cycle = PLG ya Marketplace
- Medium price + medium cycle = founder ya outbound
- Bada price + lamba cycle = enterprise field ya FDE
- Measurable outcomes = pay-per-outcome

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 4 / 7** — Paanch sales assets kaunsi hain?</summary>

**Jawab:**
1. **Pipeline** — qualified opportunities ki reliable supply
2. **Velocity** — opportunity kitni jaldi closed revenue mein convert hoti hai
3. **Deal Economics** — revenue per deal × gross margin
4. **Retention** — net revenue retention
5. **Trust** — buyer ka earned confidence

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 5 / 7** — Buyer Maturity Curve ke teen stages kaunse hain?</summary>

**Jawab:**
1. **AI-Curious** — production mein AI deploy nahi kiya → Founder-Led, FDE best
2. **AI-Piloting** — experiments run kiye → Enterprise Field, AI-Outbound best
3. **AI-Native** — AI core infrastructure hai → Pay-Per-Outcome, Value-Based best

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 6 / 7** — PLG kya hai?</summary>

**Jawab:**
**Product-Led Growth** — buyer-led motion jahan product khud sell karta hai. User sign up karta hai, use karta hai, upgrade karta hai — bina salesperson ke. Kam price + short cycle ke liye best.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 7 / 7** — FDE kya hai?</summary>

**Jawab:**
**Forward Deployed Engineer** — vendor-led motion jahan engineer client ke saath baith ke custom implementation banata hai. Bada price + lamba cycle ke liye. Strategic deals ke liye.

</details>
