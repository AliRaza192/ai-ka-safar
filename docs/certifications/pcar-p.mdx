# Panaversity Certified Architect: Professional

**PCAR-P tumhe Anthropic ki CCAR-P (Claude Certified Architect – Professional) exam ki tayyari karne ke liye design kiya gaya hai.** Ye same published blueprint follow karta hai, same objectives ko same weights pe cover karta hai, lekin wahin nahi rukta.

PCAR-P us blueprint ko ek level up le jaata hai concepts ko broader, vendor-neutral context mein test karke, related topics add karke jo real-world AI work mein important hain, aur wo gaps bharta hai jo official exam ke scope se bahar hain.

PCAR-P pass karne se tumhe strong, low-cost signal milta hai ke tum official Anthropic certification attempt karne ke liye tayyar ho, aur saath hi practical AI engineering ke liye mukammal foundation bhi ban raha hai.

[Official CCAR-P exam guide parhein](/docs/certifications#official-exam-guides).

| Exam | Items | Duration | Cut score | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PCAR-P** (Panaversity) | 63 items | 120 minutes | 720 / 1,000 | Students: do free attempts, phir fee |
| **CCAR-P** (Anthropic) | 63 items* | 120 minutes | 720 / 1,000 | $175 per attempt |

\*Ye question count independent guides se aayi hai, official Anthropic exam guide se confirm nahi hui. Is page pe har price aur har doosra count Anthropic ke published guides se hai.

[Pehle se jaante ho kya chahiye? Schedule karo↓](#apna-exam-schedule-karo)

Tum pehle se aisa system design kar chuke ho jis pe kisi ne depend kiya. Tumne models choose kiye, boundaries draw ki, trade-off ke baare mein paisa dene walon ke saamne argue kiya, phir launch ke baad us cheez ke saath rahe jab wo drift kiya aur theek karna pada.

**PCAR-P us insaan ka exam hai.** Ye Architect Foundations ke mushkil sawaalon wala nahi hai. Ye architect ke kaam ka wo hissa examine karta hai jo architecture ke bahar hota hai: discovery conversation, wo decision jise tumhe us insaan ko justify karna hai jo banaya nahi tha, handover, aur uske baad ka saal.

Ghareeb ke liye ye kuch narrow aur useful prove karta hai. Kisi ne is insaan ko business problem di aur usne result ko discovery se lekar production aur iteration tak apni zimmedari mein rakha.

Anthropic ki guide doosri taraf bhi seedhi hai. Ye exam entry-level developers ke liye nahi, casual Claude-based application users ke liye nahi, aur roles ke liye nahi jo prompt writing jaise isolated tasks tak limited hon bina broader system-design responsibility ke.

Ye FDE qualification gate ke bahar hai, jo [PCAO-F](/docs/certifications/pcao-f) aur phir [PCAR-F](/docs/certifications/pcar-f) hai. PCAR-P wo hai jo tum add karte ho jab end-to-end ownership pehle se tumhara kaam hai.

## Same Blueprint, Ek Level Upar

**PCAR-P usi blueprint ko ek level up le jaata hai.** Ye **CCAR-P**, Anthropic ka Claude Certified Architect Professional, ke published blueprint pe har objective examine karta hai: same saat domains same published weights pe. Phir chalta rehta hai, kyunki courses bhi chalte hain: wohi judgment vendors ke across poochha jaata hai.

CCAR-P **63 items hai 120 minutes mein $175 ke liye**. Us blueprint ka har objective PCAR-P pe poochha jaata hai, us weight pe jo Anthropic ne publish kiya.

Jo Panaversity sitting add karti hai wo **vendor neutrality aur professional coverage** hai. Anthropic ka exam architectural judgment aik product surface ke andar test karta hai, aur uska khud blueprint bhi ye kehta hai, halanke barely: saat domain titles mein se aik Claude ka naam leta hai. PCAR-P wohi judgment profession ke tor pe poochta hai. Model-selection ka sawaal Claude-model ke sawaal ki jagah. Observability ka sawaal vendor feature ki jagah. Regulatory obligation aik company ki policy ki jagah. Tum aise customer ke saamne baithoge jo Claude chalaye ya kuch aur, aur tumhe dono mein sahi hona padega.

**CCAR-P abhi bhi wo credential hai jo bahar count karta hai.** Ye Anthropic ki hai, independently verifiable, aur buyer usse table ke across recognize karta hai. PCAR-P uska substitute nahi hai. Ye wo sitting hai jo tumhe uske liye tayyari karti hai, aur wohi judgment vendor-neutral territory mein le jaati hai jahan capstone architect actually kaam karta hai.

| Panaversity exam | Code | Anthropic twin | Rasta mein role |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Associate: Foundations](/docs/certifications/pcao-f) | PCAO-F | CCAO-F | FDE gate ke liye required pehle |
| [Architect: Foundations](/docs/certifications/pcar-f) | PCAR-F | CCAR-F | FDE gate ke liye required doosra |
| [Developer: Foundations](/docs/certifications/pcdv-f) | PCDV-F | CCDV-F | Additional technical credential |
| Architect: Professional | **PCAR-P** | CCAR-P | Capstone, pair ke baad liya jata hai |

Har Panaversity exam wohi **720 out of 1,000** cut score use karta hai jo Anthropic use karta hai. Panaversity apne exams independently administer aur proctored karta hai.

## Ye Kya Test Karta Hai

Ye saat published domains aur unke official weights hain.

| Domain | Weight |
|---|---|
| **Integration** | **19%** |
| **Solution Design & Architecture** | **17%** |
| **Evaluation, Testing & Optimization** | **16%** |
| Governance, Safety & Risk Management | 14% |
| Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle Management | 14% |
| Claude Models, Prompting & Context Engineering | 13% |
| Developer Productivity & Operational Enablement | 7% |

**Weights kisi bhi Foundations exam se flat hain.** Kuch dominate nahi karta, aur sabse bharie domain Integration hai, jo aik skill nahi hai. Ye tool aur agent configuration, authentication aur authorisation, accuracy ke against latency, observability at scale, RAG pipelines, data shape ke mutabiq retrieval strategy, aur MCP, API ya CLI, aur agent-to-agent connection ke beech choice cover karta hai.

Ab table ka neeche dekho beech ke against. Prompting aur model selection 13% hai. Developer productivity, wo domain jahan Claude Code hai, 7% hai. Governance aur stakeholder work milke 28% hai, prompting domain se do se zyada. **Ye exam nahi poochta ke tum product chala sakte ho. Ye poochta hai ke tum system ke maalik ban sakte ho un logon ke saamne jo banaya nahi tha.**

Isi liye Panaversity sitting exam ko chauda kar sakti hai bina usse distort kiye. Saat domain titles mein se sirf aik vendor ka product naam leta hai. Baaki pehle se architecture ke tor pe likhe hain.

Toh PCAR-P har objective poochta hai aur phir chalta hai. Jahan blueprint kehta hai trade-offs pe appropriate Claude models choose karo, PCAR-P tumse poochta hai ke mixed fleet pe model choice defend karo, jismein aik model tum control nahi karte shamil ho. Jahan kehta hai teams ke liye Claude tools aur environments configure karo, PCAR-P poochta hai ke tum client jo harness pehle se chalata hai wo configure karo. Lifecycle domain wo hai jahan twin iske sabse qareeb aata hai, kyunki wo domain blueprint ka khud ka admission hai ke diagram pe architecture khatam nahi hota.

## Apna Exam Schedule Karo

Seat ke do raste hain. Matching Panaversity course mein enrolled ho toh tumhara attempt free hai aur tum apne teacher ke saath arrange karte ho. Enrolled nahi ho toh attempt khareed lo aur one-on-one proctoring ke saath baitho.

Panaversity students ko **har exam pe do free attempts** milte hain. Teesra ya baqi attempts pe fee lagti hai, aur external candidates proctoring fee dete hain. Dono abhi announce nahi hui.

## Pass Karne Ke Baad

PCAR-P gate nahi hai, aur ye seedha kehna honest hai. FDE qualification gate **PCAO-F aur phir PCAR-F** hai. Wo pair pass karna tumhe Panaversity FDE Internship Program ke liye qualify karta hai, jahan Panaversity un participants ki madad karta hai jo Anthropic exams ke liye register karein. Anthropic eligible organisational account mangta hai aur koi individual sign-up route nahi deta. Khud Anthropic koi Panaversity exam nahi mangta.

Toh PCAR-P us path ke upar baithta hai, andar nahi. Tab lo jab tum pehle se solutions end to end own karte ho aur chahte ho ke wo examine ho, sirf claim na ho, aur jab tumhe agli cheez chahiye ho wo koi aur skill nahi balki aik independent evidence ho jo koi ghareeb check kar sake.

Pass ke baad do cheezein hain. Tum **CCAR-P** dene ke liye tayyar ho bina fee kharch kiye jaanne ke, aur tum professional blueprint ke against proctored result leke chalte ho, apne last project ke claim ki jagah.

Wo policies jo decision badal dein: pehle failure ke baad 14 din wait, doosre ke baad 30, teesre ke baad 90, rolling twelve months mein maximum chaar attempts, har aik full fee pe. Appointment se 24 ghante pehle tak cancel ya reschedule kar sakte ho, aur us window ke andar fee forfeit ho jaati hai. Exam shuru hone se pehle confidentiality aur non-disclosure agreement accept karna hota hai, aur decline karna session refund ke saath khatam karta hai.
