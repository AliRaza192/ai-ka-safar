# Panaversity Certified Architect: Foundations

**PCAR-F tumhe Anthropic ki CCAR-F (Claude Certified Architect – Foundations) exam ki tayyari karne ke liye design kiya gaya hai.** Ye same published blueprint follow karta hai, same objectives ko same weights pe cover karta hai, lekin wahin nahi rukta.

PCAR-F us blueprint ko ek level up le jaata hai concepts ko broader, vendor-neutral context mein test karke, related topics add karke jo real-world AI work mein important hain, aur wo gaps bharta hai jo official exam ke scope se bahar hain.

PCAR-F pass karne se tumhe strong, low-cost signal milta hai ke tum official Anthropic certification attempt karne ke liye tayyar ho, aur saath hi practical AI engineering ke liye mukammal foundation bhi ban raha hai.

[Official CCAR-F exam guide parhein](/docs/certifications#official-exam-guides).

| Exam | Items | Duration | Cut score | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PCAR-F** (Panaversity) | 60 items | 120 minutes | 720 / 1,000 | Students: do free attempts, phir fee |
| **CCAR-F** (Anthropic) | 60 items | 120 minutes | 720 / 1,000 | $125 per attempt |

[Pehle se jaante ho kya chahiye? Schedule karo↓](#apna-exam-schedule-karo)

PCAR-F FDE path pe doosra exam hai, aur wo gate band karta wala hai. Ye design layer examine karta hai: kaam agents ke beech kaise tootta hai, tool interface ko kya kehna zaroori hai ke model use sahi se use kare, MCP kahan sahi seam hai, aur context window khatam hone pe system karta kya hai.

Ye us insaan ke liye likha gaya hai jisse system design karne ko kaha jayega, operate karne ko nahi. [PCAO-F](/docs/certifications/pcao-f) poochta hai ke tum AI responsibly use aur evaluate kar sakte ho. Ye poochta hai ke tum architecture pe bharosa layak ho. Dono usi order mein lo.

Ghareeb ke liye, credential aik khaas baat kehta hai. Tumne Anthropic ke published Architect blueprint pe bana hua proctored exam diya aur 720 out of 1,000 clear kiya. Ye nahi ke tumne agent design ke baare mein parha hai. Ye ke tumhe trade-offs pe examine kiya gaya, clock ke saath, kisi ke dekhne ke saath.

## Same Blueprint, Ek Level Upar

**PCAR-F usi blueprint ko ek level up le jaata hai.** CCAR-F ka warm-up nahi, aur na hi uska knock-off: ye official blueprint poora examine karta hai, phir chalta rehta hai, kyunki courses bhi chalte hain, vendors ke across wohi judgment poochha jaata hai.

Ye Anthropic ke Architect Foundations blueprint pe har task statement poochta hai, published weights pe. Twin **CCAR-F, Claude Certified Architect – Foundations** hai: 60 items 120 minutes mein $125 USD ke liye, chaar production scenarios mein framed jo chhe ke bank se drawn hain. Wo sab PCAR-F pe examine hota hai. Phir sitting uske aage chalti hai.

Jo add hota hai wo vendor neutrality hai. CCAR-F architectural judgment aik vendor ke product surface ke andar test karta hai: Agent SDK ke hooks, `CLAUDE.md` hierarchy, plan mode, built-in tools. Wo asli hain aur jaanne layak hain. Wo ek implementation bhi hain us sawaal ki jo customer Claude chalaye ya na chalaye mojood rehta hai.

**Aik graduate aise customer ke saamne khada hota hai jo Claude chalaye ya kuch aur, aur dono mein sahi hona padta hai.** Yahi poori wajah hai ke Panaversity sitting aage jaati hai.

Wo sab CCAR-F ko chhota exam nahi banate. **Ye wo credential hai jo market recognize karta hai, Anthropic ne issue kiya hai aur koi bhi check kar sakta hai.** PCAR-F uske neeche baithne aur usse khada rakhne ke liye bana hai: wohi blueprint, pehle liya hua, aur saath mein vendor-neutral half jo customer tumse poochega pehle ke poochhe ke tumne kaun sa SDK use kiya.

**Teen domains iska do-teen hissa carry karte hain.** Agentic Architecture & Orchestration 27 percent, Claude Code Configuration & Workflows 20, aur Prompt Engineering & Structured Output 20 milke exam ka 67 percent banate hain. Table ko ulti taraf parho aur teen domains teen courses pe depend karte hain: Claude Agent SDK, Claude Code for Teams, aur Structured Extraction Pipelines. Ye percentages se bhi sharp study hint hai.

Dekho kya top pe hai. Orchestration, prompting nahi.

Sabse bharie domain loops ke baare mein hai jo right signal pe terminate karte hain, coordinators jo kaam bina coverage khoye decompose karte hain, subagents jo tumhari assumed context inherit nahi karte, aur sessions jo resume aur fork ho sakte hain. Ye credential ka thesis hai, number ke tor pe bola gaya.

Aur kuch us se door nahi hai. Spread 15 se 27 percent tak hai, toh koi domain skip nahi kar sakte aur koi domain tumhe le nahi jayega. Scenario structure isse sharp karta hai: exam chhe ke bank se chaar scenarios draw karta hai, toh aik kamzor domain tumhare particular draw pe over-represented ho sakta hai aur kisi doosre ke pe under-represented.

### Per-domain minimum nahi hai

Tumhara CCAR-F score report domain-wise percent-correct dikhata hai, lekin guide clear hai ke wo section percentages pass ya fail decide karne ke liye use nahi hote. Natija tumhara total scaled score hai 720 cut score ke against. Kamzor domain akele fail nahi kar sakta. Phir bhi wo domain hai jo doosre scenario draw pe sabse zyada fail karne wala hai, aur isi liye strong total jo aik patli area chhupa de wo profile hai jis pe ghabrana chahiye.

Bank ke chhe production contexts published hain, aur wo exam ki sabse achi description hain jo koi de sakta hai:

- **Customer Support Resolution Agent.** Custom MCP tools pe high-ambiguity support agent, aur jaanna ke kab escalate karna hai.
- **Code Generation with Claude Code.** Team ke workflow ke andar generation, refactoring, debugging, aur documentation.
- **Multi-Agent Research System.** Coordinator jo search, analysis, synthesis, aur reporting subagents ko delegate karta hai.
- **Developer Productivity with Claude.** Built-in tools aur MCP servers ke saath unfamiliar codebases explore karna.
- **Claude Code for Continuous Integration.** Automated review aur test generation, false positives dushman ke roop mein.
- **Structured Data Extraction.** Unstructured documents se extraction, JSON schemas ke against validate kiya hua.

![Do exams, do bohot alag shapes. CCDV-F top-heavy hai, Applications aur Integration 33.1 percent pe, jabke CCAR-F zyada evenly distributed hai 15 se 27 percent tak.](/img/03-domain-weights.webp) *Shape numbers se zyada matter karta hai. CCAR-F ke paas koi dominant domain nahi jis pe depend kiya jaaye, aur ye us Developer exam ke ulta hai jo bagal mein hai.*

Guide ka jawab do, product ka nahi

Exam fixed snapshot ke against likha gaya hai aur products move kar chuke hain. Chaar jagah matter karti hain. Do pe ye kitaab tumhe aaj ka behavior sikhata hai, jo tumhare code mein sahi hai aur answer sheet pe galat.

| Guide kehta hai | Product karta hai | Exam pe |
|---|---|---|
| `allowedTools` mein `"Task"` shamil hona chahiye subagents spawn karne ke liye | SDK metadata spawning `Agent` block kehta hai | Jawab `Task` |
| Skill frontmatter mein `allowed-tools` tool access **restrict** karta hai | Ye invoking turn ke liye pre-approve karta hai: [Claude Code for Teams](/docs/claude-code-teams-crash-course), Concept 10 | Jawab **restricts** |
| **PostToolUse** kehta hai, jabke pre-call blocking describe karta hai | `PreToolUse` blocking hook hai: [Claude Agent SDK](/docs/claude-agent-sdk-crash-course), Concept 11 | Jawab **described behaviour** |
| JSON schema ke saath `tool_use` reliable structured-output path hai | `output_config.format` wo hai jo tum aaj likhte ho | Jawab `tool_use` |

Guide ke apne sample distractors mein teen artifacts hain jo kisi product mein mojood nahi hain: commands array wala `.claude/config.json`, `CLAUDE_HEADLESS=true` variable, aur `--batch` flag.

Har weight upar PCAR-F pe honor hota hai. Jo Panaversity sitting add karti hai wo har domain ka doosra half hai, bina vendor framing ke poochha hua:

- **Agentic Architecture & Orchestration.** Twin poochta hai ke subagent Claude Agent SDK mein apna context kaise receive karta hai, diye hue ke wo coordinator ka history inherit nahi karta. PCAR-F ye poochta hai, phir poochta hai ke kisi bhi coordinator ko kisi bhi worker ko kaam dene se pehle kya true hona chahiye ke handoff safe ho.
- **Claude Code Configuration & Workflows.** Twin poochta hai ke `CLAUDE.md` scoping aur path-specific rules resolve kaise hote hain. PCAR-F ye poochta hai, phir poochta hai ke agent ke standing instructions kahan aate hain jab harness Claude Code nahi hai, aur do sets disagree karein toh kya tootta hai.
- **Context Management & Reliability.** Twin poochta hai ke scenario ke product surface ke andar kab human ko escalate karna hai. PCAR-F ye poochta hai, phir poochta hai ke escalation jo kabhi nahi hua uske liye kaun accountable hai, aur ye wo sawaal hai jo tumhara customer kisi vendor se pehle poochta hai.

Blueprint ke aath courses mein se koi teeno sawaalon ko exam depth pe jawab nahi deta. Doosri study table ke saat courses dete hain. Har row mein pehle entry baaki se pehle parho.

Un courses mein se do, aur aik blueprint course, wo pattern sikhate hain jo key reward nahi karta. Give Your AI Agent a Nervous System kaam ko subscription se bhi aur parent se bhi fan-out karta hai; exam ka routing item ka jawab hub-and-spoke hai coordinator ke through.

## Pass Karne Ke Baad

PCAR-F pass karna Panaversity qualification stage complete karta hai. PCAO-F pehle se piche, gate band ho gaya.

**PCAO-F → PCAR-F → FDE Internship Program → CCAO-F → CCAR-F, agar tum baithna chaho**

Internship us sequence ka practical part hai. Anthropic ke exams [Anthropic Partner Academy](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/) ke through register hote hain aur eligible organisational account chahiye; personal addresses qubool nahi hote aur koi individual sign-up route nahi hai. Panaversity qualifying internship participants ki registration mein madad karta hai, aur Anthropic pair baithna optional hai.

Anthropic ko PCAR-F, ya PCAO-F, ya koi aur Panaversity exam zaroori nahi. Gate Panaversity ki apni hai, do wajahon se rakhi gayi. Anthropic attempt pe fee lagti hai, aur fail hone pe 14, 30, aur 90 din lagte hain agle tak. Aur Panaversity apna naam tumhare naam ke peeche laga raha hai, aur ye daawa hai jise use khada karna chahiye.

Plan karne se pehle jaanne layak aik baat: **CCAR-F Claude Partner Network tier eligibility mein count hota hai aur CCAO-F nahi.** Developer aur Architect Professional credentials bhi count karte hain. Agar tier standing tumhare organization ke liye matter karta hai, ye recommended pair ka wo half hai jo usmein aata hai.

[PCDV-F](/docs/certifications/pcdv-f) aur [PCAR-P](/docs/certifications/pcar-p) baad mein aate hain, un logon ke liye jo chahein. Dono internship gate ka part nahi hain.
