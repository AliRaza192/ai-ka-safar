# Panaversity Certified Associate: Foundations

**PCAO-F tumhe Anthropic ki CCAO-F (Claude Certified Associate – Foundations) exam ki tayyari karne ke liye design kiya gaya hai.** Ye same published blueprint follow karta hai, same objectives ko same weights pe cover karta hai, lekin wahin nahi rukta.

PCAO-F us blueprint ko ek level up le jaata hai concepts ko broader, vendor-neutral context mein test karke, related topics add karke jo real-world AI work mein important hain, aur wo gaps bharta hai jo official exam ke scope se bahar hain.

PCAO-F pass karne se tumhe strong, low-cost signal milta hai ke tum official Anthropic certification attempt karne ke liye tayyar ho, aur saath hi practical AI engineering ke liye mukammal foundation bhi ban raha hai.

[Official CCAO-F exam guide parhein](/docs/certifications#official-exam-guides).

| Exam | Items | Duration | Cut score | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PCAO-F** (Panaversity) | 60 items | 120 minutes | 720 / 1,000 | Students: do free attempts, phir fee |
| **CCAO-F** (Anthropic) | 60 items | 120 minutes | 720 / 1,000 | $99 per attempt |

[Pehle se jaante ho kya chahiye? Schedule karo↓](#schedule-your-exam)

Ye Panaversity FDE path pe pehla exam hai, aur ye aik layer examine karta hai: judgment. AI use karna, jo wo wapas deta hai evaluate karna, aur ye govern karna ke use kahan operate karne diya ja sakta hai. Build karna nahi.

Ye us insaan ke liye likha gaya hai jo pehle se AI ke saath roz kaam karta hai bina integration code likhe. Operations, marketing, project management, communications, teaching, consulting, analysis.

Ye do logon ko cover karta hai, aik nahi: internal staffer jo AI-enabled workflow ko honest rakhta hai, aur external consultant jo redesign karne ke liye laya gaya hai.

**Ghareeb ke liye ye kya prove karta hai wo narrow aur useful hai.** Jo aapka kaam kabhi nahi dekha wo verify kar sakta hai ke aik independent proctored exam ne aapko paaya ke aap acha output plausible se alag kar sakte ho, use aage badhne se pehle validate kar sakte ho, aur wo point jaante ho jahan insaan ko dekhna zaroori hai.

## Same Blueprint, Ek Level Upar

**PCAO-F usi blueprint ko ek level up le jaata hai.** Ye Anthropic ke published CCAO-F blueprint pe har objective examine karta hai, published weights pe. Phir chalta rehta hai, kyunki courses bhi chalte hain: wohi judgment vendors ke across poochha jaata hai. Iski study guide Claude aur ChatGPT ko bagal mein rakhti hai, aur isse pass karna matlab official blueprint mein kuch nahi jo tumhe pehle se examine na kiya gaya ho.

Do compete nahi kar rahe, aur PCAO-F kisi ka replacement nahi hai. **CCAO-F wo credential hai jo employer Anthropic ke saath verify kar sakta hai, aur wohi laayak hai leke jaane ke liye.** PCAO-F uske neeche khada hone ke liye bana hai: tumhe us sitting ke liye tayyar le jana, aur tumhe aise coverage ke saath chhodna jo vendor exam test karne ki zaroorat mehsoos nahi karta.

Jo extra reach milti hai wo **vendor neutrality aur professional coverage** hai.

CCAO-F yeh judgment aik company ke product surface ke andar test karta hai, usi company ki vocabulary mein: Projects, artifacts, research mode, connectors, Haiku aur Sonnet aur Opus.

PCAO-F wohi sawaal product surface se upar uthata hai. Architecture ka sawaal Claude Code ke sawaal ki jagah. Evaluation discipline product feature ki jagah jo use implement karta hai. Governance obligation jo tum regulated client ke paas leke jaate ho, us vendor ke usage policy ki jagah.

Ye preference nahi hai. Ye job hai. Tum aise customer ke saamne baithoge jo Claude chalata hai, ya kuch aur chalata hai, ya teeno ek saath, aur tumhe dono mein sahi hona padega.

| | PCAO-F | CCAO-F |
|---|---|---|
| **Chalata hai** | Panaversity, independently administer aur proctored | Anthropic, Pearson VUE ke through |
| **Blueprint** | Har CCAO-F objective published weights pe, phir product surface se upar wala level | Saat published domains |
| **Cut score** | 720 out of 1,000 | 720 out of 1,000 |
| **Cost** | Panaversity students ke liye do free attempts; retake aur external proctoring fees abhi announce nahi hui | $99 list price |
| **Kiske liye** | Panaversity ka readiness gate, aur FDE Internship Program ki qualification | Credential jo employer Anthropic ke saath verify kar sakta hai |

Anthropic ko PCAO-F zaroori nahi. Gate Panaversity ki hai, aur do wajahon se hai.

Anthropic attempt pe fee lagti hai, aur fail hone pe time bhi lagta hai. Waits hain: pehle failure ke baad 14 din, doosre ke baad 30, aur teesre ke baad 90.

Same blueprint ke against proctored exam, jo Panaversity student ke liye pehle do attempts muft hai, bohot sasta tareeqa hai jaanne ka ke tum kahan khade ho.

Doosri wajah credential khud hai. Panaversity apna naam aur internship un logon ke peeche lagata hai jo pass karte hain, toh standard ko kuch mean karna chahiye.

### Per-domain minimum nahi hai

Tumhara CCAO-F score report domain-wise percent-correct dikhata hai, lekin guide clear hai ke wo section percentages pass ya fail decide karne ke liye use nahi hote. Natija tumhara total scaled score hai 720 cut score ke against. Kamzor domain akele fail nahi kar sakta. Phir bhi wo domain hai jo doosre question draw pe sabse zyada fail karne wala hai, aur isi liye strong total jo aik patli area chhupa de wo profile hai jis pe ghabrana chahiye.

Panaversity sitting saate poochti hain, phir wahan chalti hai jahan vendor-specific version ruk jaata.

**Product aur model selection** teen model names ki class nahi hota, decision ki class ban jaati hai: speed khareedte waqt kya trade karte ho, long context actually kitna cost karta hai, session stretch karne ki jagah kab restart karna hai.

**Configuration aur knowledge management** aam taur pe instruction aur knowledge layer ban jaati hai, kisi aik product ke Projects panel ki nahi.

**Governance** obligation ban jaati hai jo tum regulated client ke paas leke jaate ho, aik vendor ke acceptable-use page ki nahi.

Aur professional layer sab ke upar baithi hai: tumhe pay karne wale ko samjhana ke system kya nahi karega, pehle ke wo khud pata chale.

## Pass Karne Ke Baad

**Agla [PCAR-F](/docs/certifications/pcar-f) hai, Architect Foundations exam.** Order deliberate hai. PCAO-F poochta hai ke tum AI responsibly use aur evaluate kar sakte ho. PCAR-F poochta hai ke tum wo system design kar sakte ho jismein ye chalta hai. Pehle judgment, phir architecture.

Dono pass karna Panaversity qualification stage complete karta hai, aur yahi tumhe **FDE Internship Program** ke liye qualify karta hai. [FTE Startup Founders Program](/docs/founders-program) mein project work PCAO-F pass karte hi shuru hota hai; tum [interest register](/docs/founders-program#how-to-enroll) kar sakte ho jaise hi pass ho jao.

Internship mechanically bhi important hai aur practically bhi. Anthropic registration ke liye eligible organisational account chahiye aur abhi koi individual sign-up route nahi hai; Panaversity qualifying internship participants ki registration mein madad karta hai.

Phir, agar tum baithna chaho, official pair usi order mein: CCAO-F, phir CCAR-F.

Aik honest limit. PCAO-F readiness credential aur access gate hai. Ye wo cheez nahi jo employer Anthropic ke registry mein dhoondega.

Wo CCAO-F hai. Ye exam wahan pahunchne ka tareeqa hai bina attempt kharch kiye jaanne ke ke tum kahan khade ho.
