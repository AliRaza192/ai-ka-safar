# AI Workers

Yeh section [*Agent Factory Thesis*](/docs/thesis) ko amal mein laata hai. Thesis AI-native company ki architecture define karti hai. Yeh catalogs use concrete banate hain.

**Agar tumne abhi Thesis nahi parhi — pehle wahan jao.** Yeh catalogs usi frame ko assume karte hain.

---

## Catalogs

Har catalog ek sawaal ka jawab deta hai jo ek AI-native company ke andar founder ya operator ko poochna hota hai:

| Catalog | Sawaal | Kab Parhein |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| **[Worker Catalog](/docs/worker-catalog)** | *Kya* AI Workers business verticals mein exist karte hain: woh kya karte hain, kya padhte hain, kya likhte hain, kaun supervise karta hai | Jab decide kar rahe ho kya banana hai, ya evaluate kar rahe ho ke kaunse Workers deploy karein |
| **Sales Catalog** | *Kaise* ek AI-native company deals close karti hai | Jab tum founder ya revenue leader ho aur GTM motion design kar rahe ho |
| **Finance Catalog** | *Kaise* price karo, forecast karo, aur unit economics model karo jab costs usage ke saath scale hon | Jab tum founder ya finance lead ho aur pricing, gross margin, runway figure out kar rahe ho |
| **Marketing Catalog** | *Kaise* AI-native products ki demand build karo | Jab tum demand engine bana rahe ho |

---

## Yeh Sab Milke Kaise Kaam Karte Hain

**Worker Catalog** = **product side**: AI-native company kya manufacture karti hai aur uske customers kya deploy karte hain.

**Sales, Marketing, Finance** = **company side**: us company ke founders kaise bechte hain, price karte hain, aur market karte hain.

## Flashcards — Yaad Karo

Yeh section ke key concepts yaad karne mein madad karta hai. Har card pe pehle khud jawab socho, phir reveal karo.

<details>
<summary>**Card 1 / 5** — Yeh catalogs kis framework ko amal mein laate hain?</summary>

**Jawab:**
Yeh catalogs *Agent Factory Thesis* ko amal mein laate hain. Thesis AI-native company ki architecture define karti hai — catalogs use concrete banate hain.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 2 / 5** — Char catalogs kaunse hain aur kaunsa sawaal jawab dete hain?</summary>

**Jawab:**
1. **Worker Catalog** — *Kya* AI Workers exist karte hain (verticals, roles, tasks)
2. **Sales Catalog** — *Kaise* AI-native company deals close karti hai
3. **Finance Catalog** — *Kaise* price karo, forecast karo, unit economics model karo
4. **Marketing Catalog** — *Kaise* AI-native products ki demand build karo

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 3 / 5** — Product side aur company side mein kya farq hai?</summary>

**Jawab:**
**Worker Catalog** = product side — AI-native company kya manufacture karti hai aur customers kya deploy karte hain.
**Sales, Marketing, Finance** = company side — us company ke founders kaise bechte hain, price karte hain, aur market karte hain.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 4 / 5** — Kya catalogs standalone parh sakte hain?</summary>

**Jawab:**
Haan, chahein catalog standalone parh sakte ho. Lekin pehle **Thesis** parhna behtar hai kyunki catalogs uska frame assume karte hain. Worker Catalog sabse comprehensive entry point hai.

</details>

<details>
<summary>**Card 5 / 5** — Worker Catalog mein kya details milti hain?</summary>

**Jawab:**
Har vertical mein: kaunse Workers hain, woh kya karte hain, kya padhte likhte hain, kaun supervise karta hai. Plus failure modes aur verification checkpoints. Zyada verticals waqt ke saath aate rahenge.

</details>
