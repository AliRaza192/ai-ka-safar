# Digital FTE Founders & FDE Program

**Certified AI engineers se startup founders aur Service Forward Deployed Engineers tak.**

Yeh 24-week ka program hai un engineers ke liye jo [PCAO-F](/docs/certifications/pcao-f) — Panaversity ka Associate Foundations exam — pass kar chuke hain. Yeh ek kaam karta hua **Digital FTE** ke saath khatam hota hai jo asal customer ke liye banaya jaata hai. Digital FTE aik AI worker hai jo aik defined business role perform karne ke liye design kiya gaya hai, saaf zimmedariyan, measurable results, aur munasib human oversight ke saath. FTE ka matlab hai full-time equivalent — aik full-time employee ka kaam. Program do roles ki taraf le jaata hai. Aik startup founder. Doosra **Forward Deployed Engineer**, ya **FDE**. FDE woh engineer hai jo customer ke business ke andar kaam karta hai taake wahan product kaam kare.

### Seedhi zubaan mein

PCAO-F pass karo aur tum usi din eligible ho jaate ho. Enrollment khud ba khud hai aur koi program fee nahi hai. Pass karne ke baad tum agla Stage 2 **cycle** join karte ho. Cycle aik stage ka scheduled run hai, aur naya cycle har 12 hafton mein shuru hota hai. Pehla cycle February 2027 mein shuru hota hai.

Program ke chaar stages hain. Stage 1 PCAO-F pass karna hai. Stage 2 aur Stage 3 24-week core hain, har aik 12 hafton ka. Stage 4 graduation ke baad aata hai aur iska koi end date nahi.

Stage 2 sab ke liye common foundation hai. Tum PCAR-F pass karte ho — Panaversity ka Architect Foundations exam; apna pehla Digital FTE pilot banate ho aur shared Digital FTE tools mein contribute karte ho; aur candidate verticals parhte waqt kam se kam das mumkin customers se interview karte ho. Entry par apna preferred track batate ho, aur track Stage 2 ke aakhir mein confirm hota hai.

Stage 3 mein tumhari team aik **vertical** ke liye aik Digital FTE banati hai, aik asal customer ke saath. Vertical aik industry ya aik business function hai, jaise banking ya customer support. Founders shared tools banate hain, phir apna product aur company. Wo product aur startup programs ke liye application package le kar jaate hain. **Service FDEs** asal customers ke liye Digital FTEs deploy karte hain, documented asal deployment le kar jaate hain, deployment portfolio banate hain, aur **placement** ki taraf barhte hain. Placement ka matlab hai Panaversity ke client organizations mein aik naukri.

### Quick glossary

- **Digital FTE**: aik AI worker jo aik defined business role perform karta hai, saaf zimmedariyan, measured results, aur human oversight ke saath. FTE ka matlab hai full-time equivalent.
- **Forward Deployed Engineer**, ya **FDE**: woh engineer jo customer ke business ke andar kaam karta hai taake wahan product kaam kare.
- **Service FDEs**: is program ke FDEs. Wo asal customers ke liye Digital FTEs deploy karte hain.
- **vertical**: aik industry ya aik business function, jaise banking ya customer support.
- **cycle**: aik stage ka scheduled run. Naya cycle har 12 hafton mein shuru hota hai.
- **stage-gate review**: stage ke aakhir mein review. Iska natija Pass ya Revise hota hai.
- **pilot**: naye system ka pehla limited run, asal users ke saath, kareebi supervision mein.
- **placement**: graduation ke baad Panaversity ke client organizations mein aik naukri.
- **Zia Developer AI**: Claude Code aur OpenCode ke liye plugin jo business problem ko spec aur working agent mein badalta hai.
- **KSoR**: open-source Knowledge System of Record SDK jo professions, industries, aur organizations ke liye governed knowledge records banane ke liye use hota hai. Vertical KSoRs yeh governed knowledge AI agents ko MCP ke zariye expose kar sakte hain.
- **Vertical Digital FTE Builders**: specific industries aur business functions ke liye Digital FTEs banane ke liye reusable systems.
- **credits**: wo free cloud ya AI usage jo companies startups ko deti hain.
- **equity**: company mein ownership ka aik share.
- **accelerator**: chhota program jo startup ko paisa aur coaching deta hai, aam tor par company ke aik share ke badle.

## Executive Summary: Certification se Startup Founder ya Service FDE tak

Panaversity ke pehle students [PCAO-F](/docs/certifications/pcao-f) — Associate Foundations exam — 20 September 2026 se dete hain. Is exam ko pass karna safar ka aakhir nahi hai. Yeh tumhare professional development ke agle stage ka darwaza kholta hai.

Jis din tum PCAO-F pass karte ho, tum program ke liye eligible ho jaate ho aur agle Stage 2 cycle mein khud ba khud enroll ho jaate ho. Stage 2 sab ke liye common foundation hai. Entry par apna preferred path batate ho, aur track Stage 2 ke aakhir mein confirm hota hai — tumhare kaam, customer discovery, aur company banane ki intent ke basis pe.

**Track 1, Formidable Startup Founders.** Yeh track un students ke liye hai jo tools, frameworks, platforms, aur companies banana chahte hain jo Digital FTE economy ko power denge. Tum ek asal startup banane aur US-based company incorporate karne ki taraf kaam karoge. Leading startup incubators aur accelerators — jaise Y Combinator, a16z Speedrun, Sequoia Arc, aur doosre — ke liye apply karne ki tayyari karoge. Mukammal tafseel: neeche [Track 1](#track-1-formidable-startup-founders).

**Track 2, Service FDEs.** Yeh track un students ke liye hai jo Forward Deployed Engineer banna chahte hain jo seedha customers ke saath kaam kar sake. Tum seekhoge ke Founder teams ne jo tools aur frameworks banaye hain unhein kaise use karein:

- Customer ke business problem ko samajhna.
- Sahi Digital FTE design karna.
- Use existing systems ke saath customize aur integrate karna.
- Customer ki organization ke andar deploy karna.
- Real world mein use improve aur operate karna.

Mukammal tafseel: neeche [Track 2](#track-2-service-forward-deployed-engineers).

Seedhi zubaan mein: Startup Founders tools banate hain. Service FDEs un tools ka use karke customers ke liye Digital FTEs banate hain. Humein dono chahiye.

### Common Foundation: Tumhari Pehli Teen Priorities

Stage 2 ke dauran, har koi teen cheezon par aik saath kaam karta hai.

1. **Architect Foundations exam ki tayyari karo aur pass karo.** Tum Associate-level knowledge se aage barhoge aur production-grade agentic systems design karne ke liye zaroori architecture skills develop karna shuru karoge. Exam aur study guide: [PCAR-F](/docs/certifications/pcar-f).
2. **Apna pehla Digital FTE pilot banao aur shared tools mein contribute karo.** Tum jo seekha use apply karoge working FTE pilot bana kar — [First Pilot](/docs/founders-program/first-pilot), GIAIC/PIAIC/Panaversity ke liye aik AI support worker. Wahan se, tum shared Digital FTE tools aur frameworks mein contribute karna shuru karoge.
3. **Asal customers se baat karo aur mumkin verticals parho.** Har team Stage 2 mein kam se kam teen customer interviews mukammal karti hai. Jo tum seekhte ho woh product, customer problem, aur Stage 3 mein pursue karne wale vertical ko shape karta hai.

### Common Foundation ke baad, Tumhara Track Confirm hota hai

**Founder track.** Tum reusable Digital FTE tools aur frameworks develop karna shuru karoge. Pehle major projects mein se aik **Zia Developer AI** hoga. Zia Developer AI Digital FTEs banane ke liye aik AI system hai. Requirements: [Zia Developer AI Requirements](/docs/founders-program/zia-developer-ai-requirements). Wahan se, Founder teams is ki taraf kaam karengi:

Build Tools → Build Products → Incorporate a Startup → Acquire Customers → Apply to World-Class Incubators

**Service FDE track.** Tum Founder teams ke tools ka use karke asal customer problems solve karne par barhoge. Tumhara progression yeh hoga:

Understand the Business → Design the FTE → Configure and Integrate → Deploy for a Real Customer → Document the Deployment → Build a Deployment Portfolio → Move Toward Client Placement

Maqsad yeh hai ke aise FDEs develop kiye jayein jo aik organization mein chal kar ja sakein, samajh sakein ke woh kaise kaam karti hai, aise Digital FTEs deploy kar sakein jo measurable business value banayein, aur asal real-world deployments ki kamyabi ka evidence banayein.

### Aik Ecosystem, Do Career Paths

PCAO-F pass → Agla Stage 2 Cycle join → PCAR-F pass + Pehla Digital FTE Pilot banao + Shared Tools mein contribute → Kam se kam 3 Customers se Interview → Track Confirm

Wahan se:

**Founder → Reusable tools aur products banao → Vertical Digital FTE banao → Company form → Customers acquire → Startup-program applications taiyar karo**

**Service FDE → Asal customers ke saath kaam → Digital FTEs integrate aur deploy → Documented deployment mukammal → Deployment portfolio banao → Client placement ki taraf barho**

Hum students ko sirf certifications pass karne ki tayyari nahi kar rahe. Hum aik group ko Digital FTE platforms of the future banane ki tayyari kar rahe hain. Hum doosre group ko un platforms ko companies mein le jaane aur kaam lagane ki tayyari kar rahe hain.

## Purpose

Digital FTE Founders & FDE Program Panaversity FDE Internship Program ka hissa hai. Yeh students ko AI tools, frameworks, aur industry-specific AI workers banane ki tayyari deta hai. Phir yeh unhein madad karta hai ke is capability ko aise startups mein badlein jo grow kar sakein, ya customers ke liye professional deployment services mein.

Students [PCAO-F](/docs/certifications/pcao-f) — Panaversity ka Associate Foundations exam — pass karne ke usi din eligible ho jaate hain aur agle Stage 2 cycle join karte hain. Wo [PCAR-F](/docs/certifications/pcar-f) — Panaversity ka Architect Foundations exam — pass karne ke baad internship mein continue karte hain. Core program 24 hafton ka chalta hai. Har participant asal Digital FTE tools aur frameworks banane mein madad karta hai. Har aik aik working vertical Digital FTE ke saath mukammal karta hai, customers aur leading startup programs ko present karne ke liye tayyar.

## Two Kinds of Engineers

Program do tarah ke engineers train karta hai. Dono Panaversity ke Claude-focused certifications rakhte hain aur Anthropic ki official exams ke liye tayyar hote hain. Dono PCAO-F pass karne ke baad aik hi Stage 2 common foundation se shuru karte hain. Students entry par preferred track batate hain, aur track assignment Stage 2 ke aakhir mein confirm hoti hai.

### Track 1: Formidable Startup Founders

Yeh track aise engineers develop karta hai jo qeemti problems identify kar sakein, aise products banayein jo customers choose karein, aur aise companies shuru karein jo grow karein.

Founders aise tools aur frameworks banate aur improve karte hain jo poora ecosystem use karta hai, jaise:

- **Zia Developer AI:** Claude Code aur OpenCode ke liye plugin. Yeh plain language mein likhi hui business problem ko architecture decision, spec, aur deploy ke liye tayyar working agent mein badalta hai. Overview: [Zia Developer AI](/docs/ecosystem/zia-developer-ai)
- **KSoR**, Knowledge System of Record SDK: professions, industries, aur organizations ke liye governed knowledge records banane ka mojooda open-source foundation. Founder teams KSoR use aur contribute kar sakti hain knowledge platform scratch se dobara banane ke bajaye. Vertical KSoRs approved domain knowledge AI agents ko MCP ke zariye expose karte hain.
- **Vertical Digital FTE Builders:** specific industries aur business functions ke liye Digital FTEs banane ke liye reusable systems.

[Agent Factory System of Record](/docs/ecosystem/system-of-record) MCP pe pehle se live hai aur shared Agent Factory method supply karta hai. KSoR governed Vertical Systems of Record banane ke liye reusable foundation deta hai. Zia Developer AI in governed sources ko consume karke Digital FTEs specify, build, test, aur hand over karta hai. Is liye yeh projects complementary layers banate hain, ek doosre ko duplicate karne ke bajaye.

Founders engineering ko customer discovery, product development, pricing, distribution, aur fundraising ke saath jodna seekhte hain. Customer discovery ka matlab hai mumkin customers se baat karna unki needs samajhne ke liye pehle build karne se. Distribution ka matlab hai product apne customers tak kaise pahunchta hai. Fundraising ka matlab hai investors se paisa uthana.

**Maqsad yeh hai ke aik aisa product banaya jaye jo customers ko chahiye aur aik aisi company jo use scale pe deliver kar sake.**

[First Pilot](/docs/founders-program/first-pilot) page pehla worker describe karta hai jo har team banati hai — GIAIC/PIAIC/Panaversity AI Support worker. [Zia Developer AI Requirements](/docs/founders-program/zia-developer-ai-requirements) us builder ko describe karti hain jo founder teams agla banati hain. Pilot us builder ka pehla customer worker hai. Dono pages is section mein published hain.

### Track 2: Service Forward Deployed Engineers

Yeh track **Service FDEs** develop karta hai — Forward Deployed Engineers jo seedha customers ke saath kaam karke Digital FTEs implement karte hain.

Founder teams ke banaye ya improve kiye shared tools aur frameworks ka use karke, Service FDEs yeh seekhte hain:

- Customer workflows samajhna aur AI workers ke liye munasib opportunities dhoondhna.
- Tools ko specific business requirements ke liye configure aur extend karna.
- Digital FTEs ko customer systems, knowledge, aur communication channels se jodna.
- Reliability evaluate karna, human oversight setup karna, aur working solutions deploy karna.
- Business results measure karna aur deployment ke baad performance improve karna.

**Maqsad yeh hai ke aise engineers develop kiye jayein jo customer problem ko successful production deployment tak le ja sakein.**

Do tracks aik doosre ke saath kaam karte hain. Founders reusable products dete hain. Service FDEs implementation experience aur customer feedback laate hain jo un products ko improve karte hain. Graduation ke baad, Service FDEs Panaversity ke client organizations ke saath placement ki taraf continue karte hain.

## Program Structure

- **Duration:** Stage 2 ke shuru se 24 haftay. Stage 2 12 haftay chalta hai aur PCAR-F exam ke saath khatam hota hai. Stage 3 12 haftay chalta hai aur graduation review ke saath khatam hota hai. Stage 4 open-ended hai, graduation ke baad continue karta hai, aur 24 hafton mein count nahi hota. Cycle shuru hone se pehle ka waiting time bhi count nahi hota.
- **Entry:** Students PCAO-F pass karne ke usi din eligible ho jaate hain aur agle scheduled Stage 2 cycle join karte hain. Cycles har 12 hafton mein shuru hoti hain. Pehla cycle February 2027 mein shuru hota hai.
- **Team size:** Founder teams mein do se teen engineers hote hain. Har founder team do Service FDEs ke saath Stage 3 ke liye pair ki jaati hai.
- **Track selection:** Students entry par preferred track batate hain. Track assignment Stage 2 ke aakhir mein confirm hoti hai, har student ke Stage 2 work, customer interviews, aur company banane ki intent ke basis pe.
- **Customer access:** Pilot naye system ka pehla limited run hai, asal users ke saath, kareebi supervision mein. Panaversity apne client network se pilot customers introduce karwata hai, aur teams khud bhi mumkin customers se contact karti hain. Har team ke paas Stage 3 shuru hone se pehle kam se kam aik mumkin customer identify hona chahiye.
- **Reviews:** Har team mentor ke saath aik haftawar progress review karti hai. Har stage aik **stage-gate review** ke saath khatam hota hai. Yeh Panaversity instructors aur industry mentors ka review hai, aur iska natija Pass ya Revise hota hai. Dekhein [Stage Gates and Assessment](#stage-gates-and-assessment).
- **Format:** Hybrid, yaani aik hissa in person aur aik hissa online. Live weekend sessions Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Faisalabad, aur Peshawar mein hote hain, aur baaki students ke liye online. Hafte mein taqreeban 20 ghante project work ka expected karein.
- **Costs:** Koi program fee nahi. Panaversity program work ke liye shared development infrastructure faraham karta hai: hosting, databases, aur har team ke liye Claude API usage allowance, har cycle ke liye set. Students apna computer aur internet connection khud lagate hain, aur official exams lene par Anthropic ki exam fees khud bharte hain. Startup **credits** wo free cloud ya AI usage hain jo companies startups ko deti hain. Jab team credits jeetati hai, woh Panaversity ke diye hue mein izafa karti hain, lekin program kabhi un par depend nahi karta.

## The Path

### Stage 1: Entry

[PCAO-F](/docs/certifications/pcao-f) pass karo — Panaversity ka Associate Foundations exam. Students is exam ko pass karne ke usi din is program ke liye eligible ho jaate hain aur agle Stage 2 cycle join karte hain.

Panaversity program participants ki Anthropic ki official exams ke registration mein madad karta hai. Yeh Claude Certified Associate Foundations (CCAO-F) aur Claude Certified Architect Foundations (CCAR-F) hain. Inhein lena optional hai. Students ko har Panaversity exam pass karne ke baad matching Anthropic exam lene ki encourage kiya jaata hai. PCAO-F aur PCAR-F Panaversity credentials hain. Anthropic credentials sirf Anthropic deta hai.

### Stage 2: Architect Certification, Tool Building, aur Customer Discovery (haftay 1 se 12)

Students teen cheezon par aik saath kaam karte hain:

1. **PCAR-F ki tayyari karo aur pass karo** — Panaversity ka Architect Foundations exam — [Panaversity PCAR-F Study Guide](/docs/certifications/pcar-f#study-guide) ke zariye.
2. **Apna pehla Digital FTE pilot banao, phir shared tools.** Har team [First Pilot](/docs/founders-program/first-pilot) se shuru karti hai — GIAIC/PIAIC/Panaversity AI Support worker. Founder teams phir shared Digital FTE tools aur frameworks ki taraf barhti hain, [Zia Developer AI project](/docs/founders-program/zia-developer-ai-requirements) se shuru karke.
3. **Customers se baat karo aur verticals parho.** Har team build karte waqt mumkin customers se interview karti hai aur candidate verticals parhti hai. Teams Stage 2 mein kam se kam teen customer interviews mukammal karti hain aur jo seekhti hain use record karti hain. Yeh findings shape karti hain ke team kya banaye aur Stage 3 mein kaun sa vertical chune.

Founders core tools aur frameworks banate hain.

Service FDEs unke saath saath banate hain aur unke apne kaam ke pieces deliver karne hote hain. In mein shamil hain aam business systems ke saath integrations, deployment templates, evaluation datasets, sample workflow implementations, documentation, aur mumkin users ke saath testing. Yeh kaam shared tools ke hisse ke tor pe release hota hai. Yeh founder teams ke mukammal hone ka intezaar nahi karta.

Founder teams neeche [Startup Programs](#startup-programs) mein list kiye gaye early startup resources ke liye bhi sign up karte hain. Yeh credits aur community dete hain, koi **equity** nahi lete, aur finished product ki requirement nahi rakhte. Equity ka matlab hai company mein ownership ka aik share.

**PCAR-F pass karna internship mein continue karne ka gate hai.** Students jo Stage 2 ke aakhir tak pass nahi kiye wo exam dobara le sakte hain. Pass hote hi wo do tareeqon se Stage 3 join karte hain. Wo aik aise cycle mein join kar sakte hain jo pehle se chal rahi hai, mentor ke saath agreed catch-up plan ke saath. Ya wo agle cycle ke aagaz se shuru kar sakte hain.

### Stage 3: Vertical Digital FTE Project (haftay 13 se 24, internship)

Har team aik business vertical select karti hai, jo uske Stage 2 customer interviews se inform hoti hai, aur apna pehla industry-specific Digital FTE banati hai. Choice ko structure karne ke liye [Choosing Your Vertical guide](/docs/ecosystem/choosing-your-vertical) use karein.

Har project yeh demonstrate karna chahiye:

- Aik clearly defined customer, business problem, aur AI worker role.
- Aik working product jo aik useful business workflow perform karta hai.
- Workflow ko jo tools aur information chahiye unke saath integration.
- Evaluation, monitoring, aur mushkil cases ko insaan ko dene ka saaf tareeqa.
- Customer feedback, behtar taur par aik asal pilot ke zariye.
- Measurable value, jaise bacha waqt, behtar accuracy, ya tez service.

Founder teams is project ka use apne product aur business model ko test karne ke liye karti hain. Customer feedback zaroori hai. strongly preferred hai lekin mandatory nahi, kyunki founder ko product aur uske peeche ka evidence deliver karna hota hai.

Service FDEs ko aik asal customer ke liye deployment mukammal karna zaroori hai — pilot ya production deployment — aur use document karna. Yeh Service FDE ka main deliverable hai. Agar customer stage se aage delay kare, do options hain. Panaversity doosra pilot customer assign karta hai, ya Service FDE deployment ko reference environment mein mukammal karta hai. Reference environment Panaversity ka hosted copy hai customer ke asal workflow ka practice karne ke liye. Koi bhi option requirement ko satisfy karta hai.

Stage 3 haftay 24 mein graduation review ke saath khatam hota hai. Founder teams apna vertical Digital FTE aur startup programs ke liye application package present karte hain. Service FDEs apni deployment documentation present karte hain.

### Stage 4: Startup Programs aur Placement (open-ended, graduation ke baad)

Stage 4 ki koi fixed length nahi hai aur koi end date nahi hai.

Founder teams [Startup Programs](#startup-programs) ke funding aur **accelerator** targets ke liye tab apply karte hain jab unka product aur customer evidence tayyar ho. Accelerator aik chhota program hai, aam tor teen mahine ka, jo startup ko paisa aur coaching deta hai, company ke aik share ke badle. Panaversity application materials ke reviews, introductions, aur interview practice ke saath unki madad continue karta hai. Teams jawab ka intezaar karte waqt customers acquire karte rehte hain aur products improve karte rehte hain. Kisi bhi program mein admission competitive hai aur graduation ki condition nahi hai.

Service FDEs apne customer deployments ke production support mein continue karte hain, deployments ka portfolio banate hain, aur Panaversity ke client organizations ke saath placement ki taraf barhte hain.

## Stage Gates and Assessment

Har gate Pass ya Revise deta hai. Revise aik aur submission ki ijazat deta hai. Doosri unsuccessful submission student ki jagah current cycle mein khatam kar deti hai. Student baad mein kisi later cycle mein same stage pe dobara join kar sakta hai.

| Gate | Zaroori evidence |
|---|---|
| Stage 2 ka aakhir (hafta 12) | PCAR-F pass. Shared tools mein kam se kam aik engineering contribution, merged (shared code mein accept) ya review mein. Teen documented customer interviews. Aik proposed vertical choice ki likhi wajah ke saath. |
| Stage 3 ka aakhir (hafta 24, graduation review) | Aik working vertical Digital FTE. Evaluation results. Customer feedback. Agreed business measures ke khilaf evidence. Founders: application package. Service FDEs: deployment documentation. |

## Graduation Requirements

Student Stage 3 ke aakhir mein graduate hota hai jab yeh mukammal ho. Graduation kisi bhi startup program ke acceptance par depend nahi karti.

- PCAO-F pass.
- PCAR-F pass.
- Aik shipped contribution Digital FTE tool ya framework mein, maintainers ne review karke merge kiya ho.
- Aik working vertical Digital FTE jo Stage 3 criteria meet karta ho.
- Founders: startup programs ke liye complete application package. Service FDEs: documented customer deployment, pilot ya production, jaisa Stage 3 mein defined hai.

Anthropic ki CCAO-F aur CCAR-F exams recommended hain, aur Panaversity participants ki registration mein madad karta hai, lekin yeh optional hain aur graduation ke liye required nahi hain.

## Ownership aur Intellectual Property

Students yeh policy Stage 2 shuru hone se pehle sign karte hain.

**Shared tools aur license:** Stage 2 program work ke tor pe banaye ya improve kiye tools aur frameworks Apache License 2.0 ke under open source mein release hote hain. Is mein Zia Developer AI, KSoR, aur Vertical Digital FTE Builders ke contributions shamil hain. Open source ka matlab hai koi bhi code parh sakta hai, use kar sakta hai, aur change kar sakta hai. Yeh license kisi ko bhi tools use aur change karne deta hai, commercial products mein bhi. Akeli is se koi required nahi hota ke apne changes share kare.

**Tumhe kya contribute karna hai:** Contribute karne ka duty program agreement se aata hai, license se nahi. Student jo shared tools mein improvements program work ke tor pe karta hai wo shared repositories mein Apache 2.0 ke under contribute hoti hain. Is se ecosystem un sab ke liye khula rehta hai jo usne banaya. Customer ke liye kiya gaya kaam neeche describe kiye gaye customer engagement agreement ke mutabiq chalta hai.

**Shared repositories kaun chalata hai:** Panaversity shared repositories ko GitHub pe panaversity organization mein host karta hai aur maintainers name karta hai. Maintainer woh insaan hai jo repository mein changes review aur accept karta hai. Contributions pull requests ke tor pe aate hain, jo proposed changes hain. Maintainer har aik review karta hai. Jab woh contribution guidelines meet karta hai, maintainer use merge karta hai, yaani shared code mein add karta hai. Contributors apne pehle merge se pehle project ka contributor agreement accept karte hain.

**Founder products:** Founder team Stage 3 mein banaya hua vertical Digital FTE product aur us product ke around jo company form karti hai uski malik hoti hai. Shared tools aur founder ka commercial product alag cheezein hain. Tools open source foundation hain, aur product woh hai jo company us foundation ke upar bechti hai. Shared tools permissive license use karte hain, jo yeh allow karta hai. Is liye company apna product proprietary rakh sakti hai, yaani closed aur sirf company ki malikiyat.

**Founder product pe Service FDEs:** Service FDE Stage 3 mein founder team ke product pe kaam kar sakta hai. Yeh sirf aik written contributor agreement ke zariye hota hai jo project shuru hone se pehle sign hota hai. Agreement FDE ka compensation state karti hai, yaani FDE ko kaam ke badle kya milta hai. Woh aik stated equity grant, aik payment, ya dono ho sakti hai. Agreement yeh bhi confirm karti hai ke founder team product ki malik hai. Bina signed agreement ke, Service FDEs sirf shared tools pe kaam karte hain, founder team ke product pe nahi.

**Customer ke liye kiya gaya kaam:** Aik specific customer ke liye banaye gaye configurations, data, aur workflows us customer ke hain engagement agreement ke mutabiq. Customer work se jo shared tools mein improvements aati hain woh sirf tab tak shared tools mein wapas jaati hain jab tak engagement agreement ise allow kare.

**Panaversity ki position:** Panaversity platform chalata hai aur student companies mein koi equity nahi leta. Founder teams jo products aur companies banati hain unhein rakhti hain. Service FDEs apne signed agreements jo unhe assign karte hain woh rakhte hain.

### Startup tables mein jo words milenge

- **Accelerator:** chhota program, aam tor teen mahine ka, jo startup ko paisa aur coaching deta hai, company ke aik share ke badle.
- **Equity:** company mein ownership ka aik share. **Common stock:** company ke ordinary shares.
- **Credits:** company ke cloud ya AI service ka free usage, startups ko diya gaya.
- **Raise:** investors se equity ke badle paisa lena. "Raise ke baad" ka matlab hai apne pehle investment round ke baad.
- **Institutional funding:** paisa kisi investment firm se, doston ya family se nahi.
- **SAFE:** aik agreement jahan investor abhi paisa deta hai aur shares baad mein leta hai, tumhare agle priced round pe.
- **Priced round:** pehli baar jab investors set company value pe shares kharidte hain.
- **Uncapped:** SAFE us later conversion ke liye koi maximum company value set nahi karti.
- **MFN:** most favored nation. SAFE tumhare priced round se pehle jo best terms tum kisi doosre early investor ko doge woh leti hai.
- **Valuation cap:** woh maximum company value jo SAFE ko shares mein convert karne ke liye use hoti hai.
- **Discount:** SAFE investor ke liye priced-round investors se kam share price.
- **Pre-seed aur seed:** startup funding ke pehle do rounds. **Series A:** seed ke baad ka round.
- **Traction:** asal customers, revenue, ya usage jo barh raha hai.
- **Incorporate:** company ko legal entity ke tor pe banana. **Flip:** us legal entity ko doosre mulk mein le jana. **Headquartered:** company ka main legal office kahan hai.
- **Batch:** aik group of startups jo accelerator aik saath guzarta hai.
- **Emerging markets:** aise mulk jinki economies tezi se barh rahi hain lekin abhi developing hain, jaise South Asia, Africa, Middle East, aur Latin America.

## Applicants ke liye Guidance

1. **Sahi order mein apply karo:** Stage 2 mein early startup resources ke liye sign up karo. Funding aur accelerator targets ke liye Stage 4 mein apply karo, jab vertical Digital FTE aur customer evidence mojood hon. Claude for Startups credits ke liye institutional funding raise karne ke baad apply karo.
2. **Equity terms parho, sirf total nahi:** Kayi accelerator offers ke do parts hote hain. Aik part tumhari company ka fixed share kharidta hai. Doosra part aik SAFE hai — aik agreement jahan investor abhi paisa deta hai aur shares baad mein leta hai, tumhare agle priced round pe. Priced round pehli baar hai jab investors set company value pe shares kharidte hain. Uncapped SAFE us later conversion ke liye koi maximum company value set nahi karti. MFN clause ka short form hai most favored nation. Is ka matlab hai SAFE baad mein tumhari jaari ki gayi kisi doosri SAFE ki best terms leti hai, MFN start date se le kar tumhare priced round tak. Woh terms kam valuation cap ya share price pe discount ho sakti hain. Valuation cap woh maximum company value hai jo SAFE ko shares mein convert karne ke liye use hoti hai. Is liye offers ko is basis pe compare karo ke tum apni company ka kitna share rakhne ka expect karte ho, dollar amount pe nahi.
3. **Apply karna, incorporate karna, aur headquarters rules alag karo:** Incorporate karne se pehle bhi Y Combinator ke liye apply kar sakte ho. Incorporate ka matlab hai company ko legal entity ke tor pe banana. Agar accept hue, YC sirf US, Canada, Cayman Islands, ya Singapore corporations mein invest karta hai. Woh wahan incorporate karne ya mojooda company ko flip karne mein madad karta hai. Flip company ka legal home doosre mulk mein le jana hai. Techstars US corporations ya approved equivalents mein invest karta hai aur flip mein madad karta hai. Sequoia Arc apply karte waqt Americas ya Europe mein headquartered company require karta hai. Flip ke liye waqt aur legal cost budget karein.
4. **In-person requirements ka plan banao:** Zyada tar bade US programs poore batch ke liye poori team ko in person expect karte hain. Travel aur visa ka timing jaldi plan karein. Techstars, 500 Global, Antler, aur Entrepreneurs First kayi shehron mein batches chalate hain. Orbit Ventures, Accelerating Asia, aur Flat6Labs emerging markets par focus karti hain. Yeh behtar regional ya market fit offer kar sakti hain.
5. **Confirm karo ke program active hai:** Apply karne se pehle program ke recent batch ki date check karein aur applications khuli hain ya nahi. Agar confirm nahi kar sakte, program ko "current intake unconfirmed" mark karein aur uske around plan na banayein.
